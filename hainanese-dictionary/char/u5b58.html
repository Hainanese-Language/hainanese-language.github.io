字义解释

sun² 同音字 3

cún

  • ①存在；生存。～在、～亡、名～实亡。To exist; survive. 存在 to exist; 存亡 survival or extinction; 名存实亡 existing in name only.
  • ②储存；保留。～粮、保～、～疑。To store; keep. 存粮 stored grain; 保存 to preserve; 存疑 to leave a question open.
  • ③寄放。～车、～款、寄～。To deposit; leave in someone's care. 存车 to park a bicycle; 存款 to deposit money.
  • ④心里怀着。～心、不～幻想。To harbour in the mind. 存心 deliberately; 不存幻想 to cherish no illusions.
