字义解释

pan⁶ 同音字 3

fàn

  • ①漂浮。～舟、～海。To float. 泛舟 to go boating; 泛海 to sail the sea.
  • ②透出；冒出。脸上～红、水面～起波纹。To show; to well up. 脸上泛红 to flush; 水面泛起波纹 ripples spread across the water.
  • ③广泛；一般地。～论、～指、广～。Broad; general. 泛论 a general discussion; 泛指 to refer to in general; 广泛 wide-ranging.
  • ④不深入。～～而谈、空～。Superficial. 泛泛而谈 to speak in generalities; 空泛 vague and empty.
  • ⑤大水漫流。～滥。Of floodwater, to spread. 泛滥 to overflow.
