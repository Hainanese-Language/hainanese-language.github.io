字义解释

mang⁵ 同音字 3

wǎng

  • ①用绳线等结成的捕鱼捉鸟的器具。鱼～、张～捕鸟。A net for catching fish or birds, made of twine or cord. 鱼网 a fishing net; 张网捕鸟 to spread a net for birds.
  • ②像网的东西。～兜、蜘蛛～、铁丝～。Something resembling a net. 网兜 a string bag; 蜘蛛网 a cobweb; 铁丝网 wire netting.
  • ③纵横交错像网的系统。～络、交通～、电～、互联～。A system laid out like a net. 网络 a network; 交通网 a transport network; 电网 a power grid; 互联网 the internet.
  • ④用网捕捉。～住一条大鱼。To catch with a net. 网住一条大鱼 to net a big fish.
