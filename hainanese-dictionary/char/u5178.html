  • ④诗文中引用的古书里的故事或词句。用～、～故。A story or phrase from an old book quoted in writing. 用典 to use allusions; 典故 a literary allusion.
  • ⑤旧时把财物抵押给人借钱。～当、～押、～卖。Formerly, to pawn property for a loan. 典当 to pawn; 典押 to mortgage; 典卖 to sell with a right of redemption.
