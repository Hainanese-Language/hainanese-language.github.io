字义解释

yud⁸ 同音字 2

yì

  • ①需要出力的事。劳～、苦～、兵～。Labour that must be performed. 劳役 forced labour; 兵役 military service.
  • ②战事。战～。A campaign; a battle. 战役 a campaign.
  • ③使唤；驱使。奴～、～使。To order about; press into service. 奴役 to enslave; 役使 to drive.
  • ④旧供使唤的人。差～、仆～。former A person kept for menial work. 差役 a runner; 仆役 a servant.
