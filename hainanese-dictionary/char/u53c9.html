字义解释

sa¹ 同音字 16

chā

  • 〔chā〕
  • ①一端有齿、用来扎取东西的器具。鱼～、～子、～车。A pronged implement for spearing or lifting things. 鱼叉 a fish spear; 叉子 a fork; 叉车 a forklift.
  • ②用叉取东西。～鱼。To spear something with a fork. 叉鱼 to spear fish.
  • ③交错。～腰、交～。To cross. 叉腰 with hands on hips; 交叉 to intersect.
  • ④形状是"×"的符号。打～、画个～。A mark in the shape of a cross. 打叉 to mark with a cross; 画个叉 to put a cross.
  • 〔chá〕
  • ⑤挡住；堵住。把路口～住了。To block up. 把路口叉住了 to have the entrance blocked.
