字义解释

du³ 同音字 7

zhǔ

  • ①权力或财物的所有者。～人、物～、当家做～。The owner of property or holder of power. 主人 a master; 物主 an owner.
  • ②事情的当事人。事～、失～、买～。The party concerned in a matter. 事主 the party involved; 买主 a buyer.
  • ③最重要的；基本的。～力、～要、～食。Principal; basic. 主力 the main force; 主食 staple food.
  • ④负责；掌管。～办、～持、～管。To take charge of. 主办 to sponsor; 主持 to preside over.
  • ⑤主张；拿定意见。～意、～见、～和。To hold an opinion; to decide. 主意 an idea; 主见 one's own view.
  • ⑥宗教徒对所信奉的神的称呼。Among religious believers, the deity worshipped.
