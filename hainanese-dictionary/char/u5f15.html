字义解释

in³ 同音字 5

yǐn

  • ①拉；伸。～弓、～颈、～而不发。To draw; stretch. 引弓 to draw a bow; 引颈 to crane one's neck; 引而不发 to draw the bow without loosing the arrow.
  • ②带领。～路、～导、～见。To lead. 引路 to show the way; 引导 to guide; 引见 to introduce.
  • ③招致；使出现。～起、～发、抛砖～玉。To bring about. 引起 to give rise to; 抛砖引玉 to offer a modest idea to draw out better ones.
  • ④用别的事物作证据。～用、～经据典、～以为戒。To cite as evidence. 引用 to quote; 引经据典 to cite the classics; 引以为戒 to take as a warning.
  • ⑤离开。～退、～避。To withdraw. 引退 to retire from office; 引避 to keep out of the way.
