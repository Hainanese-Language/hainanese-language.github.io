字义解释

ddi⁵ 同音字 2

dì

  • ①同父母而年纪比自己小的男子。～弟、兄～、胞～。A younger brother. 弟弟 younger brother; 兄弟 brothers; 胞弟 a full younger brother.
  • ②亲戚朋友中同辈而年纪比自己小的男子。表～、师～。A younger male of the same generation among relatives or friends. 表弟 a younger male cousin; 师弟 a junior fellow student.
  • ③男子对同辈的谦称。愚～、小～。A humble self-reference used by a man to his peers. 愚弟 your unworthy younger brother; 小弟 my humble self.
