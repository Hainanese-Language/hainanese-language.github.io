字义解释

hao² 同音字 9

tǎo

  • ①索取；请求。～债、～饶、～教。To demand; to ask for. 讨债 to dun for a debt; 讨教 to seek advice.
  • ②征伐；发动攻击。征～、声～、南征北～。To send a punitive expedition against. 征讨 to march against; 声讨 to denounce.
  • ③招惹。～厌、～人喜欢、自～苦吃。To invite a reaction. 讨厌 disagreeable; 讨人喜欢 to be likeable; 自讨苦吃 to ask for trouble.
  • ④研究；探求。～论、探～、研～。To study; to look into. 讨论 to discuss; 探讨 to enquire into.
