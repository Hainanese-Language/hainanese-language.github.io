字义解释

zin⁴ 同音字 7

jiān

  • ①把食物放在少量热油里弄熟。～鱼、～鸡蛋。To fry in a little hot oil. 煎鱼 to fry fish; 煎鸡蛋 a fried egg.
  • ②用水熬煮。～药、～茶。To decoct; simmer in water. 煎药 to brew medicine; 煎茶 to boil tea.
  • ③折磨。～熬。To torment. 煎熬 to suffer torment.
  • ④量词，用于中药熬的次数。头～、二～。Measure word for brewings of Chinese medicine. 头煎 the first decoction.
