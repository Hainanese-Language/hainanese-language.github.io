字义解释

hao² 同音字 9

tóu

  • ①人身最上部或动物最前部长着口、鼻、眼等器官的部分。～部、点～、昂～。The head, the topmost part of the body or foremost part of an animal. 头部 the head; 点头 to nod.
  • ②头发；发式。剃～、平～、梳～。Hair; a hairstyle. 剃头 to have a haircut; 梳头 to comb one's hair.
  • ③顶端；开始或末尾的部分。山～、街～、从～说起。The tip; the beginning or the end of something. 山头 a hilltop; 从头说起 to start from the beginning.
  • ④领头的人。～目、～领、带～。The one who leads. 头目 a ringleader; 带头 to take the lead.
  • ⑤第一；居先的。～等、～号、～班车。First; foremost. 头等 first-class; 头班车 the first bus.
  • ⑥次序在前的；临近某时的。～两天、～半年。Preceding; just before a given time. 头两天 the first two days.
  • ⑦量词，用于牲畜、蒜等。一～牛、两～蒜。Measure word for livestock and for garlic. 一头牛 a head of cattle.
  • ⑧后缀，用在名词、动词、形容词后。木～、念～、甜～。Suffix after a noun, verb or adjective. 木头 wood; 念头 a thought; 甜头 a sweet taste.
