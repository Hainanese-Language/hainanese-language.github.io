lou⁵

lòu

  • ①丑；不好看。丑～、～态。Ugly. 丑陋 unsightly; 陋态 an unseemly manner.
  • ②粗劣；不完备。简～、粗～、～室。Crude; poorly provided. 简陋 rough and simple; 粗陋 coarse; 陋室 a humble dwelling.
  • ③不文明的；不合理的。～习、～俗。Uncouth; unreasonable. 陋习 a bad habit; 陋俗 an undesirable custom.
  • ④见识少。浅～、孤～寡闻。Ill-informed. 浅陋 shallow; 孤陋寡闻 ignorant and ill-informed.
