字义解释

gao¹ 同音字 9

gōu

  • ①人工挖的水道。水～、～渠、阴～。A channel dug for water. 水沟 a ditch; 沟渠 irrigation channels; 阴沟 a covered drain.
  • ②浅槽；细长的凹处。车～、瓦～。A groove; a narrow depression. 车沟 a wheel rut; 瓦沟 the channel between roof tiles.
  • ③山间流水的地方；山谷。山～、～壑。A watercourse in the hills; a ravine. 山沟 a gully; 沟壑 a ravine.
