字义解释

heng² 同音字 15

tíng

  • ①中止；不再继续。～止、～工、～电、雨～了。To stop; to go no further. 停止 to cease; 停工 to stop work; 停电 a power cut; 雨停了 the rain has stopped.
  • ②停留。～留、～泊、～车。To stay; to be at rest. 停留 to stay; 停泊 to anchor; 停车 to park.
  • ③妥当。～当、～妥。Settled; in order. 停当 all set; 停妥 properly arranged.
