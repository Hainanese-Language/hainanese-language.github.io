字义解释

yiang² 同音字 9

yáng

  • ①太阳；日光。～光、～历、朝～、～春。The sun; sunlight. 阳光 sunshine; 阳历 the solar calendar; 朝阳 the morning sun; 阳春 the warmth of spring.
  • ②山的南面；水的北面，多用于地名。衡～、洛～。The south side of a hill or the north bank of a river, mostly in place names. 衡阳 Hengyang; 洛阳 Luoyang.
  • ③凸出的。～文。Raised; in relief. 阳文 characters cut in relief.
  • ④表面上的。～奉阴违。On the surface only. 阳奉阴违 to comply in public and oppose in private.
  • ⑤带正电的。～极、～离子。Positively charged. 阳极 an anode; 阳离子 a cation.
  • ⑥我国古代哲学中跟"阴"相对的一面。～刚、阴～。In Chinese philosophy, the counterpart of 阴. 阳刚 masculine strength; 阴阳 yin and yang.
