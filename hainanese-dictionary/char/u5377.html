海南话
gin³ gun³
普通话
juàn · juǎn

字义解释

gin³ 同音字 7

juàn

  • ①书籍；书的一部分。上～、第三～、手不释～。A book; a division of a book. 上卷 the first volume; 第三卷 volume three; 手不释卷 always with a book in hand.
  • ②考试写答案的纸。试～、交～、阅～。An examination paper. 试卷 an exam paper; 交卷 to hand in one's paper; 阅卷 to mark papers.
  • ③机关里保存的文件。～宗、案～、调～。Files kept by an office. 卷宗 a dossier; 案卷 records of a case; 调卷 to call for the file.
gun³ 同音字 5

juǎn

  • ①把东西弯转成筒形。～帘子、把袖子～起来。To roll something up. 卷帘子 to roll up a blind; 把袖子卷起来 to roll up one's sleeves.
  • ②裹挟着带走。～入、风～残云、～走了财物。To sweep along and carry off. 卷入 to be drawn into; 风卷残云 to sweep the board; 卷走了财物 to make off with the goods.
  • ③弯成筒形的东西。行李～、蛋～、胶～。Something rolled into a cylinder. 行李卷 a bedroll; 蛋卷 an egg roll; 胶卷 a roll of film.
  • ④量词，用于成卷的东西。一～纸、两～铺盖。Measure word for things in rolls. 一卷纸 a roll of paper; 两卷铺盖 two bedrolls.
