字义解释

dong¹ 同音字 13

zhōng

  • ①跟四周距离相等；中心。～央、～心、居～。The centre, equidistant from all sides. 中央 the centre; 居中 in the middle.
  • ②在一定范围内；里面。空～、家～、水～。Within; inside. 空中 in the air; 家中 at home.
  • ③位置在两端之间的。～层、～游、～旬。Between two ends. 中层 the middle layer; 中旬 the middle ten days of a month.
  • ④等级在两端之间的。～等、～级、～学。Of middling grade. 中等 medium; 中学 a secondary school.
  • ⑤不偏不倚。～立、适～、～庸。Impartial. 中立 neutral; 中庸 the golden mean.
  • ⑥指中国。～文、～医、古今～外。China. 中文 the Chinese language; 古今中外 in all ages and countries.
  • ⑦表示动作正在进行。研究～、发展～。Marks an action in progress. 研究中 under study.
dong⁴ 同音字 2

zhòng

  • ①正对上；恰好合上。～肯、猜～了、正～下怀。To hit; to fit exactly. 中肯 to the point; 猜中了 guessed right.
  • ②受到；遭受。～毒、～暑、～弹。To suffer; to be struck by. 中毒 to be poisoned; 中暑 to get sunstroke.
