字义解释

gu⁴ 同音字 7

gù

  • ①结实；牢靠。牢～、坚～、稳～。Solid; firm. 牢固 secure; 坚固 sturdy; 稳固 stable.
  • ②使坚固。～本、～沙。To strengthen. 固本 to consolidate the foundation; 固沙 to fix shifting sand.
  • ③坚决地。～守、～辞、～执。Resolutely. 固守 to hold fast; 固辞 to decline firmly; 固执 stubborn.
  • ④本来；原来。～有、～当如此。Originally; as a matter of course. 固有 inherent; 固当如此 it ought to be so.
