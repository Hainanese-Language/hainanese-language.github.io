字义解释

geng¹ 同音字 12

jīng

  • ①织物上纵向的纱线；跟"纬"相对。～纱、～纬。The warp of a fabric; the opposite of 纬. 经纱 warp yarn; 经纬 warp and weft.
  • ②中医指人体内气血运行的通路。～络、～脉。In Chinese medicine, the channels through which vital energy flows. 经络 the meridians; 经脉 the channels.
  • ③经过；通过。～手、途～、～年累月。To pass through. 经手 to handle personally; 途经 by way of; 经年累月 year in, year out.
  • ④经受；承受。～不起、久～考验。To undergo; withstand. 经不起 unable to bear; 久经考验 long tested.
  • ⑤治理；管理。～营、～商、～理。To manage. 经营 to run a business; 经商 to trade; 经理 a manager.
  • ⑥正常的；常道。～常、荒诞不～。Regular; the normal course. 经常 constantly; 荒诞不经 absurd and irregular.
  • ⑦作为思想或行为准则的书籍。～典、佛～、四书五～。A canonical text. 经典 the classics; 佛经 Buddhist scriptures; 四书五经 the Four Books and Five Classics.
  • ⑧月经。～期、停～。Menstruation. 经期 a monthly period; 停经 amenorrhoea.
