字义解释

bin⁵ 同音字 3

biàn

  • ①方便；容易。～利、～于携带、轻～。Convenient; easy. 便利 handy; 便于携带 easy to carry; 轻便 light and easy to use.
  • ②简单的；非正式的。～饭、～服、～条。Simple; informal. 便饭 a plain meal; 便服 everyday clothes; 便条 an informal note.
  • ③屎或尿；排泄。大～、小～、～秘。Excrement or urine; to relieve oneself. 大便 stool; 小便 urine; 便秘 constipation.
  • ④副词，就。说完～走、一说～明白。Then; straight away. 说完便走 to leave as soon as one has spoken; 一说便明白 to grasp it at once.
  • ⑤顺便的时机。～中捎来、乘～。A convenient occasion. 便中捎来 to send it along when convenient; 乘便 when convenient.
pin² 同音字 7

pián

  • 用于"便宜"，指价钱低，也指不该得的好处。～宜货、占～宜。Used in 便宜: cheap, and also an undeserved advantage. 便宜货 a bargain; 占便宜 to gain at others' expense.
