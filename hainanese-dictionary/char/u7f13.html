字义解释

hhuan³

huǎn

  • ①慢；跟"急"相对。～慢、～步、迟～。Slow; the opposite of 急. 缓慢 sluggish; 缓步 to walk unhurriedly; 迟缓 tardy.
  • ②延迟；推迟。～期、～办、～两天再说。To postpone. 缓期 to defer; 缓办 to put off; 缓两天再说 let it wait a couple of days.
  • ③松；不紧张。～和、～冲、放～。To ease; relax. 缓和 to relieve tension; 缓冲 to buffer; 放缓 to slow down.
  • ④恢复正常的生理状态。～过气来。To come round. 缓过气来 to get one's breath back.
