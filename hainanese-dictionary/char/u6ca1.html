字义解释

vo² 同音字 2

méi

  • ①没有；不存在；跟"有"相对。～钱、～人来、～关系。Not to have; the opposite of 有. 没钱 to have no money; 没人来 nobody came; 没关系 it doesn't matter.
  • ②不及；不如。他～你高。Not as much as. 他没你高 he is not as tall as you.
  • ③副词，表示对过去动作的否定。～去过、～说完。Used to negate a past action. 没去过 have never been; 没说完 have not finished speaking.

muad⁷ 同音字 6

mò

  • ①沉下去；淹没。沉～、～顶、～入水中。To sink; to be submerged. 沉没 to founder; 没顶 to be drowned; 没入水中 to sink beneath the water.
  • ②漫过；高过。水～了膝盖。To come up over. 水没了膝盖 the water came over the knees.
  • ③隐藏；不显露。埋～、出～、隐～。To hide; not to appear. 埋没 to bury in obscurity; 出没 to appear and disappear; 隐没 to fade from sight.
  • ④收归公有。～收、抄～。To confiscate. 没收 to seize; 抄没 to confiscate property.
  • ⑤终；尽。～世、～齿难忘。To end; to the end of. 没世 all one's life; 没齿难忘 never to be forgotten as long as one lives.
