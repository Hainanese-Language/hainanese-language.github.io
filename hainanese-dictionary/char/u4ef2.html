字义解释

dong⁶

zhòng

  • ①兄弟排行居第二的。～兄、伯～叔季。Second among brothers. 仲兄 one's second elder brother; 伯仲叔季 the four brothers in order of age.
  • ②一季的第二个月。～春、～秋、～夏。The second month of a season. 仲春 mid-spring; 仲秋 mid-autumn; 仲夏 midsummer.
  • ③居中的。～裁。In the middle. 仲裁 arbitration.
