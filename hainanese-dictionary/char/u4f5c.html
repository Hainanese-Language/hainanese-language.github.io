字义解释

do⁶ 同音字 5

zuò

  • 〔zuò〕
  • ①从事某种活动；进行。～工、～战、～报告、精耕细～。To engage in an activity; to carry out. 作工 to work; 作战 to fight a battle; 作报告 to give a talk; 精耕细作 intensive cultivation.
  • ②写作；创作。～文、～曲、～画。To write; to compose. 作文 to write an essay; 作曲 to compose music; 作画 to paint.
  • ③作品。杰～、名～、新～。A work. 杰作 a masterpiece; 名作 a famous work; 新作 a new piece.
  • ④当作；充当。过期～废、认贼～父。To take as; to serve as. 过期作废 invalid after the date; 认贼作父 to take the enemy for one's father.
  • ⑤装出某种样子。～势、装模～样、故～镇静。To put on an appearance. 作势 to strike a pose; 装模作样 to put on airs; 故作镇静 to feign calm.
  • ⑥起某种作用。～用、～怪、发～。To have a certain effect. 作用 an effect; 作怪 to make trouble; 发作 to flare up.
  • 〔zuō〕
  • ⑦作坊，手工业制造或加工的场所。～坊、石～、洗衣～。A workshop for handicraft manufacture. 作坊 a workshop; 石作 a stonemason's shop; 洗衣作 a laundry.
  • 〔zuó〕
  • ⑧用于"作料"，指调味品。Used in 作料: seasoning.
