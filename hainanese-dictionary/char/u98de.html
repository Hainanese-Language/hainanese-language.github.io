bue¹

fēi

  • ①鸟虫等在空中活动。～鸟、～翔、～蛾扑火。To fly, of birds and insects. 飞鸟 a bird on the wing; 飞蛾扑火 a moth to the flame.
  • ②利用动力在空中行动。～机、～船、～行员。To fly under power. 飞机 an aeroplane; 飞行员 a pilot.
  • ③在空中飘浮。～雪、～沙走石。To drift in the air. 飞雪 driving snow; 飞沙走石 sand flying and stones rolling.
  • ④形容极快。～奔、～驰、～涨。Very fast. 飞奔 to run at full speed; 飞涨 to soar.
  • ⑤意外的；无根据的。～灾、～来横祸、流言～语。Unforeseen; groundless. 飞来横祸 a bolt from the blue; 流言飞语 rumour and hearsay.
