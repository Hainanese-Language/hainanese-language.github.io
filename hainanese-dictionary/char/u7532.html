ga⁶ 同音字 6

jiǎ

  • ①天干的第一位；也表示第一。～等、桂林山水～天下。The first of the Ten Heavenly Stems; also first in order. 甲等 grade A; 桂林山水甲天下 Guilin's scenery is the finest under heaven.
  • ②动物身上的硬壳。龟～、鳞～。A shell or carapace. 龟甲 a tortoise shell; 鳞甲 scales.
  • ③古代军人穿的护身衣。盔～、～胄。Armour. 盔甲 helmet and armour.
  • ④手指、脚趾上的角质硬壳。指～。A nail. 指甲 a fingernail.
  • ⑤旧时的户口编制单位。保～。Formerly, a unit of household registration. 保甲 the baojia system.
