bui⁶ 同音字 5

pèi

  • ①挂在身上。～戴、～剑、～刀。To wear something hung on the person. 佩戴 to wear; 佩剑 to wear a sword; 佩刀 to carry a sword at the belt.
  • ②佩服；敬仰。敬～、钦～、可～。To admire. 敬佩 to hold in esteem; 钦佩 to admire; 可佩 admirable.
  • ③古代系在衣带上的装饰品。玉～。An ornament worn at the girdle in ancient times. 玉佩 a jade pendant.
