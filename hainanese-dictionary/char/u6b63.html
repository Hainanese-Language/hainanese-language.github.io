字义解释

zia⁴ 同音字 2

zhèng

  • ①不偏；不斜。～中、端～、～前方。Straight; not slanting. 正中 dead centre; 端正 upright.
  • ②合于法则或规矩。～派、～当、～道。Conforming to rule. 正派 upright; 正道 the right path.
  • ③纯；不杂。～色、～宗。Pure; unmixed. 正色 a primary colour; 正宗 orthodox.
  • ④主要的；基本的。～文、～题、～餐。Principal; basic. 正文 the main text; 正餐 a main meal.
  • ⑤表示动作在进行或状态在持续。～在开会、～下着雨。Marks an action in progress or a state continuing. 正在开会 a meeting is going on.
  • ⑥恰好。～好、～合适。Just; exactly. 正好 just right; 正合适 exactly suitable.
  • ⑦使位置或状态正确。～音、～骨、～衣冠。To set right. 正音 to correct pronunciation; 正衣冠 to straighten one's dress.
  • ⑧大于零的。～数、～号。Greater than zero. 正数 a positive number.
