字义解释

voi¹

mài

  • ①用东西换钱，跟"买"相对。～菜、～货、买～。To sell — the opposite of 买. 卖菜 to sell vegetables; 买卖 buying and selling.
  • ②出卖；背叛。～国、～友求荣。To betray. 卖国 to sell out one's country; 卖友求荣 to betray a friend for gain.
  • ③尽量使出。～力、～劲儿。To exert to the full. 卖力 to spare no effort; 卖劲儿 to work with a will.
  • ④故意显示。～弄、～关子。To show off deliberately. 卖弄 to parade; 卖关子 to keep people in suspense.
