字义解释

bag⁷ 同音字 8

bó

  • ①多；丰富。渊～、地大物～、～览群书。Abundant; wide-ranging. 渊博 erudite; 地大物博 vast in territory and rich in resources; 博览群书 widely read.
  • ②知道得多。～学、～古通今。Widely learned. 博学 learned; 博古通今 versed in both past and present.
  • ③取得；换取。～得同情、～取信任。To win; to gain. 博得同情 to win sympathy; 博取信任 to gain trust.
  • ④赌博。～徒、赌～。To gamble. 博徒 a gambler; 赌博 gambling.
