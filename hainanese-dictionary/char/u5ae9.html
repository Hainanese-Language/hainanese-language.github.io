nun⁵

nèn

  • ①初生而柔弱；跟"老"相对。～芽、～叶、细皮～肉。Young and tender; the opposite of 老. 嫩芽 a young shoot; 细皮嫩肉 with a fine soft skin.
  • ②食物烹调时间短，容易咀嚼。这肉炒得很～。(of food) Lightly cooked and tender. 这肉炒得很嫩 the meat is done just right.
  • ③颜色浅。～绿、～黄。(of colour) Light; pale. 嫩绿 pale green; 嫩黄 light yellow.
  • ④阅历浅；不老练。他还～了点。Green; inexperienced. 他还嫩了点 he is still a bit raw.
