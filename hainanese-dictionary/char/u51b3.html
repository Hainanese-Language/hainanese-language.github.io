字义解释

gid⁷ 同音字 25

jué

  • ①拿定主意。～定、～心、犹豫不～。To decide. 决定 to determine; 决心 resolve; 犹豫不决 unable to make up one's mind.
  • ②判定；裁断。～赛、判～、表～。To judge; to settle. 决赛 the final; 判决 a verdict; 表决 to put to the vote.
  • ③一定（用于否定）。～不退缩、～无此事。Definitely (with a negative). 决不退缩 never to flinch; 决无此事 there is no such thing.
  • ④堤岸被水冲开。～口、溃～。For a dyke to be breached. 决口 a breach; 溃决 to burst.
  • ⑤执行死刑。枪～、处～。To execute. 枪决 to shoot; 处决 to put to death.
