字义解释

bun¹ 同音字 3

fēn

用于第 ❶❷❸❹ 义

  • ①使整体变成几部分；跟"合"相对。～开、～割、～成两组。To divide a whole into parts; the opposite of 合. 分开 to separate; 分割 to carve up; 分成两组 to split into two groups.
  • ②分配；分派。～发、～工、～给他一半。To distribute; to assign. 分发 to hand out; 分工 to divide the work; 分给他一半 to give him half.
  • ③辨别。～辨、～清是非、不～好坏。To tell apart. 分辨 to distinguish; 分清是非 to tell right from wrong; 不分好坏 to make no distinction between good and bad.
  • ④由整体分出的。～店、～会、～队。Branching off from a whole. 分店 a branch shop; 分会 a branch association; 分队 a detachment.
  • ⑤分数。三～之二、百～之五。A fraction. 三分之二 two thirds; 百分之五 five per cent.
  • ⑥计量单位，用于时间、货币、长度、成绩等。十～钟、一角二～、得九十～。A unit of measure, used for time, money, length and marks. 十分钟 ten minutes; 一角二分 one jiao two fen; 得九十分 to score ninety.
hhun¹第 ❺❻ 义
pun⁶ 同音字 2

fèn

  • ①成分。水～、养～、糖～。A component. 水分 moisture; 养分 nutrients; 糖分 sugar content.
  • ②职责或权利的限度。本～、过～、～内之事、恰如其～。The limit of one's duty or entitlement. 本分 one's proper duty; 过分 excessive; 分内之事 one's own business; 恰如其分 apt and proper.
  • ③情分；情谊。看在老朋友的～上。Affection between people. 看在老朋友的分上 for the sake of an old friendship.
