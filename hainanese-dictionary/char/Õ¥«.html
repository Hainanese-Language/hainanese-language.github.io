字义解释

vui² 同音字 4

wēi

  • ①细小；轻微。～小、细～、～不足道。Tiny; slight. 微小 minute; 微不足道 negligible.
  • ②稍；略。～笑、～感不适、稍～。Slightly. 微笑 to smile; 微感不适 feeling a little unwell.
  • ③衰落。衰～、式～。To decline. 衰微 to wane; 式微 to fall into decay.
  • ④精深奥妙。精～、～妙。Subtle; profound. 精微 abstruse; 微妙 delicate.
  • ⑤主单位的百万分之一。～米、～克。One millionth of the base unit; micro-. 微米 a micrometre; 微克 a microgram.
