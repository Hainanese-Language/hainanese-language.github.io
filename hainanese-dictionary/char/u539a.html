普通话
hòu

字义解释

gao⁵

hòu

  • ①扁平物上下两面的距离大，跟"薄"相对。～纸、～棉衣。Thick — the opposite of 薄. 厚纸 thick paper; 厚棉衣 a heavy padded coat.
  • ②厚度。三尺～、～薄不均。Thickness. 三尺厚 three feet thick; 厚薄不均 of uneven thickness.
  • ③感情深。深情～谊、～爱。Deep in feeling. 深情厚谊 deep affection; 厚爱 your kind regard.
  • ④优待；重视。～待、～此薄彼。To favour. 厚待 to treat generously; 厚此薄彼 to favour one and slight another.
  • ⑤不刻薄；忠厚。～道、宽～。Generous; not mean. 厚道 kind and honest; 宽厚 magnanimous.
  • ⑥味道浓。酒味很～。Strong in flavour. 酒味很厚 the wine is full-bodied.
  • ⑦财物多；利润大。～礼、～利、家底～。Ample; large. 厚礼 a generous gift; 厚利 a handsome profit; 家底厚 well-off.
