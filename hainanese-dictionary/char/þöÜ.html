字义解释

dim⁵

shèn

  • ①很；极。～好、～快、不～满意。Very; extremely. 甚好 very good; 甚快 very fast; 不甚满意 not particularly pleased.
  • ②超过；胜过。日～一日、更有～者。To exceed; to surpass. 日甚一日 worse with every passing day; 更有甚者 what is more.
  • ③〈书〉过分。太～、欺人太～。〈lit.〉 Excessive. 太甚 to go too far; 欺人太甚 to bully beyond all bounds.
