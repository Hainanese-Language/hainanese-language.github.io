字义解释

mu⁴ 同音字 3

mù

  • ①覆盖在上面的帐篷。～天席地、帐～。A tent spread overhead. 幕天席地 with the sky for a roof and the earth for a mat.
  • ②挂着的大块布，用来遮挡或作背景。银～、～布、开～。A large hanging cloth used as a screen or backdrop. 银幕 a cinema screen; 开幕 to raise the curtain.
  • ③戏剧中较完整的段落。第一～、独～剧。An act of a play. 第一幕 act one; 独幕剧 a one-act play.
  • ④〈旧〉将帅办公的地方。～府、～僚。〈former〉 A commander's headquarters. 幕府 a shogunate; 幕僚 an aide.
