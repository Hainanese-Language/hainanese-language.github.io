字义解释

hhia⁵ 同音字 6

wǎ

  • ①铺屋顶用的建筑材料。～片、～房、屋～。Tile; a building material used for roofing. 瓦片 roof tile; 瓦房 tiled-roof house; 屋瓦 roof tiles.
  • ②用陶土烧成的。～盆、～器、～罐。Made of fired clay; earthenware. 瓦盆 earthenware basin; 瓦器 earthenware; 瓦罐 earthenware pot.
  • ③比喻分裂、崩溃。～解、土崩～解。Figuratively, to break apart or collapse. 瓦解 to disintegrate; 土崩瓦解 to collapse completely.
  • ④"瓦特"的简称，功率单位。千～、百～灯泡。Watt; short for 瓦特, a unit of power. 千瓦 kilowatt; 百瓦灯泡 a 100-watt light bulb.
