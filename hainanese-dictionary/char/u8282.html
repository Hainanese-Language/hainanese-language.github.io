字义解释

dad⁷ 同音字 3

jié

  • ①竹子、草木分段的地方；物体的分段。竹～、关～、骨～、～肢动物。A joint or node in bamboo and other plants; a division or joint in an object. 竹节 bamboo joint; 关节 joint; 骨节 knuckle or bone joint; 节肢动物 arthropod.
  • ②段落；项目。章～、～目、～次。A section; paragraph; item. 章节 section or chapter; 节目 item or programme; 节次 sequence of sections or lessons.
  • ③节日；纪念日。春～、～日、过～、国庆～。A festival or commemorative day. 春节 Spring Festival; 节日 festival; 过节 to celebrate a festival; 国庆节 National Day.
  • ④删略。～录、～选、～译。To abridge or excerpt. 节录 to excerpt; 节选 to select passages; 节译 to translate in abridged form.
  • ⑤事项；情节。细～、情～、生活小～。A detail or part of an event. 细节 detail; 情节 plot or circumstances; 生活小节 minor details of daily life.
  • ⑥航速单位，一海里每小时。船速二十～。Knot; a unit of speed equal to one nautical mile per hour. 船速二十节 a speed of twenty knots.
  • ⑦量词，用于分段的事物。一～课、三～车厢、两～电池。Measure word for things divided into sections or segments. 一节课 one lesson; 三节车厢 three railway cars; 两节电池 two battery cells.

  • ①减少；限制。～约、～省、～制、开源～流。To reduce; to economize; to restrict. 节约 to economize; 节省 to save; 节制 to exercise restraint; 开源节流 to increase income and reduce expenditure.
  • ②礼度；操守。礼～、气～、～操、守～。Proper conduct; moral integrity. 礼节 etiquette; 气节 moral integrity; 节操 moral principles; 守节 to remain true to one’s principles.
  • ③音乐中有规律的强弱、长短现象。～奏、～拍。Rhythm; beat in music. 节奏 rhythm; 节拍 beat.
