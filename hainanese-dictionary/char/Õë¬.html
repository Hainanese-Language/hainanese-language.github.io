字义解释

ga¹ 同音字 5

jiǎn

  • ①剪刀。～子、火～、～刀。Scissors. 剪子 scissors; 火剪 fire tongs; 剪刀 shears.
  • ②用剪刀铰。～纸、～发、～断。To cut with scissors. 剪纸 papercutting; 剪发 to cut one's hair; 剪断 to snip through.
  • ③除掉。～除、～灭。To wipe out. 剪除 to eliminate; 剪灭 to exterminate.
