字义解释

hhun⁴ 同音字 3

xùn

  • ①教导；告诫。～话、教～、～诫。To instruct; to admonish. 训话 to give a pep talk; 教训 a lesson; 训诫 to reprimand.
  • ②可以作为准则的话。家～、遗～、古～。Words to be taken as a rule. 家训 family precepts; 遗训 a legacy of teaching.
  • ③训练。～马、集～、军～。To train. 训马 to break a horse; 军训 military training.
  • ④解释字义。～诂。To gloss the meaning of a word. 训诂 exegesis.
