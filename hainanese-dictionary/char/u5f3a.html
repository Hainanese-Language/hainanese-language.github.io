字义解释

hiang²

qiáng

  • ①力量大；跟"弱"相对。～大、～壮、～国。Strong; the opposite of 弱. 强大 powerful; 强壮 robust; 强国 a strong nation.
  • ②感情或意志坚定。～硬、责任心～。Firm in feeling or will. 强硬 unyielding; 责任心强 with a keen sense of duty.
  • ③使用强力。～占、～攻、～渡。By force. 强占 to seize by force; 强攻 to storm; 强渡 to force a crossing.
  • ④优越；好。要～、他比我～。Better; superior. 要强 eager to excel; 他比我强 he is better than I am.
  • ⑤表示略多于某个数目。三分之一～。Marking slightly more than a stated figure. 三分之一强 a little over a third.
