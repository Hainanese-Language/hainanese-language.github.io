字义解释

giang⁴ 同音字 3

jiàng

  • 〔jiàng〕
  • ①落下；由高到低；跟"升"相对。～落、～雨、下～。To come down; the opposite of 升. 降落 to descend; 降雨 rainfall; 下降 to drop.
  • ②使落下；减低。～价、～级、～低成本。To bring down; to reduce. 降价 to cut prices; 降级 to demote; 降低成本 to lower costs.
  • 〔xiáng〕
  • ③投降；使驯服。～服、投～、招～、～龙伏虎。To surrender; to subdue. 降服 to yield; 投降 to capitulate; 招降 to call on an enemy to surrender; 降龙伏虎 to overcome the mightiest foes.
