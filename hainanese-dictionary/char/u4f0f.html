字义解释

pag⁷ 同音字 2

fú

  • ①身体向前靠在物体上；趴。～案、～在桌上。To lean forward onto something; to lie prone. 伏案 to bend over a desk; 伏在桌上 to lie across a table.
  • ②隐藏。～兵、潜～、埋～、～击。To lie hidden. 伏兵 troops in ambush; 潜伏 to lie low; 埋伏 to ambush; 伏击 to attack from ambush.
  • ③低下去。此起彼～、起～不定。To sink low. 此起彼伏 to rise and fall one after another; 起伏不定 fluctuating.
  • ④屈服；使屈服。～法、～罪、降～。To submit; to subdue. 伏法 to be executed; 伏罪 to plead guilty; 降伏 to bring to heel.
  • ⑤三伏，夏季最热的时期。初～、～天、数～。The hottest period of summer. 初伏 the first fu period; 伏天 dog days; 数伏 the fu period sets in.
  • ⑥电压单位伏特的简称。二百二十～。Short for the volt. 二百二十伏 two hundred and twenty volts.
