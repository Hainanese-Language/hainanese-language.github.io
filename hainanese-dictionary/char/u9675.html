字义解释

leng² 同音字 20

líng

  • ①大土山。丘～、～谷。A large mound. 丘陵 hills; 陵谷 hills and valleys.
  • ②帝王或伟人的坟墓。～墓、～园、中山～。The tomb of a ruler or great figure. 陵墓 a mausoleum; 陵园 a memorial park; 中山陵 the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum.
  • ③〈书〉侵犯；欺压。～侮。〈lit.〉 To encroach on; to bully. 陵侮 to humiliate.
