字义解释

beng⁴ 同音字 4

bìng

  • ①合在一起。合～、兼～、～案处理。To join together. 合并 to merge; 兼并 to annex; 并案处理 to deal with cases jointly.
  • ②一齐；平排着。～肩、～排、～驾齐驱。Side by side; abreast. 并肩 shoulder to shoulder; 并排 in a row; 并驾齐驱 to keep pace with one another.
  • ③副词，用在否定词前，加强语气。～不知道、～非如此。Used before a negative for emphasis. 并不知道 to have no idea at all; 并非如此 that is simply not so.
  • ④连词，并且。讨论～通过。And; furthermore. 讨论并通过 to discuss and adopt.
