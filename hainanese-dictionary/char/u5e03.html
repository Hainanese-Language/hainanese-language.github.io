字义解释

bu⁴

bù

  • ①用棉、麻等织成的材料。棉～、～料、～鞋。Cloth woven from cotton, hemp and the like. 棉布 cotton cloth; 布料 dress material; 布鞋 cloth shoes.
  • ②宣告；对众陈述。宣～、公～、发～。To announce publicly. 宣布 to declare; 公布 to promulgate; 发布 to issue.
  • ③散开；分散。分～、散～、～满乌云。To spread; be scattered over. 分布 to be distributed; 布满乌云 overcast with dark clouds.
  • ④安排；设置。～置、～局、～防。To arrange; set out. 布置 to lay out; 布局 an overall layout; 布防 to deploy defences.
