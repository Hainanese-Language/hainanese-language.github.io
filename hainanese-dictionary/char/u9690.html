字义解释

in³ 同音字 5

yǐn

  • ①藏起来不显露。～藏、～蔽、～居、～姓埋名。To hide oneself away. 隐藏 to conceal; 隐蔽 to take cover; 隐居 to live in seclusion; 隐姓埋名 to live under an assumed name.
  • ②潜藏不露的；不公开的。～患、～情、～衷、～私。Hidden; not made public. 隐患 a latent danger; 隐情 facts one keeps back; 隐衷 unspoken feelings; 隐私 private matters.
  • ③不明显。～约、～～作痛。Faint; indistinct. 隐约 vague; 隐隐作痛 to ache dully.
