字义解释

diang² 同音字 9

cháng

  • ①归还；抵补。～还、赔～、补～、得不～失。To repay; to make good. 偿还 to repay; 赔偿 to compensate; 补偿 to make up for; 得不偿失 the gain does not make up for the loss.
  • ②满足。～愿、如愿以～。To fulfil. 偿愿 to have one's wish fulfilled; 如愿以偿 to have one's wish come true.
