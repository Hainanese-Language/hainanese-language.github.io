字义解释

ham⁵ 同音字 2

fàn

  • ①抵触；违反。～法、～规、触～。To violate; go against. 犯法 to break the law; 触犯 to offend against.
  • ②侵犯；进攻。～境、进～、人不～我。To invade; encroach on. 犯境 to invade the borders; 人不犯我 if others do not attack us.
  • ③犯人。战～、要～、囚～。A criminal. 战犯 a war criminal; 囚犯 a prisoner.
  • ④发作；发生。～病、～错误、～脾气。To have an attack of; commit. 犯病 to fall ill again; 犯错误 to make a mistake.
