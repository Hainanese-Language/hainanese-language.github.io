字义解释

diang² 同音字 9

xiáng

  • ①细密；完备，跟"略"相对。～细、～尽、～情。Detailed; full — the opposite of 略. 详细 detailed; 详尽 exhaustive; 详情 particulars.
  • ②清楚地知道。内容不～、生卒年月不～。To be known clearly. 内容不详 the contents are not known; 生卒年月不详 dates of birth and death unknown.
  • ③说明；细说。内～、～述。To set out in detail. 内详 particulars enclosed; 详述 to recount fully.
