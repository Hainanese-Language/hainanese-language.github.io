字义解释

ngui² 同音字 4

wēi

  • ①危险；不安全。～急、～难、～重、转～为安。Dangerous; unsafe. 危急 critical; 危难 peril; 危重 in a critical condition; 转危为安 to pull through a crisis.
  • ②使处于危险境地。～害、～及生命。To endanger. 危害 to harm; 危及生命 to threaten life.
  • ③书高；陡。～楼、～峰。lit. High; steep. 危楼 a lofty building; 危峰 a towering peak.
  • ④书端正。正襟～坐。lit. Upright. 正襟危坐 to sit up straight and proper.
