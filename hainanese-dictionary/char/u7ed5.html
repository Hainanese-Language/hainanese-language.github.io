字义解释

yiao² 同音字 14

rào

  • ①缠绕。～线、～毛线。To wind; to coil. 绕线 to wind thread; 绕毛线 to wind wool.
  • ②围着转动。～场一周、～着圈子走。To move round something. 绕场一周 to circle the ground once; 绕着圈子走 to walk in circles.
  • ③不从正面通过；迂回。～道、～远、～过障碍。To go round rather than straight through; to make a detour. 绕道 to take a roundabout route; 绕远 to go the long way; 绕过障碍 to skirt an obstacle.
  • ④纠缠；使迷糊。～口令、这问题把我～住了。To tangle; to confuse. 绕口令 a tongue-twister; 这问题把我绕住了 the question has me tied in knots.
yiao³ 同音字 6

rǎo

  • 〈旧〉围绕；缠绕。今统读 rào。
