字义解释

in⁴ 同音字 5

yìn

  • ①图章。～章、盖～、～信、公～。A seal. 印章 a seal; 盖印 to affix a seal; 印信 an official seal; 公印 an official stamp.
  • ②痕迹。手～、脚～、烙～。A mark. 手印 a fingerprint; 脚印 a footprint; 烙印 a brand.
  • ③使文字图画留在纸上。～刷、～书、复～。To put words or pictures on paper. 印刷 to print; 印书 to print books; 复印 to photocopy.
  • ④彼此相合。心心相～、～证。To tally with one another. 心心相印 to be of one mind; 印证 to confirm by comparison.
