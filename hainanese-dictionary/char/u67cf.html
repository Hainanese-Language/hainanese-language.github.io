字义解释

bog⁷ 同音字 23

bǎi

  • 〔bǎi〕
  • ①常绿乔木，木质坚硬，可做建筑材料。～树、松～、翠～。cypress, an evergreen tree with hard timber used in building.
  • 〔bó〕
  • ②用于姓氏和译音。～林、～拉图。used in surnames and in transliterations.
  • 〔bò〕
  • ③用于"黄柏"，即黄檗，树皮可入药。黄～。used in 黄柏, the Amur cork tree, whose bark is used in medicine.
