字义解释

heg⁷ 同音字 8

kè

  • ①用刀在物体上雕出形象或文字。雕～、～图章、～舟求剑。To carve a figure or characters into something. 雕刻 to carve; 刻图章 to cut a seal; 刻舟求剑 to take measures without regard to changed circumstances.
  • ②时间。此～、时～、立～、顷～。A moment in time. 此刻 this moment; 时刻 a point in time; 立刻 at once; 顷刻 in an instant.
  • ③量词，十五分钟为一～。三点一～、差一～十点。Measure word: a quarter of an hour. 三点一刻 a quarter past three; 差一刻十点 a quarter to ten.
  • ④程度深。深～、～苦、～意。Deep in degree. 深刻 profound; 刻苦 painstaking; 刻意 deliberate.
  • ⑤苛求；待人不厚道。～薄、尖～、～毒。Harsh; unkind. 刻薄 mean; 尖刻 caustic; 刻毒 vicious.
