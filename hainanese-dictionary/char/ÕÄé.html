chǎng

  • ①工厂。～房、～长、机械～。A factory. 厂房 factory premises; 厂长 a factory director.
  • ②有空地可以存货或加工的场所。木～、煤～。A yard where goods are stored or worked. 木厂 a timber yard; 煤厂 a coal yard.
