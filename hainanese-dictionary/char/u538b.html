字义解释

dde⁶

yā

  • ①从上面加力。～住、～碎、～路机。To press down. 压住 to hold down; 压碎 to crush; 压路机 a road roller.
  • ②使稳定；使平静。～惊、～住阵脚、～住火气。To steady; to calm. 压惊 to soothe after a fright; 压住阵脚 to hold the line.
  • ③用威力制服。镇～、～制、～迫。To subdue by force. 镇压 to suppress; 压制 to stifle; 压迫 to oppress.
  • ④逼近。大军～境、太阳～山了。To bear down on. 大军压境 a great army presses on the border; 太阳压山了 the sun is dipping behind the hills.
  • ⑤搁着不动。积～、这件事～了很久。To shelve. 积压 to leave piled up; 这件事压了很久 this matter has been left hanging a long time.
