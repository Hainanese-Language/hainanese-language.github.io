nang³ 同音字 3

nǎng

  • 拼命地往嘴里塞食物。～了一嘴饭。To cram food into the mouth. 馕了一嘴饭 to stuff one's mouth with rice.
俗读niang⁵
nang² 同音字 2

náng

  • 一种烤制的面饼，是维吾尔、哈萨克等民族的主食。打～、～坑。Naan, a baked flatbread that is a staple food of the Uyghur, Kazakh and other peoples. 打馕 to bake naan; 馕坑 a naan oven.
