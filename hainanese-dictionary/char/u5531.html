字义解释

sang⁴ 同音字 4

chàng

  • ①依照音律发出声音。～歌、演～、～戏。To sing. 唱歌 to sing a song; 唱戏 to perform opera.
  • ②大声叫。～名、鸡～三遍。To call out. 唱名 to call the roll; 鸡唱三遍 the cock has crowed thrice.
  • ③歌曲；唱词。小～、渔～。A song; the sung lines of an opera. 小唱 a ditty; 渔唱 a fisherman's song.
