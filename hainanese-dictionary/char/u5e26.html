字义解释

ddua⁴

dài

  • ①带子；像带子的长条物。皮～、鞋～、传送～。A belt or strip. 皮带 a leather belt; 鞋带 a shoelace; 传送带 a conveyor belt.
  • ②轮胎。车～、内～。A tyre. 车带 a tyre; 内带 an inner tube.
  • ③区域。热～、沿海一～。A zone; an area. 热带 the tropics; 沿海一带 the coastal region.
  • ④随身拿着。～钱、～行李、携～。To carry on one's person. 带钱 to bring money; 携带 to take along.
  • ⑤捎；顺便做。～个信、～一句话。To take along in passing. 带个信 to pass on a message.
  • ⑥呈现；含有。面～笑容、～有酸味。To show; contain. 面带笑容 with a smile on one's face; 带有酸味 slightly sour.
  • ⑦引导；领。～路、～队、～徒弟。To lead; guide. 带路 to show the way; 带队 to lead a team; 带徒弟 to train an apprentice.
  • ⑧连着；附带。连说～笑、～叶的橘子。Together with; accompanied by. 连说带笑 talking and laughing; 带叶的橘子 oranges with the leaves on.
