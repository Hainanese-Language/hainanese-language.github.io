字义解释

leng⁵

lìng

  • ①上级对下级的指示；发布指示。命～、下～、责～改正。An order from a superior; to issue one. 命令 an order; 下令 to give an order; 责令改正 to order corrections.
  • ②使；让。～人感动、～人满意。To cause; to make. 令人感动 moving; 令人满意 satisfactory.
  • ③时节。时～、夏～、节～。A season. 时令 the season; 夏令 summertime; 节令 a seasonal period.
  • ④古代官名。县～、尚书～。An official title in former times. 县令 a county magistrate; 尚书令 a president of the board.
  • ⑤敬辞，称对方的亲属。～尊、～堂、～郎。Polite form used of the other party's relatives. 令尊 your father; 令堂 your mother; 令郎 your son.
  • ⑥词曲的一种体裁，篇幅短小。小～、如梦～。A short form of verse or song. 小令 a short lyric; 如梦令 the tune Rumengling.
