字义解释

sin² 同音字 11

chén

  • ①摆设；排列。～列、～设、～兵。To set out; to arrange. 陈列 to display; 陈设 furnishings; 陈兵 to deploy troops.
  • ②述说。～述、～情、详～。To state. 陈述 to set forth; 陈情 to plead one's case; 详陈 to explain in detail.
  • ③时间久的；旧的。～旧、～醋、推～出新。Long kept; old. 陈旧 outdated; 陈醋 mature vinegar; 推陈出新 to bring forth the new from the old.
  • ④姓。A surname.
