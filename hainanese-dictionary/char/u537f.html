字义解释

heng¹ 同音字 6

qīng

  • ①古代高级官名。三公九～、～相。A senior official title in ancient times. 三公九卿 the three dukes and nine ministers; 卿相 high ministers.
  • ②古代君主对臣下的称呼；也用于夫妻、朋友之间的爱称。爱～。A form of address used by a sovereign to a subject, and affectionately between spouses or friends. 爱卿 my dear minister.
