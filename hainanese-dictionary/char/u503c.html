字义解释

zeg⁸ 同音字 2

zhí

  • ①价值；数值。产～、比～、币～。Value; a numerical value. 产值 output value; 比值 a ratio; 币值 the value of a currency.
  • ②价钱是；相当于。这本书～二十元、不～一提。To be worth. 这本书值二十元 this book costs twenty yuan; 不值一提 not worth mentioning.
  • ③遇到；碰上。正～节日、～此之际。To happen upon. 正值节日 just at the time of the festival; 值此之际 at this juncture.
  • ④轮流担任一定时间的工作。～班、～日、～勤、～夜。To take one's turn on duty. 值班 to be on duty; 值日 to be on daily duty; 值勤 to be on the job; 值夜 to keep night watch.
