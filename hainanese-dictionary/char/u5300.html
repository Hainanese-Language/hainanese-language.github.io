字义解释

yun³ 同音字 4

yún

  • ①分布一致；不偏多偏少。～称、～净、颜色涂得很～。Evenly spread; neither too much nor too little in any part. 匀称 well proportioned; 匀净 uniform; 颜色涂得很匀 the colour is evenly applied.
  • ②使均匀。～一～、把颜色～开。To even out. 匀一匀 to spread it evenly; 把颜色匀开 to blend the colour.
  • ③从中分出一部分给别人。～出两间房、～给他一些。To spare part of something for someone else. 匀出两间房 to free up two rooms; 匀给他一些 to let him have some.
