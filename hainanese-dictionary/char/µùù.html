字义解释

gi² 同音字 4

qí

  • ①用布、绸等做成的标志。～帜、国～、～杆。A flag. 旗帜 a banner; 国旗 a national flag; 旗杆 a flagpole.
  • ②〈旧〉清代满族的军队和户口编制。八～、～人。〈former〉 The Manchu military and household divisions of the Qing. 八旗 the Eight Banners; 旗人 a Bannerman.
  • ③内蒙古自治区的行政区划单位，相当于县。A banner, an administrative division in Inner Mongolia equivalent to a county.
