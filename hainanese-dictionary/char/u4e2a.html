gai² 同音字 3

gè

  • ①量词，用于没有专用量词的事物。一～人、三～苹果、两～问题。Measure word for things without a special measure word. 一个人 one person; 三个苹果 three apples; 两个问题 two questions.
  • ②单独的。～人、～体、～别、～性。Individual. 个人 an individual; 个体 an individual unit; 个别 particular; 个性 individuality.
  • ③用在动词和补语之间，表示程度。说～不停、吃～痛快。Used between a verb and its complement to show extent. 说个不停 to talk without stopping; 吃个痛快 to eat to one's heart's content.
