字义解释

zeng⁵ 同音字 7

jìng

  • ①清洁；没有杂质。干～、～水、窗明几～。Clean; free of impurity. 干净 clean; 窗明几净 bright windows and clean tables.
  • ②使清洁。～面、～手。To cleanse. 净面 to wash the face; 净手 to wash one's hands.
  • ③什么也没有。～光、碗里吃～了。With nothing left. 净光 all gone; 碗里吃净了 the bowl is cleaned out.
  • ④纯粹的。～重、～利、～收入。Net. 净重 net weight; 净收入 net income.
  • ⑤单；只。～说不做、满地～是树叶。Only; nothing but. 净说不做 all talk and no action; 满地净是树叶 the ground is nothing but leaves.
  • ⑥戏曲角色行当之一，俗称花脸。A role type in Chinese opera, commonly called 花脸, the painted face.
