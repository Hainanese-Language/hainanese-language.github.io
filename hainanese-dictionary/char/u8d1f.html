pu⁶
普通话
fù

字义解释

pu⁶ 同音字 5

fù

  • ①背；用背驮。～重、～荆请罪。To carry on the back. 负重 to bear a heavy load; 负荆请罪 to offer a humble apology.
  • ②担负；承担。～责、～担、身～重任。To shoulder; to bear. 负责 to be responsible; 负担 a burden; 身负重任 to carry heavy responsibilities.
  • ③遭受。～伤、～屈。To suffer. 负伤 to be wounded; 负屈 to suffer a wrong.
  • ④依仗。～险固守、～隅顽抗。To rely on. 负险固守 to hold out behind natural defences; 负隅顽抗 to put up a desperate last stand.
  • ⑤亏欠。～债、～心。To owe. 负债 to be in debt; 负心 to be unfaithful in love.
  • ⑥失败；跟"胜"相对。胜～、～于对手。To lose; the opposite of 胜. 胜负 victory or defeat; 负于对手 to lose to one's opponent.
  • ⑦小于零的；跟"正"相对。～数、～号。Less than zero; the opposite of 正. 负数 a negative number; 负号 a minus sign.
  • ⑧带负电的。～极、～离子。Negatively charged. 负极 the negative pole; 负离子 an anion.
  • ⑨违背；辜负。～约、忘恩～义、不～众望。To go back on; to fail. 负约 to break an agreement; 忘恩负义 to be ungrateful; 不负众望 to live up to what is expected.
