字义解释

dio¹ 同音字 4

shāo

  • ①使东西着火。～火、燃～、～毁。To set alight. 烧火 to light a fire; 燃烧 to burn; 烧毁 to burn down.
  • ②加热使物体起变化。～水、～砖、～菜。To heat until something changes. 烧水 to boil water; 烧砖 to fire bricks; 烧菜 to cook a dish.
  • ③体温升高。发～、退～。To run a fever. 发烧 to have a temperature; 退烧 for a fever to subside.
  • ④施肥过多使植物枯萎。～苗。(of over-fertilising) To wither a crop. 烧苗 to scorch the seedlings.
