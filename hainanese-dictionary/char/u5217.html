字义解释

lid⁸ 同音字 6

liè

  • ①排成行；摆出。排～、陈～、～队、～举事实。To arrange in a row; to set out. 排列 to arrange; 陈列 to display; 列队 to line up; 列举事实 to cite facts one by one.
  • ②行列。前～、行～、名～前茅。A row; a rank. 前列 the front rank; 行列 ranks; 名列前茅 to rank among the best.
  • ③归入某一类。～入名单、～为重点。To place in a category. 列入名单 to put on a list; 列为重点 to designate as a priority.
  • ④量词，用于成行的车厢等。一～火车。Measure word for trains. 一列火车 one train.
  • ⑤各；众。～国、～位来宾。Various; all. 列国 the various states; 列位来宾 all you guests.
