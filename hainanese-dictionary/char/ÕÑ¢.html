字义解释

hho³

hǎo

  • ①优点多；令人满意；跟"坏"相对。～人、～事、天气很～。Good; satisfactory — the opposite of 坏. 好人 a good person; 天气很好 the weather is fine.
  • ②友爱；和睦。友～、他们很～。On friendly terms. 友好 friendly; 他们很好 they get on well.
  • ③健康；痊愈。病～了、您～。Well; recovered. 病好了 to have got better; 您好 how do you do.
  • ④表示同意或结束。～，就这么办。Marking agreement or closure. 好，就这么办 right, let's do that.
  • ⑤容易。这事～办。Easy. 这事好办 this is easily managed.
  • ⑥表示程度深。～冷、～大的雨。Marking a high degree. 好冷 how cold; 好大的雨 what heavy rain.
  • ⑦便于；以便。早点走，～赶上车。So as to; in order to. 早点走，好赶上车 leave early so as to catch the bus.
hhao⁴

hào

  • ①喜爱；跟"恶"相对。爱～、～学、～高骛远。To like; the opposite of 恶. 爱好 a hobby; 好学 fond of study; 好高骛远 to reach for what is beyond one's grasp.
  • ②容易发生某种情况。～感冒、地上～滑。Liable to. 好感冒 prone to catch cold; 地上好滑 the ground gets slippery easily.
