字义解释

meng³ 同音字 3

míng

  • ①鸟兽或昆虫叫。鸡～、蝉～、虫～。(of birds, beasts and insects) To cry. 鸡鸣 the cock crows; 蝉鸣 the cicadas sing.
  • ②发出声音；使发出声音。耳～、～枪、～锣开道。To sound; make sound. 耳鸣 ringing in the ears; 鸣枪 to fire a shot.
  • ③表达（情感、意见）。～谢、～冤、百家争～。To voice (a feeling or view). 鸣谢 to express thanks; 鸣冤 to cry out against injustice.
