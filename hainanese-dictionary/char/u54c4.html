字义解释

hhong¹ 同音字 3

hōng

  • ①许多人同时发出声音。～堂大笑、～传。(of many people) To make a noise together. 哄堂大笑 the whole room roared with laughter.
  • ②象声词。～的一声。A booming sound. 哄的一声 with a roar.
hhuang³ 同音字 5

hǒng

  • ①说假话骗人。～骗、你别～我。To fool; deceive. 哄骗 to hoodwink.
  • ②用言语或行动使人高兴。～孩子、～他开心。To coax; humour. 哄孩子 to soothe a child.
hhong⁵ 同音字 2

hòng

  • 吵闹；捣乱。起～、一～而散。To make a din; cause a disturbance. 起哄 to kick up a row; 一哄而散 to break up in confusion.
