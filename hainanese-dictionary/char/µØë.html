字义解释

diam¹ 同音字 3

shān

  • 〔shān〕
  • ①常绿乔木，树干高直，木材可做建筑材料。～树、～木、红～。China fir, an evergreen tree with a tall straight trunk whose timber is used in building.
  • 〔shā〕
  • ②用于口语，指杉木。～篙、～木板。colloquial reading, used of China fir timber and poles.
