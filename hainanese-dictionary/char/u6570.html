字义解释

diao⁴ 同音字 2

shù

用于第 ❶❷❸ 义

  • ①数目；数量。～量、次～、人～。Number; quantity. 数量 quantity; 次数 number of times; 人数 number of people.
  • ②数学上表示数量的概念。整～、分～、代～。In mathematics, a concept representing quantity. 整数 integer; 分数 fraction; 代数 algebra.
  • ③天命；命运。气～、在～难逃。Fate; destiny. 气数 one's allotted fate; 在数难逃 destined to be unavoidable.
  • ④表示不确定的少量。～人、～次、～日之后。Used to indicate an indefinite small number. 数人 several people; 数次 several times; 数日之后 after several days.
do⁴第 ❹ 义
diao⁴ 同音字 2

shǔ

  • ①点算；计算数目。～钱、不可胜～。To count; to calculate the number of. 数钱 to count money; 不可胜数 too many to count.
  • ②比较起来最突出或最值得一提。～一～二、就～他能干。To rank as the most outstanding or noteworthy. 数一数二 among the very best; 就数他能干 he is the most capable of them.
  • ③列举；责备。～落、历～其罪。To enumerate; to criticize or reproach. 数落 to scold or criticize; 历数其罪 to enumerate someone's offences.

shuò

  • 书屡次；频繁。～见不鲜、频～。<Lit.> Repeatedly; frequently. 数见不鲜 no longer surprising because it happens often; 频数 frequent.
