字义解释

hua⁴ 同音字 5

kè

  • ①教学的科目。语文～、～程、～本。A subject of study. 语文课 the Chinese lesson; 课程 a course; 课本 a textbook.
  • ②教学的时段。上～、～时、一节～。A period of teaching. 上课 to attend class; 课时 a class hour; 一节课 one lesson.
  • ③教材的段落。第一～、～文。A lesson in a textbook. 第一课 lesson one; 课文 the text.
  • ④旧时指赋税。～税、国～。Formerly, tax. 课税 to levy tax; 国课 state revenue.
