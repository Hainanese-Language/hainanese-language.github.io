so² 同音字 4

shǎ

  • ①头脑迟钝；不明事理。～子、～话、装疯卖～。Slow-witted; unable to see sense. 傻子 a fool; 傻话 nonsense; 装疯卖傻 to play the fool.
  • ②死心眼；不知变通。～干、～等、～劲儿。Single-minded to a fault. 傻干 to plug away blindly; 傻等 to wait doggedly; 傻劲儿 dogged effort.
