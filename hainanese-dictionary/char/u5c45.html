字义解释

gi¹ 同音字 21

jū

  • ①住。～住、～民、～室。To live; reside. 居住 to dwell; 居民 a resident; 居室 living quarters.
  • ②住处。故～、迁～、新～。A dwelling. 故居 a former residence; 迁居 to move house.
  • ③处在；处于。～中、～首、后来～上。To occupy a position. 居中 in the middle; 居首 to come first; 后来居上 the latecomer surpasses the rest.
  • ④存着；怀着。～心叵测。To harbour (an intention). 居心叵测 harbouring unfathomable designs.
  • ⑤当；自认为。以专家自～。To set oneself up as. 以专家自居 to pose as an expert.
