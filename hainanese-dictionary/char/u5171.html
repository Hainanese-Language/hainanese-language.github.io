字义解释

gong⁵ 同音字 2

gòng

  • ①相同的；共同具有的。～性、～识、～同、和平～处。Shared; held in common. 共性 a common quality; 共识 consensus; 共同 joint; 和平共处 peaceful coexistence.
  • ②一起；一同。～事、～勉、同甘～苦。Together. 共事 to work together; 共勉 to encourage one another; 同甘共苦 to share joys and hardships.
  • ③总计。总～、～计、～十五人。Altogether. 总共 in all; 共计 to total; 共十五人 fifteen people in all.
