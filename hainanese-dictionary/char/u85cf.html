藏
海南话
sang² dang⁶
普通话
cáng · zàng

字义解释

sang² 同音字 2

cáng

  • ①躲起来；不让人发现。躲～、～身、～头露尾。To hide; keep out of sight. 躲藏 to conceal oneself; 藏身 to take cover.
  • ②收存；储存。收～、～书、～污纳垢。To collect; store away. 收藏 to collect; 藏书 a book collection.
dang⁶

zàng

  • ①储存大量东西的地方。宝～、矿～。A place where much is stored. 宝藏 treasure; mineral wealth; 矿藏 mineral deposits.
  • ②佛教、道教经典的总称。大～经、三～。The canon of Buddhist or Daoist scriptures. 大藏经 the Tripitaka.
  • ③指西藏或藏族。～语、～历。Tibet; Tibetan. 藏语 the Tibetan language; 藏历 the Tibetan calendar.
