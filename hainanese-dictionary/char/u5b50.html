字义解释

zi³ 同音字 18

zǐ

  • ①古代指儿女，现专指儿子。～女、父～、～孙。Anciently children; now specifically a son. 子女 sons and daughters; 父子 father and son.
  • ②人的通称。男～、女～、士～。A person. 男子 a man; 女子 a woman; 士子 a scholar.
  • ③古代对有学问男子的尊称。孔～、诸～百家。An honorific for a learned man in antiquity. 孔子 Confucius; 诸子百家 the hundred schools of thought.
  • ④植物的种子。瓜～、菜～、结～。A seed. 瓜子 melon seeds; 结子 to set seed.
  • ⑤动物的卵。鱼～、蚕～。The egg of an animal. 鱼子 roe; 蚕子 silkworm eggs.
  • ⑥幼小的；嫩的。～鸡、～姜。Young; tender. 子鸡 a spring chicken; 子姜 young ginger.
  • ⑦地支的第一位。～时、～鼠。The first of the Earthly Branches. 子时 the hours from 11 pm to 1 am.
  • ⑧派生的；附属的。～公司、～目。Derived; subsidiary. 子公司 a subsidiary; 子目 a subheading.
