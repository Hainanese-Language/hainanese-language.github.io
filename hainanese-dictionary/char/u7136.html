字义解释

yin² 同音字 10

rán

  • ①对；不错。不以为～、～否。Right; correct. 不以为然 to think otherwise; 然否 whether so or not.
  • ②这样；那样。当～、不尽～、知其～。Thus; so. 当然 of course; 不尽然 not entirely so; 知其然 to know that it is so.
  • ③表示转折。～而、～则。Marking a contrast. 然而 however; 然则 that being so.
  • ④用作后缀，表示状态。忽～、显～、飘飘～。Suffix forming adverbs of state. 忽然 suddenly; 显然 evidently; 飘飘然 elated.
