字义解释

sa¹ 同音字 16

chā

  • ①区别；不相同。～别、～异、～距。Difference; distinction. 差别 difference; 差异 difference; 差距 gap.
  • ②错误；过失。～错、偏～、一念之～。An error; a mistake. 差错 error; 偏差 deviation; 一念之差 a momentary lapse in judgement.
  • ③数学上减法运算的得数。两数之～。The result of subtraction in mathematics. 两数之差 the difference between two numbers.
  • ④书稍微；大致。～可告慰。lit. Slightly; more or less. 差可告慰 somewhat comforting.
sa¹ 同音字 16

chà

  • ①不好；不够标准。～劲、质量太～。Poor; substandard. 差劲 poor; 质量太差 substandard quality.
  • ②欠缺；短少。不～多、还～一个人、相～甚远。To lack; to fall short. 不差多 almost; 还差一个人 one person short; 相差甚远 far apart.
sai¹ 同音字 8

chāi

  • ①派遣。～遣。To dispatch; to send. 差遣 to dispatch.
  • ②被派遣去做的事或职务。～事、出～、交～。An errand; an assigned task. 差事 an errand or job; 出差 to go on a business trip; 交差 to hand over a completed task.
  • ③旧被派遣去办事的人。～役、信～。former A person sent on an errand. 差役 a yamen runner; 信差 a courier.
