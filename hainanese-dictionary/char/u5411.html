字义解释

hhio⁴

xiàng

  • ①方向；目标。风～、志～、方～。A direction; an aim. 风向 wind direction; 志向 aspiration.
  • ②对着；朝着，跟"背"相对。～阳、面～大海、～前看。To face — the opposite of 背. 向阳 facing the sun; 向前看 to look ahead.
  • ③表示动作的方向。～东走、～群众学习。Marks the direction of an action. 向东走 to go east; 向群众学习 to learn from the masses.
  • ④偏袒。偏～、别老～着他。To take sides with. 偏向 to be partial; 别老向着他 stop taking his side.
  • ⑤〈书〉从来；一向。～有研究、～无此例。〈lit.〉 All along. 向无此例 there has never been such a precedent.
