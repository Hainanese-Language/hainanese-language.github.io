字义解释

bao¹ 同音字 7

bāo

  • ①用纸、布等裹起来。～饺子、～扎、～装。To wrap in paper, cloth and the like. 包饺子 to make dumplings; 包扎 to bind up; 包装 to package.
  • ②包装好的东西。药～、邮～、～裹。A wrapped parcel. 药包 a packet of medicine; 邮包 a postal parcel; 包裹 a package.
  • ③装东西的口袋。书～、皮～、背～。A bag for carrying things. 书包 a satchel; 皮包 a briefcase; 背包 a rucksack.
  • ④量词，用于成包的东西。一～糖、两～水泥。Measure word for things in packets. 一包糖 a packet of sweets; 两包水泥 two bags of cement.
  • ⑤容纳在内；总括在一起。～含、～括、无所不～。To contain; to take in together. 包含 to comprise; 包括 to include; 无所不包 all-embracing.
  • ⑥承担；担保。～办、～干、～在我身上。To take on; to guarantee. 包办 to take sole charge of; 包干 to be responsible for a task; 包在我身上 leave it to me.
  • ⑦约定专用。～车、～场、～机。To hire for exclusive use. 包车 to hire a car; 包场 to book the whole house; 包机 a chartered flight.
  • ⑧物体上鼓起的疙瘩。头上碰了个～。A lump raised on something. 头上碰了个包 to raise a bump on one's head.
