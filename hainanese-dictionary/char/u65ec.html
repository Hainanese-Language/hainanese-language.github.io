字义解释

dun² 同音字 15

xún

  • ①十天为一旬，一个月分上、中、下三旬。上～、下～、～刊。A period of ten days, a month being divided into three. 上旬 the first ten days; 下旬 the last ten days; 旬刊 a publication issued every ten days.
  • ②用于年龄，十岁为一旬。年过六～。A period of ten years, used of age. 年过六旬 over sixty years old.
