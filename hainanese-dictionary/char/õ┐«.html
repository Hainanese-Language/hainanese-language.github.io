字义解释

diu¹ 同音字 3

xiū

  • ①使损坏的东西恢复原状。～理、～车、～补、维～。To restore what is damaged. 修理 to repair; 修车 to fix a vehicle; 修补 to mend; 维修 to service.
  • ②建造；兴建。～建、～路、～水库。To build. 修建 to construct; 修路 to build a road; 修水库 to build a reservoir.
  • ③剪削使整齐。～剪、～指甲、～树枝。To trim. 修剪 to prune; 修指甲 to do one's nails; 修树枝 to trim branches.
  • ④钻研学习；培养品德。～学、进～、～养、～身。To study; to cultivate one's character. 修学 to pursue studies; 进修 to take further training; 修养 self-cultivation; 修身 to cultivate oneself.
  • ⑤编写；撰述。～史、编～、～订。To compile; to write up. 修史 to compile a history; 编修 to compile; 修订 to revise.
  • ⑥〈书〉长；高。～长、～竹。〈lit.〉 Long; tall. 修长 slender; 修竹 tall bamboo.
