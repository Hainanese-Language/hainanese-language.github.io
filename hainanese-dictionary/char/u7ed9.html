字义解释

gib⁷ 同音字 9

gěi

  • 〔gěi〕
  • ①使对方得到。～他一本书、送～。To give. 给他一本书 to give him a book; 送给 to present to.
  • ②叫；让。～我看看。To let; allow. 给我看看 let me have a look.
  • ③介词，替；为。～大家帮忙。Preposition: for; on behalf of. 给大家帮忙 to help everyone out.
  • ④介词，向；对。～老师问好。Preposition: to. 给老师问好 to send one's regards to the teacher.
  • ⑤助词，用在动词前，加强语气。碗～打了。Particle before a verb, adding emphasis. 碗给打了 the bowl got broken.
  • 〔jǐ〕
  • ⑥供应。供～、补～、自～自足。To supply. 供给 to provide; 补给 to replenish; 自给自足 self-sufficient.
  • ⑦富裕；充足。家～人足。Well provided; ample. 家给人足 every household well off.
