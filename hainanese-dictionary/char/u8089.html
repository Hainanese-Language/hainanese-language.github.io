字义解释

hhiog⁸

ròu

  • ①人或动物体内红色柔软的组织。肌～、猪～、～食。The soft red tissue of a human or animal body. 肌肉 muscle; 猪肉 pork; 肉食 a meat diet.
  • ②某些瓜果里可以吃的部分。果～、桂圆～。The edible part of certain fruits. 果肉 pulp; 桂圆肉 dried longan flesh.
  • ③〈方〉不脆；性子慢。这西瓜～、～脾气。〈dial.〉 Not crisp; slow in temperament. 这西瓜肉 this melon is mushy; 肉脾气 a sluggish nature.
