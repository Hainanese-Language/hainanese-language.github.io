字义解释

yim⁵ 同音字 3

rèn

  • ①任用；委派。～命、～免、委～、～人唯贤。To appoint. 任命 to appoint; 任免 to appoint and remove; 委任 to commission; 任人唯贤 to appoint on merit.
  • ②担当；承受。担～、～职、～劳～怨。To take on; to bear. 担任 to hold a post; 任职 to serve in office; 任劳任怨 to work hard and bear criticism.
  • ③职务。上～、就～、到～、连～。A post. 上任 to take office; 就任 to assume a post; 到任 to arrive at one's post; 连任 to serve a further term.
  • ④听凭；不加干涉。～意、～性、听～、放～自流。To let be; to leave alone. 任意 arbitrarily; 任性 wilful; 听任 to let something take its course; 放任自流 to let things drift.
  • ⑤连词，不论；无论。～谁也说不动他。No matter; whoever or whatever. 任谁也说不动他 no one can talk him round.
  • ⑥量词，用于担任职务的次数。做过两～校长。Measure word for terms of office. 做过两任校长 to have been principal twice.
yim² 同音字 3

rén

  • ①姓。A surname.
  • ②用于地名，如任丘（在河北）。Used in place names, such as 任丘 Renqiu in Hebei.
