字义解释

ddang⁵

zhòng

  • ①分量大，跟"轻"相对。～担、举～、这箱子很～。Heavy — the opposite of 轻. 重担 a heavy load; 这箱子很重 this case is heavy.
  • ②分量。体～、净～、举足轻～。Weight. 体重 body weight; 举足轻重 to hold the balance.
  • ③程度深。～伤、～病、～感冒。Serious; severe. 重伤 a serious injury; 重病 a grave illness.
  • ④重要。～点、～任、军事～地。Important. 重点 a focal point; 重任 a heavy responsibility.
  • ⑤重视；看重。尊～、器～、～男轻女。To value. 尊重 to respect; 重男轻女 to favour sons over daughters.
song² 同音字 5

chóng

  • ①再；又。～来、～逢、旧地～游。Again. 重来 to start over; 旧地重游 to revisit an old haunt.
  • ②层。～叠、双～领导、万～山。A layer. 重叠 to overlap; 万重山 range upon range of mountains.
