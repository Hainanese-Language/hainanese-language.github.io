字义解释

ziang⁶ 同音字 13

zhàng

  • ①战争；战斗。打～、胜～、这一～打得漂亮。War; a battle. 打仗 to fight a war; 胜仗 a victory; 这一仗打得漂亮 that was a fine battle.
  • ②拿着（兵器）。～剑。To hold (a weapon). 仗剑 sword in hand.
  • ③倚靠；凭借。倚～、依～、～势欺人。To rely on. 倚仗 to lean on; 依仗 to count on; 仗势欺人 to bully others on the strength of one's backing.
  • ④兵器的总称。仪～、明火执～。Weapons generally. 仪仗 a guard of honour; 明火执仗 to act openly and brazenly.
