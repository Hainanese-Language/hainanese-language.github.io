hi³ 同音字 6

tǐ

  • 〔tǐ〕
  • ①人或动物的全身。身～、～重、～温、遍～鳞伤。The body of a person or animal. 身体 the body; 体重 weight; 体温 body temperature; 遍体鳞伤 covered with cuts and bruises.
  • ②物体；事物的整体。固～、液～、集～、全～。An object; a thing taken as a whole. 固体 a solid; 液体 a liquid; 集体 a collective; 全体 the whole body.
  • ③事物的格式或规矩。～裁、～例、文～、字～。The form or rules a thing follows. 体裁 a literary form; 体例 stylistic rules; 文体 a style of writing; 字体 a typeface.
  • ④亲身；设身处地。～验、～会、～察。To experience for oneself; to put oneself in another's place. 体验 to experience; 体会 to realize from experience; 体察 to observe closely.
  • ⑤关怀；同情。～谅、～恤、～贴。To show concern for. 体谅 to make allowances for; 体恤 to sympathize with; 体贴 to be considerate.
  • 〔tī〕
  • ⑥用于"体己"，指个人的私蓄，也指亲近贴心的。～己钱、～己话。Used in 体己: private savings, and also close or intimate. 体己钱 private savings; 体己话 confidences.
