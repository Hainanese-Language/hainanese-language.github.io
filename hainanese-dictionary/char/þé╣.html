字义解释

ddiam³ 同音字 2

diǎn

  • ①液体的小滴。雨～、水～。A drop of liquid. 雨点 raindrops; 水点 droplets.
  • ②细小的痕迹。斑～、污～、黑～。A small mark. 斑点 a speck; 污点 a stain; 黑点 a black spot.
  • ③汉字的笔画"丶"；也指小数点。三～水、三～五。The stroke 丶 in Chinese characters; also the decimal point. 三点水 the water radical; 三点五 three point five.
  • ④几何学上指只有位置而没有大小的图形。～线面。In geometry, a point. 点线面 point, line and plane.
  • ⑤一定的地方或标志。地～、起～、沸～。A fixed place or marker. 地点 a location; 起点 a starting point; 沸点 boiling point.
  • ⑥事物的方面或部分。优～、重～、特～。An aspect or part of something. 优点 a merit; 重点 the key point; 特点 a characteristic.
  • ⑦用笔加上点子。～题、画龙～睛。To mark with a dot. 点题 to bring out the theme; 画龙点睛 to add the finishing touch.
  • ⑧一下一下地向下动。～头、～穴。To move downward briefly. 点头 to nod; 点穴 to press an acupoint.
  • ⑨使液体一滴一滴地落下。～眼药。To let fall drop by drop. 点眼药 to put in eye drops.
  • ⑩引着火。～火、～灯、～燃。To light. 点火 to kindle; 点灯 to light a lamp; 点燃 to ignite.
  • ⑪一个一个地查对。～名、清～、～货。To check one by one. 点名 to call the roll; 清点 to take stock; 点货 to check goods.
  • ⑫指示；启发。～破、指～、～拨。 ⑬ 计时单位；规定的时刻。五～钟、误～。 ⑭ 量词，表示少量。一～小事、有～冷。To hint; prompt. 点破 to lay bare; 指点 to give guidance; 点拨 to coach. ⑬ An hour; an appointed time. 五点钟 five o'clock; 误点 behind schedule. ⑭ Measure word for a small amount. 一点小事 a trifling matter; 有点冷 a bit cold.
