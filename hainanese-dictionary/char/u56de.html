字义解释

ddui³

huí

  • ①曲折环绕。～旋、～廊、迂～。To circle; wind about. 回旋 to revolve; 回廊 a winding corridor; 迂回 roundabout.
  • ②从别处到原来的地方。～家、～国、～来。To return to where one came from. 回家 to go home; 回来 to come back.
  • ③掉转。～头、～过身来。To turn round. 回头 to turn one's head; 回过身来 to turn about.
  • ④答复；报答。～信、～话、～敬。To reply; return in kind. 回信 to write back; 回敬 to return a courtesy.
  • ⑤谢绝；辞去。～绝、把他～了。To decline; dismiss. 回绝 to refuse; 把他回了 turned him away.
  • ⑥量词，用于事情或章回。两～事、第一～。Measure word for occurrences and for chapters. 两回事 two different matters; 第一回 chapter one.
  • ⑦指回族。～民。The Hui people. 回民 a Hui Muslim.
