字义解释

ge³ 同音字 2

jiǎ

  • ①不真实的；伪造的；跟"真"相对。～话、～发、～钞、弄虚作～。False; forged; the opposite of 真. 假话 a lie; 假发 a wig; 假钞 counterfeit notes; 弄虚作假 to resort to deception.
  • ②借用；利用。～手于人、～公济私、久～不归。To borrow; to make use of. 假手于人 to get someone else to do the dirty work; 假公济私 to use public office for private gain; 久假不归 to keep what one has borrowed.
  • ③假如；如果。～如、～使、～若。If. 假如 supposing; 假使 in case; 假若 if.
ge⁴ 同音字 6

jià

  • 按规定或经批准暂时不工作、不学习的时间。放～、请～、寒～、～期。Time off from work or study, granted by rule or approval. 放假 to have a holiday; 请假 to ask for leave; 寒假 the winter vacation; 假期 a period of leave.
