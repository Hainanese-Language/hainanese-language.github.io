字义解释

guad⁷ 同音字 4

guā

  • ①用刀等贴着物体表面移动，去掉附着的东西。～脸、～锅、～目相看。To move a blade along a surface to remove what is on it. 刮脸 to shave; 刮锅 to scrape a pot; 刮目相看 to look at someone with new eyes.
  • ②风吹动。～风、～了一夜北风。Of wind, to blow. 刮风 to be windy; 刮了一夜北风 the north wind blew all night.
  • ③掠夺财物。搜～民财。To extort property. 搜刮民财 to fleece the people.
