普通话

字义解释

yin² 同音字 10

rén

  • ①同情、爱护他人的心。～爱、～慈、～义、～至义尽。Kindness and love towards others. 仁爱 benevolence; 仁慈 merciful; 仁义 humanity and justice; 仁至义尽 to do all one can for someone.
  • ②果核或果壳里可以吃的部分。杏～、花生～、虾～。The edible kernel inside a stone or shell. 杏仁 an almond; 花生仁 a peanut kernel; 虾仁 shelled shrimps.
  • ③敬辞，用于称同辈朋友。～兄。Polite form used to a friend of one's own generation. 仁兄 my dear friend.
  • ④感觉灵敏。麻木不～。Responsive to sensation. 麻木不仁 numb and unfeeling.
