字义解释

guan¹ 同音字 7

guān

  • ①使开着的物体合上。～门、～窗、把箱子～上。To shut something that is open. 关门 to close a door; 关窗 to shut a window; 把箱子关上 to close a case.
  • ②使机器等停止运转。～灯、～机、～掉电源。To switch off machinery. 关灯 to turn off the light; 关机 to shut down; 关掉电源 to cut the power.
  • ③拘禁。～押、把人～起来。To lock up. 关押 to detain; 把人关起来 to shut someone up.
  • ④古代设在交通要道的守卫处所。～口、～卡、山海～、～税。A guard post on a main route in former times. 关口 a pass; 关卡 a checkpoint; 山海关 Shanhaiguan; 关税 customs duty.
  • ⑤起转折关联作用的部分。～节、～键、～头。A part that links or turns. 关节 a joint; 关键 the crux; 关头 a critical juncture.
  • ⑥牵连；涉及。～系、～心、无～、～乎大局。To involve; to concern. 关系 a relation; 关心 to be concerned about; 无关 unrelated; 关乎大局 to bear on the whole situation.
