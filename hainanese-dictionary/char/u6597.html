字义解释

ddao² 同音字 6

dǒu

  • ①容量单位，十升为一斗。～米、车载～量。A dou, a measure of capacity equal to ten sheng. 斗米 a dou of rice; 车载斗量 by the cartload — too many to count.
  • ②量粮食的器具。～筲之人。A vessel for measuring grain. 斗筲之人 a person of small capacity.
  • ③形状略像斗的东西。漏～、烟～、～笠。Something shaped like a dou. 漏斗 a funnel; 烟斗 a pipe; 斗笠 a bamboo hat.
  • ④星宿名。北～星、气冲～牛。A constellation. 北斗星 the Plough; 气冲斗牛 with soaring spirit.
ddou⁴ 同音字 3

dòu

  • ①对打；争斗。～殴、搏～、～争。To fight. 斗殴 to brawl; 搏斗 to grapple; 斗争 to struggle.
  • ②使动物相斗。～鸡、～蟋蟀。To set animals fighting. 斗鸡 cockfighting; 斗蟋蟀 cricket fighting.
  • ③比赛胜负。～智、～嘴、～法。To contend for mastery. 斗智 a battle of wits; 斗嘴 to bicker.
  • ④拼合；凑近。～榫、～在一起。To fit together. 斗榫 to join by mortise and tenon; 斗在一起 pieced together.
