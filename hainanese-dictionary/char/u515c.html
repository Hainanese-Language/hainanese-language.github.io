字义解释

ddou⁶ 同音字 4

dōu

  • ①口袋一类的东西。裤～、网～、～子。A pocket or pouch. 裤兜 a trouser pocket; 网兜 a string bag; 兜子 a pouch.
  • ②做成兜形把东西拢住。用手巾～着、～住。To hold something in a fold of cloth. 用手巾兜着 to carry it in a handkerchief; 兜住 to catch and hold.
  • ③环绕；围。～圈子、～抄、～风。To circle; to go round. 兜圈子 to go round in circles; 兜抄 to close in from both sides; 兜风 to go for a spin.
  • ④承担；负责。～揽、～底、有事我～着。To take on; to answer for. 兜揽 to canvass for; 兜底 to reveal the whole thing; 有事我兜着 I'll take the responsibility.
