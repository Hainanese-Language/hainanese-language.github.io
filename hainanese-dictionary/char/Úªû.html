字义解释

diu³ 同音字 3

shǒu

  • ①头。～饰、昂～阔步、～当其冲。The head. 首饰 jewellery; 昂首阔步 to stride with head held high; 首当其冲 to bear the brunt.
  • ②领导人；带头的。～领、元～、～脑、罪魁祸～。A leader; the one at the head. 首领 a chief; 元首 a head of state; 首脑 a leader; 罪魁祸首 the chief culprit.
  • ③第一；最高的。～都、～席、～位。First; highest. 首都 a capital; 首席 the chief seat; 首位 first place.
  • ④最先；开始。～创、～演、～届。Earliest; initial. 首创 to pioneer; 首演 a premiere; 首届 the first session.
  • ⑤出面告发。自～、出～。To give oneself up or inform. 自首 to surrender to the authorities; 出首 to inform against someone.
  • ⑥量词，用于诗词歌曲。一～诗、两～歌。Measure word for poems and songs. 一首诗 one poem; 两首歌 two songs.
