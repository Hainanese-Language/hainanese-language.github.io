字义解释

ddog⁸ 同音字 8

dú

  • ①单一的；一个的。～生子、～木桥、～一无二。Single; sole. 独生子 an only son; 独木桥 a single-plank bridge; 独一无二 unique.
  • ②独自地。～居、～来～往、～当一面。Alone; by oneself. 独居 to live alone; 独来独往 to come and go on one's own; 独当一面 to take sole charge of a field.
  • ③只有；唯独。～有他没来、～树一帜。Only; alone among others. 独有他没来 he alone did not come; 独树一帜 to fly a banner of one's own.
  • ④老而无子的人。鳏寡孤～。An old person without children. 鳏寡孤独 widowers, widows, orphans and the childless.
