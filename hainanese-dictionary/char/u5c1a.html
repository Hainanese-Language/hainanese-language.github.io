字义解释

diang⁵ 同音字 4

shàng

  • ①还；仍然。～未完成、年纪～小。Still; yet. 尚未完成 not yet finished; 年纪尚小 still young.
  • ②尊崇；注重。崇～、～武。To esteem; set store by. 崇尚 to advocate; 尚武 to prize martial spirit.
  • ③风气；习惯。风～、时～。A prevailing custom. 风尚 a trend; 时尚 fashion.
