字义解释

zeg⁷ 同音字 38

jí

  • ①就是。非此～彼、～是。To be precisely. 非此即彼 either this or that; 即是 that is to say.
  • ②就；便。一触～发、招之～来、～将。At once; then. 一触即发 ready to go off at a touch; 招之即来 to come the moment one is called; 即将 about to.
  • ③靠近；接触。～位、若～若离、可望而不可～。To draw near; to come into contact with. 即位 to ascend the throne; 若即若离 to keep at arm's length; 可望而不可即 within sight but out of reach.
  • ④当时；当下。～日、～刻、～席。At that very time. 即日 that same day; 即刻 immediately; 即席 impromptu.
  • ⑤〈书〉即使。～便、～或。〈lit.〉 Even if. 即便 even though; 即或 even supposing.
