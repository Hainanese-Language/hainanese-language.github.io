字义解释

duang¹ 同音字 6

zhuāng

  • ①修饰打扮。梳～、化～、～扮。To make up; adorn oneself. 梳妆 to dress one's hair; 化妆 to put on make-up.
  • ②女子身上的装饰。红～、卸～。A woman's attire and adornment. 红妆 a woman's finery; 卸妆 to remove one's make-up.
  • ③嫁妆。送～、～奁。A dowry. 送妆 to send the trousseau; 妆奁 a bride's outfit.
