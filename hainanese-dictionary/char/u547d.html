字义解释

mia¹

mìng

  • ①生命。～根、性～、救～、～在旦夕。Life. 命根 one's very life; 性命 life; 救命 to save a life; 命在旦夕 to be at death's door.
  • ②上级的指示；发布指示。奉～、遵～、～令。An order from above; to issue one. 奉命 by order; 遵命 to comply with an order; 命令 an order.
  • ③给予（名称等）。～名、～题。To assign (a name and the like). 命名 to name; 命题 to set a topic.
  • ④迷信认为生来注定的遭遇。～运、算～、认～。Fate, in superstitious belief. 命运 destiny; 算命 to tell fortunes; 认命 to resign oneself to fate.
