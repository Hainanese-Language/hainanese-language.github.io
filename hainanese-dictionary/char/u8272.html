字义解释

deg⁷ 同音字 15

sè

  • 〔sè〕
  • ①颜色。红～、～彩、～调、五颜六～。Colour. 红色 red; 色彩 hue; 色调 a tone; 五颜六色 of all colours.
  • ②脸上的表情。脸～、神～、和颜悦～、喜形于～。The look on one's face. 脸色 one's complexion; 神色 an expression; 和颜悦色 with a kindly face; 喜形于色 to beam with joy.
  • ③情景；景象。景～、夜～、行～匆匆。A scene; a sight. 景色 scenery; 夜色 the look of the night; 行色匆匆 to set off in haste.
  • ④种类。各～各样、货～齐全。A kind. 各色各样 of every sort; 货色齐全 a full range of goods.
  • ⑤质量。成～、足～。Quality. 成色 the fineness of gold or silver; 足色 of full fineness.
  • ⑥女子的美貌。姿～、～艺双全。A woman's good looks. 姿色 good looks; 色艺双全 gifted in both beauty and art.
  • 〔shǎi〕
  • ⑦"色"的口语音，指颜色。掉～、这布～儿正。The colloquial reading of 色, meaning colour. 掉色 to fade; 这布色儿正 this cloth has a true colour.
