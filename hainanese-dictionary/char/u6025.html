字义解释

gib⁷ 同音字 9

jí

  • ①焦躁。着～、～躁、～得直跺脚。Anxious; impatient. 着急 worried; 急躁 edgy; 急得直跺脚 stamping with anxiety.
  • ②使着急。这事真～人。To make anxious. 这事真急人 this is a worrying business.
  • ③快而猛烈。～流、～行军、水流很～。Rapid; violent. 急流 a torrent; 急行军 a forced march; 水流很急 the current runs fast.
  • ④迫切；紧要。～需、～事、当务之～。Urgent. 急需 urgently needed; 急事 a pressing matter; 当务之急 the most urgent task.
  • ⑤赶紧帮助。～人所难、～公好义。To rush to another's aid. 急人所难 to help others in trouble; 急公好义 public-spirited.
