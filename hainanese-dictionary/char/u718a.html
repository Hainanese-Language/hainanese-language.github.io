字义解释

hhiong² 同音字 2

xióng

  • ①哺乳动物，身体大，尾巴短，能爬树。狗～、北极～。The bear, a large short-tailed mammal that can climb trees. 狗熊 a black bear; 北极熊 a polar bear.
  • ②〈方〉斥责。挨了一顿～。〈dial.〉 To upbraid. 挨了一顿熊 to get a dressing-down.
  • ③〈方〉怯懦；无能。你真～。〈dial.〉 Cowardly; useless. 你真熊 you really are hopeless.
