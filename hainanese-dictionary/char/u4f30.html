字义解释

gu¹ 同音字 16

gū

  • 〔gū〕
  • ①大致推算。～计、～量、～价、低～。To reckon roughly. 估计 to estimate; 估量 to appraise; 估价 to value; 低估 to underestimate.
  • 〔gù〕
  • ②用于"估衣"，指出售的旧衣服或质地较次的成衣。～衣铺。Used in 估衣: second-hand or cheap ready-made clothes for sale. 估衣铺 a second-hand clothes shop.
