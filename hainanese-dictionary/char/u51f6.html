字义解释

hhiong¹ 同音字 3

xiōng

  • ①不幸的；跟"吉"相对。～事、～信、吉～未卜。Ill-fated; the opposite of 吉. 凶事 a funeral; 凶信 news of a death; 吉凶未卜 the outcome is uncertain.
  • ②年成很坏。～年、～荒。Of a bad harvest. 凶年 a lean year; 凶荒 famine.
  • ③厉害；程度深。闹得太～、雨下得真～。Fierce; to a high degree. 闹得太凶 to make far too much of a row; 雨下得真凶 it is pouring down.
  • ④凶恶；残暴。～狠、～残、～相毕露。Cruel; brutal. 凶狠 ferocious; 凶残 savage; 凶相毕露 to show one's true, ugly face.
  • ⑤行凶的人或行为。～手、～器、帮～、行～。One who commits an act of violence, or the act itself. 凶手 a murderer; 凶器 a murder weapon; 帮凶 an accomplice; 行凶 to commit an act of violence.
