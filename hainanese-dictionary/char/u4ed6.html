字义解释

ha¹ 同音字 5

tā

  • ①人称代词，称自己和对方以外的某个人。～们、告诉～一声。He; a third person other than oneself and the person addressed. 他们 they; 告诉他一声 let him know.
  • ②别的；另外的。～乡、～人、其～、别无～求。Other; another. 他乡 a place away from home; 他人 others; 其他 the rest; 别无他求 to ask for nothing else.
  • ③虚指，用在动词和数量词之间。睡～一觉、唱～几句。Used between a verb and a quantity, without referring to anything definite. 睡他一觉 to have a good sleep; 唱他几句 to sing a few lines.
