字义解释

lang² 同音字 9

láng

  • ①旧时对年轻男子的称呼。～才女貌、令～。In former times, a form of address for a young man. 郎才女貌 a gifted man and a beautiful woman; 令郎 your son.
  • ②女子称丈夫或情人。～君、情～。A woman's word for her husband or lover. 郎君 my husband; 情郎 a sweetheart.
  • ③旧时某些官名。侍～、员外～。Certain official titles in former times. 侍郎 a vice-minister; 员外郎 a departmental official.
  • ④称从事某种职业的人。货～、放牛～。One engaged in a particular trade. 货郎 a pedlar; 放牛郎 a cowherd.
