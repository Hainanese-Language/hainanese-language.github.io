bēng

  • ①倒塌。～塌、山～地裂。To collapse. 崩塌 to cave in; 山崩地裂 the mountains crumble and the earth splits.
  • ②破裂。～裂、气球吹～了。To burst. 崩裂 to split apart; 气球吹崩了 the balloon burst.
  • ③被崩裂的东西击中。～了一脸泥。To be struck by something flying off. 崩了一脸泥 got splattered with mud.
  • ④君主时代称帝王死。驾～。(of an emperor) To die. 驾崩 the emperor's demise.
