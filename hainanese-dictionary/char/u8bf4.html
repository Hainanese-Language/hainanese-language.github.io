字义解释

de⁶

shuō

  • ①用话表达意思。～话、～明、诉～。To say; to speak. 说话 to talk; 说明 to explain; 诉说 to relate.
  • ②解释。～文解字、这话怎么～？To interpret. 说文解字 to explain characters; 这话怎么说？ how is that to be understood?
  • ③言论；主张。学～、著书立～、邪～。A doctrine; a view. 学说 a theory; 著书立说 to write and put forward a doctrine; 邪说 a heresy.
  • ④责备。挨～、他～了我一顿。To scold. 挨说 to be told off; 他说了我一顿 he gave me a talking-to.
  • ⑤介绍；说合。～媒、～亲。To act as go-between. 说媒 to arrange a match; 说亲 to make a marriage proposal.
