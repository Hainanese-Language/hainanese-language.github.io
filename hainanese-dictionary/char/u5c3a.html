字义解释

sio⁶ 同音字 2

chǐ

  • ①市制长度单位，一尺等于十寸。三～布、～寸。A chi, a unit of length equal to ten cun. 三尺布 three chi of cloth; 尺寸 measurements.
  • ②量长度的器具。皮～、卷～、～子。A ruler. 皮尺 a tape measure; 卷尺 a retractable tape.
  • ③像尺的东西。计算～、镇～。Something shaped like a ruler. 计算尺 a slide rule; 镇尺 a paperweight.
se¹ 同音字 26

chě

  • 我国传统乐谱的记音符号之一。工～谱。One of the note symbols in traditional Chinese notation. 工尺谱 gongche notation.
