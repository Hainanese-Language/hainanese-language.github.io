niang²

niáng

  • ①母亲。～亲、爹～。Mother. 娘亲 one's mother; 爹娘 father and mother.
  • ②称长一辈或年长的已婚妇女。大～、婶～。A form of address for an older married woman. 大娘 auntie; 婶娘 an uncle's wife.
  • ③年轻女子。姑～、新～。A young woman. 姑娘 a girl; 新娘 a bride.
