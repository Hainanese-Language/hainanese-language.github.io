字义解释

hhui⁵ 同音字 3

huì

  • 〔huì〕
  • ①聚合；合在一起。～合、～师、～诊。To come together; to converge. 会合 to assemble; 会师 to join forces; 会诊 a joint consultation.
  • ②有目的的集合。开～、晚～、～场、～议。A gathering held for a purpose. 开会 to hold a meeting; 晚会 an evening party; 会场 a meeting place; 会议 a conference.
  • ③团体；组织。工～、协～、～员。A body; an organization. 工会 a trade union; 协会 an association; 会员 a member.
  • ④见面；会见。～客、～面、约～。To meet; to see someone. 会客 to receive a visitor; 会面 to meet; 约会 an appointment.
  • ⑤理解；懂得。领～、误～、心领神～。To grasp; to understand. 领会 to comprehend; 误会 to misunderstand; 心领神会 to understand tacitly.
  • ⑥能；擅长。～说英语、他很～办事。To be able to; to be good at. 会说英语 to speak English; 他很会办事 he handles things well.
  • ⑦表示有可能。明天～下雨、他不～来了。Indicating likelihood. 明天会下雨 it will rain tomorrow; 他不会来了 he won't be coming.
  • ⑧短暂的时间。一～儿、多～儿。A short while. 一会儿 a moment; 多会儿 when.
  • ⑨大城市。都～、省～。A large city. 都会 a metropolis; 省会 a provincial capital.

kuài

  • 〔kuài〕
  • ⑩用于"会计"，指管理和核算财务的工作或人员。Used in 会计: accounting, or an accountant.
