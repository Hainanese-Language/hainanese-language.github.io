字义解释

duad⁷ 同音字 10

shuā

  • 〔shuā〕
  • ①刷子，清除污垢或涂抹用的用具。牙～、板～、鞋～。A brush, used for cleaning or applying something. 牙刷 a toothbrush; 板刷 a scrubbing brush; 鞋刷 a shoe brush.
  • ②用刷子清除或涂抹。～牙、～鞋、～标语、～油漆。To clean or apply with a brush. 刷牙 to brush one's teeth; 刷鞋 to brush shoes; 刷标语 to paint up a slogan; 刷油漆 to apply paint.
  • ③淘汰。～下来、第一轮就被～掉了。To eliminate from a selection. 刷下来 to be cut; 第一轮就被刷掉了 to be eliminated in the first round.
  • ④拟声，形容迅速擦过的声音。～地站了起来。Imitating the sound of a swift movement. 刷地站了起来 to stand up with a swish.
  • 〔shuà〕
  • ⑤用于"刷白"，形容白而略显苍白的颜色。脸色～白。Only in 刷白: pale white. 脸色刷白 to turn pale.
