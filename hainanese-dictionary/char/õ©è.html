字义解释

zio¹ 同音字 4

shàng

  • ①位置在高处的，跟"下"相对。～面、～游、楼～。Higher in position — the opposite of 下. 上面 above; 上游 upper reaches; 楼上 upstairs.
  • ②次序或时间在前的。～次、～半年、～文。Earlier in order or time. 上次 last time; 上半年 the first half of the year; 上文 the preceding text.
zio⁵ 同音字 2

shàng

  • ①由低处到高处。～山、～楼、～台阶。To go up; to move from a lower place to a higher one. 上山 to climb a hill; 上楼 to go upstairs; 上台阶 to go up the steps.
  • ②去；到。～街、～班、～北京。To go; to go to. 上街 to go out into the street; 上班 to go to work; 上北京 to go to Beijing.
  • ③向前进。～前、见困难就～。To go forward. 上前 to step forward; 见困难就上 to face difficulties head-on.
  • ④添加；补足。～水、～货、～肥。To add; to replenish. 上水 to fill with water; 上货 to restock; 上肥 to apply fertiliser.
  • ⑤装；安。～刺刀、～螺丝。To fit; to install. 上刺刀 to fix a bayonet; 上螺丝 to put in a screw.
  • ⑥涂；抹。～药、～色、～油漆。To apply; to spread on. 上药 to apply medicine; 上色 to colour; 上油漆 to paint.
  • ⑦登载；记上。～报、～账。To publish; to enter in a record. 上报 to appear in the press; 上账 to enter in the accounts.
  • ⑧拧紧。～弦、～发条。To wind up; to tighten. 上弦 to wind; 上发条 to wind a spring.
  • ⑨达到；够。～百人、～年纪。To reach; to amount to. 上百人 over a hundred people; 上年纪 to be getting on in years.
  • ⑩用在动词后，表示由低到高、开始或达到目的。爬～、爱～、考～大学。Used after a verb to indicate upward movement, a beginning, or attainment of a goal. 爬上 to climb up; 爱上 to fall in love with; 考上大学 to get into university.
siang² 同音字 4

shàng

  • ①旧时乐谱记音符号之一，相当于简谱的"1"。An old musical notation symbol, equivalent to low "1" in numbered musical notation.
diang⁵ 同音字 4

shàng

  • ①等级或品质高的。～等、～策、～宾。Higher in grade or quality. 上等 first-rate; 上策 the best course; 上宾 a guest of honour.
diang⁵ 同音字 4

shǎng

  • 上声，汉语声调之一。
