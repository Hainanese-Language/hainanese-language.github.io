字义解释

zio⁴ 同音字 3

zhào

  • ①光线射在物体上。阳光～在窗上、～射。(of light) To shine on. 阳光照在窗上 sunlight falls on the window; 照射 to irradiate.
  • ②对着镜子等看自己的影像。～镜子。To look at oneself in a mirror. 照镜子 to look in the glass.
  • ③摄影。～相、拍～。To photograph. 照相 to take a photo; 拍照 to snap a picture.
  • ④照片。剧～、玉～。A photograph. 剧照 a still; 玉照 your photograph.
  • ⑤看顾。～料、～看、关～。To look after. 照料 to take care of; 照看 to mind; 关照 to keep an eye on.
  • ⑥对着；向着。～这个方向走。Facing; towards. 照这个方向走 go in this direction.
  • ⑦依照；按照。～办、依～、～原样。According to. 照办 to act accordingly; 依照 in conformity with.
  • ⑧明白；知晓。心～不宣。To understand. 心照不宣 a tacit mutual understanding.
  • ⑨凭证。护～、执～、牌～。A licence; a permit. 护照 a passport; 执照 a licence; 牌照 a registration plate.
