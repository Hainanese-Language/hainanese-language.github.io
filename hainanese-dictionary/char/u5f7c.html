字义解释

bi² 同音字 3

bǐ

  • ①那；那个；跟"此"相对。～岸、顾此失～、由此及～。That; the other — the opposite of 此. 彼岸 the far shore; 顾此失彼 to attend to one thing and lose sight of another.
  • ②对方；他。知己知～、～此。The other party. 知己知彼 know yourself and know your opponent; 彼此 each other.
