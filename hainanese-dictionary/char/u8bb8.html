字义解释

hhu³ 同音字 4

xǔ

  • ①应允；认可。允～、～可、特～。To permit. 允许 to allow; 许可 to authorise; 特许 to license.
  • ②答应给予。～愿、以身～国。To promise. 许愿 to make a vow; 以身许国 to dedicate one's life to one's country.
  • ③称赞；承认优点。称～、赞～、推～。To praise; to acknowledge merit. 称许 to commend; 推许 to speak highly of.
  • ④或者；可能。也～、或～、他～来了。Perhaps. 也许 maybe; 他许来了 he may have come.
  • ⑤表示大约的数量。少～、些～、年三十～。Marks an approximate quantity. 少许 a little; 年三十许 about thirty years of age.
  • ⑥处所。何～人也。A place. 何许人也 where does he come from?
  • ⑦姓。A surname.
