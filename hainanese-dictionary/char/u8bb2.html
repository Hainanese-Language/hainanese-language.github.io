字义解释

gong³ 同音字 3

jiǎng

  • ①说；谈。～话、演～、～故事。To speak. 讲话 to talk; 演讲 to give a lecture; 讲故事 to tell a story.
  • ②解释；说明。～解、～课、～清道理。To explain. 讲解 to expound; 讲课 to teach a class; 讲清道理 to make the reasoning clear.
  • ③商量；商议。～和、～价、～条件。To negotiate. 讲和 to make peace; 讲价 to haggle; 讲条件 to bargain.
  • ④讲求；注重。～卫生、～质量、～排场。To be particular about. 讲卫生 to pay attention to hygiene; 讲排场 to go in for ostentation.
  • ⑤就某方面说。～水平他不差、～年龄他最小。As far as a given respect goes. 讲年龄他最小 in point of age he is the youngest.
