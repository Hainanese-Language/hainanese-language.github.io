字义解释

hui¹ 同音字 7

kāi

  • ①使关闭的东西不再关闭。～门、～锁、～盖。To open something shut. 开门 to open a door; 开锁 to unlock.
  • ②打通；开辟。～路、～渠、～荒。To open up; to cut through. 开路 to open a road; 开荒 to reclaim wasteland.
  • ③开始；举行。～工、～学、～会。To begin; to hold. 开工 to start work; 开学 for term to begin; 开会 to hold a meeting.
  • ④发动；操纵。～车、～枪、～机器。To start; to operate. 开车 to drive; 开枪 to fire a gun; 开机器 to run a machine.
  • ⑤写出。～方子、～发票。To write out. 开方子 to write a prescription; 开发票 to make out a receipt.
  • ⑥分离；离开。离～、～小差、想不～。To part; to leave. 离开 to leave; 想不开 unable to get over something.
  • ⑦（花）绽放。～花、桃花～了。(Of flowers) to bloom. 开花 to blossom.
  • ⑧液体受热沸腾。水～了。(Of liquid) to boil. 水开了 the water is boiling.
  • ⑨解除。～禁、～戒。To lift a ban. 开禁 to remove a prohibition.
  • ⑩用在动词后，表示扩大、分散或容纳。传～、屋子小，坐不～。After a verb: marks spreading, dispersal or capacity. 传开 to spread; 坐不开 not enough room to seat everyone.
