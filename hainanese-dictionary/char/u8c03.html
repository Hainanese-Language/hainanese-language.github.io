字义解释

hiao² 同音字 7

tiáo

  • ①配合得均匀合适。～和、～味、风～雨顺。Well blended; suitably matched. 调和 harmonious; 调味 to season; 风调雨顺 seasonable weather.
  • ②使和谐。～解、～停、～剂。To bring into harmony. 调解 to mediate; 调剂 to adjust and balance.
  • ③挑逗；戏弄。～戏、～笑、～情。To tease. 调戏 to make advances to; 调笑 to banter; 调情 to flirt.
ddiao⁵

diào

  • ①分派；安排。～动、～职、～兵遣将。To assign; to transfer. 调动 to redeploy; 调兵遣将 to move troops and dispatch generals.
  • ②访查。～查、～研。To investigate. 调查 to survey; 调研 to research.
  • ③音乐上高低长短配合成的组织。曲～、～子、走～。A melody. 曲调 a tune; 调子 a melody; 走调 to sing out of tune.
  • ④语音上的声调。～号、～类、平～。Tone, in phonetics. 调号 a tone mark; 平调 the level tone.
  • ⑤论调；看法。陈词滥～、唱高～。A line of argument. 陈词滥调 stale platitudes; 唱高调 to mouth high-sounding words.
