字义解释

hheng² 同音字 8

xíng

  • ①样子。～状、～式、圆～。Shape; form. 形状 a shape; 形式 form; 圆形 circular.
  • ②形体；实体。～影不离、～同虚设。A physical body. 形影不离 inseparable as body and shadow; 形同虚设 existing in name only.
  • ③显露；表现。～诸文字、喜～于色。To show; give expression to. 形诸文字 to put into writing; 喜形于色 delight showing on one's face.
  • ④对照；比较。相～见绌。To set against; compare. 相形见绌 to pale by comparison.
