字义解释

ngiang³

yǎng

  • ①脸向上；跟"俯"相对。～望、～卧、～面朝天。To face upward; the opposite of 俯. 仰望 to look up; 仰卧 to lie on one's back; 仰面朝天 flat on one's back.
  • ②敬慕。敬～、景～、信～。To admire. 敬仰 to revere; 景仰 to look up to; 信仰 a faith.
  • ③依靠。～仗、～赖、～人鼻息。To depend on. 仰仗 to rely on; 仰赖 to be dependent on; 仰人鼻息 to live at someone's mercy.
