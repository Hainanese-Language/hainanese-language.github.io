字义解释

diang² 同音字 9

cháng

  • ①吃一点试试味道。～一口、品～、卧薪～胆。To taste. 尝一口 to take a taste; 品尝 to savour; 卧薪尝胆 to endure hardship for a purpose.
  • ②经历；体验。备～艰苦、～试。To experience; go through. 备尝艰苦 to have known every hardship; 尝试 to try.
  • ③〈书〉曾经。未～、何～。〈lit.〉 Once; ever. 未尝 never; 何尝 how could it ever be.
