字义解释

din⁴ 同音字 8

xìn

  • ①诚实；不欺骗。～用、～誉、守～、～守诺言。Honest; not deceiving. 信用 credit; 信誉 reputation; 守信 to keep one's word; 信守诺言 to honour a promise.
  • ②相信；认为可靠。～任、～赖、～以为真、将～将疑。To believe; to hold reliable. 信任 to trust; 信赖 to have confidence in; 信以为真 to take it as true; 将信将疑 half in doubt.
  • ③信仰。～徒、～教、～奉。To have faith in. 信徒 a believer; 信教 to practise a religion; 信奉 to profess.
  • ④书信。写～、～封、家～。A letter. 写信 to write a letter; 信封 an envelope; 家信 a letter home.
  • ⑤消息。～息、音～、报～、喜～。News. 信息 information; 音信 word from someone; 报信 to send word; 喜信 good news.
  • ⑥凭据。～物、印～。A token of proof. 信物 a token; 印信 an official seal.
  • ⑦随便；听凭。～步、～口开河、～手拈来。At will; as one pleases. 信步 to stroll; 信口开河 to talk off the top of one's head; 信手拈来 to have at one's fingertips.
