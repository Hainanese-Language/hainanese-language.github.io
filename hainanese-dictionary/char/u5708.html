字义解释

huan¹ 同音字 4

quān

  • ①环形；环形的东西。～子、花～、铁～。A ring; a circular thing. 圈子 a circle; 花圈 a wreath; 铁圈 an iron hoop.
  • ②围起来的范围。～内、～外、生活～。An enclosed sphere. 圈内 within the circle; 生活圈 one's circle of life.
  • ③画环形；围绕。～阅、～起来、～上记号。To circle; mark with a ring. 圈阅 to initial by circling one's name; 圈上记号 to ring a mark.
gin⁴ 同音字 9

juàn

  • 养家畜的棚栏。猪～、羊～、～肥。A pen for livestock. 猪圈 a pigsty; 羊圈 a sheepfold; 圈肥 farmyard manure.
gin¹ 同音字 15

juān

  • 关在圈里。把鸡～起来。To shut up in a pen. 把鸡圈起来 to coop the chickens up.
