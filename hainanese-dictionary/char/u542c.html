字义解释

hia¹ 同音字 2

tīng

用于第 ❶❷❸ 义

  • ①用耳朵接受声音。～广播、～见、倾～。To hear; listen. 听广播 to listen to the radio; 倾听 to listen attentively.
  • ②听从；接受。～话、～劝、言～计从。To obey; heed. 听话 obedient; 言听计从 to follow another's every word.
  • ③治理；判断。～政、～讼。To administer; adjudicate. 听政 to attend to affairs of state; 听讼 to try a case.
  • ④任凭；随便。～任、～便、～之任之。To let be; leave to. 听任 to allow; 听之任之 to let things drift.
  • ⑤〈外〉量词，用于罐装的食品、饮料。一～可乐。〈loan〉 Measure word for tinned food and drink. 一听可乐 a can of cola.
