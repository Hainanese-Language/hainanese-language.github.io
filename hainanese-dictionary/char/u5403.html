字义解释

zia⁶ 同音字 3

chī

  • ①把食物放进嘴里咀嚼咽下。～饭、～药。To eat. 吃饭 to have a meal; 吃药 to take medicine.
  • ②在某处进食。～食堂、～馆子。To take one's meals somewhere. 吃食堂 to eat in the canteen; 吃馆子 to eat out.
  • ③依靠某种事物生活。靠山～山、～老本。To live off something. 靠山吃山 to live off what is at hand; 吃老本 to rest on past gains.
  • ④消灭。～掉敌人一个团。To wipe out. 吃掉敌人一个团 to destroy an enemy regiment.
  • ⑤吸收（液体）。这纸不～墨。To absorb (liquid). 这纸不吃墨 this paper does not take ink.
  • ⑥承受；禁受。～苦、～亏、～不消。To bear; suffer. 吃苦 to endure hardship; 吃亏 to come off badly.
  • ⑦领会。～透文件精神。To grasp fully. 吃透文件精神 to master the substance of a document.
