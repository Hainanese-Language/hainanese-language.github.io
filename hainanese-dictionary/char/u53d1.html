字义解释

hhuad⁷ 同音字 2

fā

  • ①送出；交付；跟"收"相对。～信、～货、分～、～工资。Hair. 头发 hair; 发型 a hairstyle; 理发 to have a haircut; 白发苍苍 hoary-headed.
  • ②放射；射出。～射、～炮、百～百中。To emit; to fire. 发射 to launch; 发炮 to open fire; 百发百中 to hit the mark every time.
  • ③产生；发生。～芽、～电、～病、～水。To come into being; to occur. 发芽 to sprout; 发电 to generate power; 发病 to fall ill; 发水 to flood.
  • ④表达；说出。～言、～问、～誓、～表。To express; to utter. 发言 to speak; 发问 to ask; 发誓 to swear; 发表 to publish.
  • ⑤扩大；开展。～展、～扬、～挥。To expand; to develop. 发展 to develop; 发扬 to carry forward; 发挥 to bring into play.
  • ⑥因得财而兴旺。～财、～家。To prosper through gain. 发财 to make a fortune; 发家 to build up a family fortune.
  • ⑦食物因发酵或浸泡而膨胀。面～了、～海参。Of food, to swell through fermenting or soaking. 面发了 the dough has risen; 发海参 to soak sea cucumbers.
  • ⑧显现；流露。～黄、脸色～白、～愁。To show; to take on a look. 发黄 to turn yellow; 脸色发白 to go pale; 发愁 to look worried.
  • ⑨感到（多指不适）。～麻、～痒、～困。To feel (usually something unpleasant). 发麻 to go numb; 发痒 to itch; 发困 to feel sleepy.
  • ⑩量词，用于枪弹炮弹。三～子弹。Measure word for rounds of ammunition. 三发子弹 three rounds. fā
