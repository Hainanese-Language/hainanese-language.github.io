字义解释

nai⁴

tài

  • ①过分；过于。～长了、～贵。Excessively; too. 太贵 too dear.
  • ②表示程度很高，多用于赞叹或否定。～好了、不～清楚。Very — chiefly in praise or negation. 太好了 wonderful; 不太清楚 not very clear.
  • ③极；最。～古、～空。Utmost; highest. 太古 remote antiquity; 太空 outer space.
  • ④用于称谓，表示辈分更高。～爷、～婆。In titles, marking a senior generation. 太爷 great-grandfather; 太婆 great-grandmother.
