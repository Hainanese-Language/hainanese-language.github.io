字义解释

pan² 同音字 9

fán

用于第 ❶❷❸❹ 义

  • ①平常的；不出奇的。平～、～人、自命不～。Ordinary; nothing out of the way. 平凡 commonplace; 凡人 an ordinary person; 自命不凡 to think oneself no ordinary being.
  • ②人世间；跟"仙"相对。～间、～尘、下～。The mortal world; the opposite of 仙. 凡间 the human world; 凡尘 this world of dust; 下凡 to descend to the mortal world.
  • ③所有的；凡是。～事、～来过的人都知道。Every; whoever. 凡事 in all matters; 凡来过的人都知道 everyone who has been here knows.
  • ④总共；大概。全书～十卷。In all; roughly. 全书凡十卷 the work runs to ten volumes in all.
  • ⑤我国民族音阶上的一级，乐谱中用作记音符号，相当于简谱的'4'。A degree of the traditional Chinese scale, used as a notation sign.
