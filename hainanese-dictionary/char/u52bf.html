字义解释

di⁴ 同音字 6

shì

  • ①权力；威力。权～、～力、仗～欺人。Power; might. 权势 power and influence; 势力 influence; 仗势欺人 to bully others on the strength of one's backing.
  • ②事物表现出来的情况或趋向。局～、形～、来～、大～所趋。The state or trend of things. 局势 the situation; 形势 circumstances; 来势 the force of an oncoming thing; 大势所趋 the general trend.
  • ③自然界的样子。地～、山～、水～。The lie of natural features. 地势 the terrain; 山势 the contour of a mountain; 水势 the flow of water.
  • ④姿态。姿～、手～、装腔作～。A posture. 姿势 a stance; 手势 a gesture; 装腔作势 to strike a pose.
  • ⑤雄性生殖器。去～。The male genitals. 去势 to castrate.
