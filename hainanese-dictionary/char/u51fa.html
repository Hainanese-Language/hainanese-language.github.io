字义解释

sud⁷ 同音字 7

chū

  • ①从里面到外面，跟"进""入"相对。～门、～国、～来。To go out — the opposite of 进 and 入. 出门 to go out; 出国 to go abroad.
  • ②来到。～席、～场、～勤。To attend; to turn up. 出席 to attend; 出勤 to report for work.
  • ③超过。～众、～格、不～三天。To exceed. 出众 outstanding; 不出三天 within three days.
  • ④往外拿。～钱、～力、～主意。To put out. 出钱 to put up money; 出主意 to offer an idea.
  • ⑤长出；发生。～芽、～汗、～问题。To come forth; to arise. 出芽 to sprout; 出问题 for trouble to arise.
  • ⑥发出；产生。～声、～名、～成绩。To produce. 出名 to become famous; 出成绩 to achieve results.
  • ⑦显露。～现、～头露面。To appear. 出现 to appear; 出头露面 to show oneself in public.
  • ⑧量词，用于戏曲。一～戏。Measure word for operas. 一出戏 an opera.
