字义解释

vo² 同音字 2

wú

  • ①没有，跟"有"相对。～效、～穷、～中生有。To have not; without — the opposite of 有. 无效 invalid; 无中生有 to make something out of nothing.
  • ②不；不要。～须、～妨、～庸讳言。Not; need not. 无须 no need; 无妨 there is no harm in it.
  • ③不论。事～大小、～分彼此。No matter. 事无大小 whether the matter is great or small.
