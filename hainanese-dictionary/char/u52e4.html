字义解释

hin² 同音字 14

qín

  • ①尽力多做；不偷懒；跟"懒"相对。～奋、～劳、～学苦练。Diligent; the opposite of 懒. 勤奋 industrious; 勤劳 hardworking; 勤学苦练 to study hard and train diligently.
  • ②经常；次数多。～洗手、～来～往。Often; frequently. 勤洗手 to wash one's hands often; 勤来勤往 to visit each other often.
  • ③在规定时间内的工作或到班。出～、考～、外～、～务。Attendance or duty within set hours. 出勤 to turn up for work; 考勤 to record attendance; 外勤 fieldwork; 勤务 duty service.
