字义解释

duad⁸

jué

  • ①断；断绝。～交、～缘、络绎不～。To cut off; sever. 绝交 to break off relations; 绝缘 insulation; 络绎不绝 in an endless stream.
  • ②完全没有了；穷尽。～望、～迹、法子想～了。To be exhausted; come to nothing. 绝望 despair; 绝迹 to vanish; 法子想绝了 out of ideas.
  • ③独一无二的；超出一般的。～技、～色、～唱。Unrivalled; exceptional. 绝技 a consummate skill; 绝色 peerless beauty; 绝唱 a masterpiece.
  • ④极；最。～大多数、～密。Utterly; most. 绝大多数 the vast majority; 绝密 top secret.
  • ⑤绝对；完全（多用于否定）。～无此意、～不后退。Absolutely — chiefly in the negative. 绝无此意 nothing of the sort was meant; 绝不后退 never to give ground.
  • ⑥旧体诗的一种体裁。五～、七～。A form of classical verse, the quatrain. 五绝 five-character quatrain; 七绝 seven-character quatrain.
