字义解释

hhiu¹ 同音字 8

yōu

  • ①美好的；出众的；跟"劣"相对。～良、～秀、～点、～势。Fine; outstanding; the opposite of 劣. 优良 excellent; 优秀 outstanding; 优点 a merit; 优势 an advantage.
  • ②充足；宽裕。～裕、～厚、养尊处～。Ample; well provided for. 优裕 affluent; 优厚 generous; 养尊处优 to live in ease and comfort.
  • ③优待。～惠、～先、拥军～属。To give preferential treatment. 优惠 preferential; 优先 to have priority; 拥军优属 to support the army and give preference to servicemen's families.
  • ④古代指演戏的人。～伶、俳～。An actor in former times. 优伶 a player; 俳优 a court entertainer.
