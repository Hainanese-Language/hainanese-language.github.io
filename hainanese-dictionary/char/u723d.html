shuǎng

  • ①明朗；清亮。～朗、清～、秋高气～。Bright and clear. 爽朗 hearty; bracing; 清爽 fresh; 秋高气爽 crisp autumn weather.
  • ②舒服；痛快。～快、～心、身体不～。Comfortable; refreshed. 爽快 pleasant; 身体不爽 feeling unwell.
  • ③直率；干脆。豪～、直～。Frank; straightforward. 豪爽 open-hearted; 直爽 candid.
  • ④违背；差失。～约、屡试不～。To fail; deviate. 爽约 to break an appointment; 屡试不爽 never once failing.
