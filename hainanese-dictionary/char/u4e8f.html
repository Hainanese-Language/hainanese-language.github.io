字义解释

hui¹ 同音字 7

kuī

  • ①缺损；不足。～损、～空、理～。Deficient; short. 亏损 a loss; 亏空 in debt; 理亏 to be in the wrong.
  • ②损失。吃～、～本。A loss. 吃亏 to come off badly; 亏本 to lose money.
  • ③亏负；对不起。～心、～待。To do someone wrong. 亏心 to have a guilty conscience; 亏待 to treat unfairly.
  • ④多亏；幸而。～他提醒、～了大家帮忙。Thanks to; luckily. 亏他提醒 thanks to his reminder; 亏了大家帮忙 luckily everyone helped.
  • ⑤反说，表示讥讽。～你还是老手、这种话～你说得出口。Said ironically, in reproach. 亏你还是老手 and you call yourself an old hand; 这种话亏你说得出口 how could you bring yourself to say such a thing.
