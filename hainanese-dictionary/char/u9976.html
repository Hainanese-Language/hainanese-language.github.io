字义解释

yiao² 同音字 14

ráo

  • ①丰富；多。富～、～有兴趣。Abundant; plentiful. 富饶 richly endowed; 饶有兴趣 of great interest.
  • ②额外增添。～上一个、买十～一。To give in addition. 饶上一个 to throw one in; 买十饶一 buy ten and get one free.
  • ③宽恕；免除处罚。～恕、求～、～了他。To pardon; let off. 饶恕 to forgive; 求饶 to beg for mercy.
  • ④姓。A surname.
