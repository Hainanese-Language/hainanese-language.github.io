iu¹ 同音字 5

yōu

  • ①深远；僻静。～深、～静、～谷。Deep and secluded. 幽深 remote and still; 幽静 quiet; 幽谷 a deep valley.
  • ②隐蔽的；不公开的。～会、～居。Hidden; private. 幽会 a secret tryst; 幽居 to live in seclusion.
  • ③沉静；文雅。～雅、～默。Serene; refined. 幽雅 quietly elegant; 幽默 humour.
  • ④囚禁。～禁、～闭。To confine. 幽禁 to keep under house arrest; 幽闭 to shut away.
  • ⑤指阴间。～冥、～灵。The nether world. 幽冥 the shades; 幽灵 a ghost.
