字义解释

ze¹ 同音字 4

zhēng

  • ①力求得到或达到。～取、～夺、～先恐后。To strive for. 争取 to fight for; 争夺 to contend for; 争先恐后 to vie with one another to be first.
  • ②争执；争论。～吵、～辩、～端、意气之～。To dispute. 争吵 to quarrel; 争辩 to argue; 争端 a dispute; 意气之争 a clash of tempers.
  • ③〈方〉差；欠。～不多。〈dial.〉 To fall short by. 争不多 not far off.
