字义解释

hhiu¹ 同音字 8

xiū

  • ①停止。～息、～止、～会、争论不～。To stop. 休息 to rest; 休止 to cease; 休会 to adjourn; 争论不休 to argue endlessly.
  • ②歇息。～假、～养、公～日。To take a rest. 休假 to go on leave; 休养 to recuperate; 公休日 a public holiday.
  • ③旧时丈夫赶走妻子，断绝夫妻关系。～妻、～书。Formerly, of a husband, to cast off his wife. 休妻 to divorce one's wife; 休书 a bill of divorcement.
  • ④书别；不要。～想、～提当年。lit. Do not. 休想 don't imagine it; 休提当年 say no more of those days.
