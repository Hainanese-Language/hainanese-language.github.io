字义解释

geng¹ 同音字 12

gēng

  • ①改变；改换。～改、～换、～正。To change; to replace. 更改 to alter; 更正 to correct.
  • ②经历。少不～事。To go through. 少不更事 young and inexperienced.
  • ③旧时夜间的计时单位，一夜分五更。三～半夜、打～。Formerly, a night watch; the night was divided into five. 三更半夜 in the dead of night; 打更 to sound the night watches.

geng⁴ 同音字 5

gèng

  • ①更加。～好、～深一层。Still more. 更好 better; 更深一层 a step deeper.
  • ②书再；又。～进一步。lit. Again; further. 更进一步 to go a step further.
