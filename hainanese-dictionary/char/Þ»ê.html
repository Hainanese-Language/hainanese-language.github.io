字义解释

da⁶ 同音字 18

zhà

  • ①欺骗。欺～、～骗、兵不厌～。To cheat. 欺诈 to defraud; 兵不厌诈 all is fair in war.
  • ②假装。～降、～死。To feign. 诈降 to pretend to surrender; 诈死 to feign death.
  • ③用假话试探。他是拿话～我。To draw someone out with false words. 他是拿话诈我 he was trying to trick it out of me.
