字义解释

ngou³ 同音字 3

ǒu

  • ①用木头、泥土等做成的人像。木～、～像。A human figure made of wood or clay. 木偶 a puppet; 偶像 an idol.
  • ②双数的；成对的；跟"奇"相对。～数、～蹄、对～。Even-numbered; paired; the opposite of 奇. 偶数 an even number; 偶蹄 cloven-hoofed; 对偶 antithesis.
  • ③配偶。佳～、丧～。A spouse. 佳偶 a happy couple; 丧偶 to lose one's spouse.
  • ④偶然；不经常。～然、～尔、～遇。By chance; now and then. 偶然 accidental; 偶尔 occasionally; 偶遇 to meet by chance.
