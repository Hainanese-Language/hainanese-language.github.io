字义解释

lu² 同音字 13

ér

  • ①小孩子。～童、婴～、～科。A child. 儿童 children; 婴儿 an infant; 儿科 paediatrics.
  • ②儿子。～女、～媳、生～育女。A son. 儿女 sons and daughters; 儿媳 a daughter-in-law; 生儿育女 to bring up children.
  • ③年轻的男子。男～、健～。A young man. 男儿 a man; 健儿 a stout fellow.
  • ④后缀，读作卷舌音。花～、玩～、慢慢～。A suffix pronounced with a retroflex ending. 花儿 a flower; 玩儿 to play; 慢慢儿 slowly.
