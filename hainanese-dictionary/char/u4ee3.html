字义解释

ddai⁶ 同音字 8

dài

用于第 ❶ 义

  • ①替。～替、～办、～课、～人受过。To take the place of. 代替 to replace; 代办 to act on another's behalf; 代课 to teach in someone's stead; 代人受过 to take the blame for another.
  • ②历史的分期；朝代。古～、现～、汉～、年～。A period of history; a dynasty. 古代 antiquity; 现代 modern times; 汉代 the Han dynasty; 年代 a decade.
  • ③家族世系的辈分。第二～、后～、传～。A generation in a family line. 第二代 the second generation; 后代 descendants; 传代 to pass on to later generations.
