字义解释

seng² 同音字 14

chéng

  • ①骑；坐。～车、～船、～飞机。To ride. 乘车 to go by car; 乘船 to travel by boat.
  • ②趁着；利用。～机、～势、～虚而入。To take advantage of. 乘机 to seize the chance; 乘虚而入 to slip in through a gap.
  • ③数学运算之一。～法、～号、二～三得六。Multiplication. 乘法 multiplication; 二乘三得六 two times three is six.
  • ④佛教的教派。大～、小～。A vehicle of Buddhist doctrine. 大乘 Mahayana; 小乘 Hinayana.
seng⁴ 同音字 2

shèng

  • ①古代称四匹马拉的一辆车。千～之国。In antiquity, a four-horse chariot. 千乘之国 a state of a thousand chariots.
