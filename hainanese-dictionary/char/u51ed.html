peng² 同音字 14

píng

  • ①依靠；倚着。～栏、～借、～险固守。To lean on; to rely on. 凭栏 to lean on a railing; 凭借 by means of; 凭险固守 to hold out behind natural defences.
  • ②根据；依据。～票入场、真～实据、～证。On the strength of; according to. 凭票入场 admission by ticket; 真凭实据 hard evidence; 凭证 proof.
  • ③连词，任凭；无论。～你怎么说。No matter; whatever. 凭你怎么说 no matter what you say.
