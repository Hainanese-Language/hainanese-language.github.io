字义解释

suan² 同音字 9

quán

  • ①完整；齐备。～部、～套、东西不～。Complete; with nothing missing. 全部 the whole; 全套 a full set; 东西不全 the things are not all there.
  • ②整个。～国、～身、～家、～世界。Whole; entire. 全国 the whole country; 全身 the whole body; 全家 the whole family; 全世界 the whole world.
  • ③都；完全。～不知道、～班～到。All; entirely. 全不知道 to know nothing at all; 全班全到 the whole class is present.
  • ④使完整不受损伤。保～、成～、两～其美。To keep intact. 保全 to preserve; 成全 to help bring about; 两全其美 to satisfy both sides.
