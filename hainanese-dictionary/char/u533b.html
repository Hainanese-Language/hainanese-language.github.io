字义解释

i¹ 同音字 15

yī

  • ①医生。军～、～护人员。A doctor. 军医 an army surgeon; 医护人员 medical staff.
  • ②医学。中～、西～、学～。Medicine, the science. 中医 Chinese medicine; 学医 to study medicine.
  • ③治病。～治、～疗、把他的病～好了。To treat; to cure. 医治 to treat; 医疗 medical treatment.
