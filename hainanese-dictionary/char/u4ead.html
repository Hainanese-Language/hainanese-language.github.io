ddeng²

tíng

  • ①有顶无墙、供休息用的建筑。凉～、～子、～台楼阁。A pavilion, roofed but without walls. 凉亭 a wayside pavilion; 亭台楼阁 pavilions and towers.
  • ②像亭子的小房子。邮～、书报～、岗～。A booth. 邮亭 a postal kiosk; 书报亭 a newsstand; 岗亭 a sentry box.
  • ③〈书〉适中；均匀。～午。〈lit.〉 Even; balanced. 亭午 high noon.
