字义解释

dog⁸ 同音字 5

shǔ

  • ①类别。金～、吾～。A category. 金属 metals; 吾属 we; our sort.
  • ②生物分类的一个等级。豹～。A genus in biological classification. 豹属 the genus Panthera.
  • ③归属；隶属。～于、直～、附～。To belong to; come under. 属于 to pertain to; 直属 directly subordinate.
  • ④家属；亲属。军～、眷～。Family members. 军属 a serviceman's family; 眷属 dependants.
  • ⑤是。查明～实、纯～虚构。To be. 查明属实 verified as true; 纯属虚构 pure fabrication.
ziag⁷ 同音字 10

zhǔ

  • ①连缀；连接。～文、前后相～。To join; link. 属文 to compose writing; 前后相属 joined end to end.
  • ②集中在一点。～意、～目。To fix on one point. 属意 to have one's heart set on; 属目 to fix one's eyes on.
