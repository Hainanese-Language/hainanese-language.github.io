字义解释

ddid⁷

dé

  • 〔dé〕
  • ①得到；获得。取～、～奖、～病。To get; obtain. 取得 to acquire; 得奖 to win a prize; 得病 to fall ill.
  • ②演算所得的结果。二三～六。The result of a calculation. 二三得六 two threes are six.
  • ③适合。～体、～当、～法。Fitting; apt. 得体 in good taste; 得当 appropriate; 得法 done the right way.
  • ④满意。～意、洋洋自～。Pleased with oneself. 得意 complacent; 洋洋自得 thoroughly self-satisfied.
  • ⑤完成。饭～了。Done; ready. 饭得了 the meal is ready.
  • ⑥表示禁止或同意。～了，别说了。Marking prohibition or assent. 得了，别说了 all right, that's enough.
  • 〔děi〕
  • ⑦需要。这活儿～三天。To need. 这活儿得三天 this job will take three days.
  • ⑧必须；一定要。你～去一趟。Must; have to. 你得去一趟 you'll have to make a trip.
  • 〔de〕
  • ⑨助词，用在动词后表示可能。拿～动、去～了。Particle after a verb marking possibility. 拿得动 able to lift it; 去得了 able to go.
  • ⑩助词，用在动词、形容词后连接补语。跑～快、好～很。Particle linking a verb or adjective to its complement. 跑得快 runs fast; 好得很 very good indeed.
