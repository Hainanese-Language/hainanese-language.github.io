字义解释

gong⁵ 同音字 2

gōng

  • 〔gōng〕
  • ①把所需的东西给需要的人使用。～应、～养、～电、提～。To supply what is needed. 供应 to supply; 供养 to provide for; 供电 to supply power; 提供 to furnish.
  • ②提供条件让人使用。～参考、仅～研究。To make available for use. 供参考 for reference; 仅供研究 for study purposes only.
  • 〔gòng〕
  • ③摆出祭品；祭品。～奉、～佛、～果。To set out offerings; an offering. 供奉 to make offerings; 供佛 to make offerings to the Buddha; 供果 fruit offered at a shrine.
  • ④受审者陈述案情；所陈述的话。招～、口～、～认、翻～。Of an accused person, to give an account; such a statement. 招供 to confess; 口供 a confession; 供认 to admit; 翻供 to retract a confession.
  • ⑤担任；从事。～职。To hold a post. 供职 to serve in office.
