字义解释

hib⁸

jí

  • ①到；达到。～格、由此～彼、～早。To reach. 及格 to pass a test; 由此及彼 from one to the other.
  • ②比得上。我不～他、望尘莫～。To come up to. 我不及他 I am not his equal; 望尘莫及 far behind and unable to catch up.
  • ③趁着。～时、～早动手。While there is time. 及时 timely; 及早 at the earliest opportunity.
  • ④和；跟。图书、仪器～其他设备。And. 图书、仪器及其他设备 books, instruments and other equipment.
