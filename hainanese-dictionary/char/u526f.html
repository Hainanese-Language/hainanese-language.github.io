字义解释

pu⁵ 同音字 6

fù

  • ①居第二位的；辅助的。～手、～班长、～作用。Second in rank; assisting. 副手 an assistant; 副班长 a deputy squad leader; 副作用 a side effect.
  • ②附带的；次要的。～食、～业、～产品。Incidental; secondary. 副食 non-staple food; 副业 a sideline; 副产品 a by-product.
  • ③相称；符合。名～其实、名不～实。To match; to correspond to. 名副其实 to live up to one's name; 名不副实 to be undeserving of one's reputation.
  • ④量词，用于成套的东西或面部表情。一～手套、一～笑脸、全～武装。Measure word for sets of things and facial expressions. 一副手套 a pair of gloves; 一副笑脸 a smiling face; 全副武装 fully armed.
