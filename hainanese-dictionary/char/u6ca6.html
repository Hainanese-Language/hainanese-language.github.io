字义解释

lun² 同音字 8

lún

  • ①沉没；陷入。～陷、沉～、～为附庸。To sink; to fall into. 沦陷 to fall to an enemy; 沉沦 to sink into ruin; 沦为附庸 to be reduced to a dependency.
  • ②没落；衰败。～落、～亡。To decline; to come to nothing. 沦落 to fall on hard times; 沦亡 to be subjugated.
