字义解释

gio⁴

jiào

  • ①人或动物发出较大的声音。大～、鸡～、～喊。To cry out; (of animals) to call. 大叫 to shout; 鸡叫 the cock crows.
  • ②呼唤；招呼。～人、～门、～住他。To call; summon. 叫人 to call someone; 叫门 to knock and be let in.
  • ③名称是。他～王明。To be named. 他叫王明 he is called Wang Ming.
  • ④使；命令。～他进来、～人担心。To tell; make. 叫他进来 tell him to come in; 叫人担心 worrying.
  • ⑤雇；订。～车、～菜。To hire; order. 叫车 to call a cab; 叫菜 to order dishes.
  • ⑥表示被动。衣服～雨淋湿了。Marking the passive. 衣服叫雨淋湿了 the clothes got soaked by the rain.
