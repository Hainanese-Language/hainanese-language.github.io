字义解释

dun³ 同音字 6

zhǔn

  • ①允许；许可。批～、～许、不～迟到。To permit. 批准 to approve; 准许 to allow; 不准迟到 no lateness allowed.
  • ②依照。～此办理。In accordance with. 准此办理 to act accordingly.
  • ③标准；法则。～则、～绳、以此为～。A standard; a norm. 准则 a criterion; 以此为准 to take this as the standard.
  • ④正确。～确、瞄～、这表走得～。Accurate. 准确 exact; 瞄准 to take aim; 这表走得准 this watch keeps good time.
  • ⑤一定。～能完成、他～来。Certainly. 准能完成 sure to get it done; 他准来 he is bound to come.
  • ⑥程度上接近。～平原、～将。Approaching a given category; quasi-. 准平原 a peneplain; 准将 a brigadier.
