字义解释

yid⁸ 同音字 3

rè

  • ①温度高；跟"冷"相对。～水、天气～、～带。Hot; the opposite of 冷. 热水 hot water; 热带 the tropics.
  • ②使温度升高。～饭、把菜～一下。To heat up. 热饭 to warm up the rice; 把菜热一下 give the dish a warm-up.
  • ③生病引起的高体温。发～、退～。A fever. 发热 to run a temperature; 退热 to bring a fever down.
  • ④情意深厚。～情、～爱、亲～。Warm in feeling. 热情 enthusiasm; 热爱 to love ardently; 亲热 affectionate.
  • ⑤受很多人欢迎的。～门、～销。Popular; sought after. 热门 in demand; 热销 selling well.
  • ⑥急切；渴望。～衷、眼～。Eager; craving. 热衷 to be bent on; 眼热 to covet.
