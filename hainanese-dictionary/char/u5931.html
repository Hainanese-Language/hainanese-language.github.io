字义解释

did⁷ 同音字 13

shī

  • ①丢掉；丧失，跟"得"相对。遗～、～而复得、～业。To lose — the opposite of 得. 遗失 to mislay; 失而复得 lost and found again; 失业 to lose one's job.
  • ②没有掌握住。～手、～足、～控。To lose control of. 失手 to slip; 失足 to lose one's footing.
  • ③违背；没有做到。～信、～约、～职。To break faith with. 失信 to go back on one's word; 失职 to neglect one's duty.
  • ④错误；过错。过～、～误、千虑一～。A fault; an error. 过失 a mistake; 千虑一失 even the wise err at times.
  • ⑤找不着。迷～、～踪。To be missing. 迷失 to lose one's way; 失踪 to disappear.
  • ⑥改变常态。～色、～声痛哭、大惊～色。To lose one's composure. 失色 to turn pale; 大惊失色 to blanch with terror.
