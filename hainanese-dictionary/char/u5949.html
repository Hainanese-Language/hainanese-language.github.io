字义解释

pong⁵ 同音字 4

fèng

  • ①恭敬地捧着或接受。～命、～上、～还。To hold or receive with respect. 奉命 to act on orders; 奉还 to return with thanks.
  • ②尊重；遵守。～行、信～、～公守法。To honour; to abide by. 奉行 to carry out; 奉公守法 to be law-abiding.
  • ③侍候。侍～、～养。To wait on. 侍奉 to attend upon; 奉养 to support one's elders.
  • ④敬辞，用于自己的行为涉及对方。～告、～陪、～劝。Polite prefix for one's own act towards another. 奉告 to inform; 奉劝 to advise.
