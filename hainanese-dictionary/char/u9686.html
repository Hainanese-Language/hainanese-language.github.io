字义解释

long² 同音字 8

lóng

  • ①盛大；兴盛。～重、兴～、～盛。Grand; flourishing. 隆重 ceremonious; 兴隆 thriving; 隆盛 prosperous.
  • ②深厚；程度深。～情厚谊、～冬。Deep; profound. 隆情厚谊 deep affection and friendship; 隆冬 the depth of winter.
  • ③高起；凸出。～起、～鼻。To rise; to bulge. 隆起 to swell up; 隆鼻 a high-bridged nose.
