字义解释

dang⁴ 同音字 6

sāng

  • 〔sāng〕
  • ①跟死了人有关的事。～事、治～、～礼。Funeral matters. 丧事 a funeral; 治丧 to make funeral arrangements; 丧礼 funeral rites.
  • 〔sàng〕
  • ②丢掉；失去。～失、～命、～权辱国。To lose. 丧失 to forfeit; 丧命 to lose one's life; 丧权辱国 to surrender sovereignty and disgrace the country.
  • ③情绪低落。沮～、垂头～气、灰心～气。Downcast. 沮丧 dejected; 垂头丧气 crestfallen.
