字义解释

hheng¹

xīng

  • ①兴盛；旺盛。～旺、复～、～衰、生意～隆。Thriving; flourishing. 兴旺 prosperous; 复兴 to revive; 兴衰 rise and fall; 生意兴隆 business is booming.
  • ②使盛行；创办。～办、～建、～修水利。To promote; to set up. 兴办 to establish; 兴建 to build; 兴修水利 to undertake water conservancy works.
  • ③流行。现在～这种式样。To be in fashion. 现在兴这种式样 this style is in fashion now.
  • ④方准许。不～胡说。dial. To permit. 不兴胡说 you're not to talk nonsense.
hheng⁴

xìng

  • 兴趣；高兴的情绪。～趣、高～、～致、扫～、诗～大发。Interest; high spirits. 兴趣 interest; 高兴 glad; 兴致 zest; 扫兴 to have one's spirits dampened; 诗兴大发 to be seized by poetic inspiration.
