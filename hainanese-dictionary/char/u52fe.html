字义解释

gao¹ 同音字 9

gōu

  • ①用笔画出符号，表示删除或选取。～销、～去几个字。To mark with a stroke of the pen, to delete or to select. 勾销 to write off; 勾去几个字 to strike out a few characters.
  • ②描画；用灰泥填抹缝隙。～勒、～边、～缝。To outline; to point up joints with mortar. 勾勒 to sketch the outline; 勾边 to trace an edge; 勾缝 to point brickwork.
  • ③招引；引起。～引、～结、～起回忆。To draw out; to call forth. 勾引 to entice; 勾结 to collude; 勾起回忆 to stir memories.
  • ④用带钩的东西挂取。用竿子～下来。To hook something down or across. 用竿子勾下来 to hook it down with a pole.
gou⁴ 同音字 8

gòu

  • 用于"勾当"，指事情，多指坏事。丑恶～当。Used in 勾当: an affair, usually a shady one. 丑恶勾当 a foul business.
