字义解释

hang⁵

dòng

  • ①改变位置或状态；跟"静"相对。～作、走～、别～。To move; the opposite of 静. 动作 a movement; 走动 to stir about; 别动 don't move.
  • ②使起作用；使用。～脑筋、～手、～武。To bring into play; to use. 动脑筋 to use one's head; 动手 to set to work; 动武 to resort to force.
  • ③感情起变化。～心、～情、～怒。Of feelings, to be stirred. 动心 to be moved; 动情 to be roused; 动怒 to lose one's temper.
  • ④触动；使感动。～人、生～。To touch; to move someone. 动人 moving; 生动 vivid.
  • ⑤动不动；常常。～辄得咎。At every turn; often. 动辄得咎 to be blamed at every turn.
