字义解释

guan³ 同音字 3

guǎn

  • ①招待宾客居住的房屋。宾～、旅～。A place where guests are lodged. 宾馆 a guesthouse; 旅馆 a hotel.
  • ②使馆。大使～、领事～。A diplomatic mission. 大使馆 an embassy; 领事馆 a consulate.
  • ③陈列文物或供人活动的场所。博物～、图书～、体育～。A public building for exhibits or activities. 博物馆 a museum; 图书馆 a library.
  • ④某些服务性商店。饭～、理发～、照相～。Certain service establishments. 饭馆 a restaurant; 理发馆 a barber's shop.
