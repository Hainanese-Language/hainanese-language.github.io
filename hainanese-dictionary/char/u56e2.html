字义解释

huan²

tuán

  • ①圆形的。～扇、～脐。Round. 团扇 a round fan; 团脐 the round belly-flap of a female crab.
  • ②揉成球形的东西。饭～、纸～、面～。Something rolled into a ball. 饭团 a rice ball; 纸团 a crumpled ball of paper.
  • ③会合在一起。～聚、～结、～圆。To gather together. 团聚 to reunite; 团结 to unite; 团圆 a family reunion.
  • ④工作或活动的集体。剧～、代表～、社～。An organised body. 剧团 a theatre company; 代表团 a delegation.
  • ⑤军队的一级编制。～长、独立～。A regiment. 团长 a regimental commander.
  • ⑥量词，用于成团的东西。一～毛线、一～和气。Measure word for lumps or balls. 一团毛线 a ball of wool; 一团和气 all smiles and no substance.
