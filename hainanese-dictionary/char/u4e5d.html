字义解释

gao³ 同音字 6

jiǔ

  • ①数目，八加一所得。The number nine; eight plus one.
  • ②表示多次或多数。～死一生、～霄云外。Marks many times or a great number. 九死一生 a narrow escape; 九霄云外 far beyond the clouds.
  • ③从冬至起每九天为一个"九"，共九个九。数～、三～天。From the winter solstice, each period of nine days, nine such periods in all. 数九 the depth of winter; 三九天 the coldest days.
