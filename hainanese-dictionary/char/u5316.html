字义解释

hhue⁴ 同音字 3

huà

  • ①变化；使改变。～装、～险为夷、感～、潜移默～。To change; to transform. 化装 to disguise oneself; 化险为夷 to turn danger into safety; 感化 to reform someone; 潜移默化 to influence imperceptibly.
  • ②融解；消散。～冻、雪～了、～痰。To melt; to dissolve away. 化冻 to thaw; 雪化了 the snow has melted; 化痰 to reduce phlegm.
  • ③加在名词、形容词后，表示转变成某种性质或状态。现代～、绿～、机械～。Added to a noun or adjective to mean turning into that state. 现代化 modernization; 绿化 to make green; 机械化 mechanization.
  • ④指化学。～工、～肥、理～。Short for chemistry. 化工 the chemical industry; 化肥 chemical fertilizer; 理化 physics and chemistry.
  • ⑤僧道向人乞求施舍。～缘、～斋。Of monks and priests, to beg for alms. 化缘 to collect alms; 化斋 to beg for food.
  • ⑥焚烧。～纸、火～。To burn. 化纸 to burn paper offerings; 火化 to cremate.
