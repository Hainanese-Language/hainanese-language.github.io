字义解释

pin¹ 同音字 5

piān

  • ①不正；歪向一边；跟"正"相对。～斜、～旁、太阳～西。Slanting; leaning to one side; the opposite of 正. 偏斜 aslant; 偏旁 a character component at the side; 太阳偏西 the sun is past its height.
  • ②不公正。～心、～袒、～爱、不～不倚。Partial; unfair. 偏心 biased; 偏袒 to take sides; 偏爱 to favour; 不偏不倚 impartial.
  • ③副词，表示故意跟要求或情理相反。～不听、你越急他～慢。Deliberately contrary to what is asked or expected. 偏不听 to refuse to listen out of contrariness; 你越急他偏慢 the more you hurry him the slower he goes.
