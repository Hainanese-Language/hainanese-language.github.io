gao³ 同音字 6

jiǔ

用于第 ❶ 义

  • ①"九"的大写，多用于票据、账目等。人民币～元。The formal financial form of 九 "nine," used mainly in cheques, accounts, receipts, and other financial documents. 人民币玖元 nine yuan.
  • ②像玉的一种黑色美石，古书中也用作人名用字。An old term for a dark, jade-like ornamental stone; also used in personal names.
