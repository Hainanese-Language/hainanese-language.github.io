字义解释

zin³ 同音字 11

zhǎn

  • ①张开；放开。～开、舒～、～翅。To spread out; open. 展开 to unfold; 舒展 to stretch; 展翅 to spread the wings.
  • ②施展。一筹莫～、大～宏图。To give play to. 一筹莫展 at one's wits' end; 大展宏图 to realise a grand design.
  • ③陈列出来供人观看。～览、～销、画～。To put on show. 展览 an exhibition; 展销 a sales exhibition; 画展 an art show.
  • ④放宽（时间）。～期、～限。To extend (a deadline). 展期 to postpone; 展限 to extend a time limit.
