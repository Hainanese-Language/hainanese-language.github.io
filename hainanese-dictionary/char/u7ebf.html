字义解释

dua⁴ 同音字 5

xiàn

  • ①用棉、麻、丝、金属等制成的细长物。棉～、电～、～团。A thread; a wire. 棉线 cotton thread; 电线 an electric wire; 线团 a ball of thread.
  • ②一个点移动所成的图形。直～、曲～。A line traced by a moving point. 直线 a straight line; 曲线 a curve.
  • ③像线的东西。光～、～路。Something line-like. 光线 a ray of light; 线路 a circuit.
  • ④交通路线。航～、干～、路～。A route. 航线 an air or shipping route; 干线 a trunk line; 路线 a route.
  • ⑤边缘交界的地方。防～、前～、界～。A boundary. 防线 a line of defence; 前线 the front; 界线 a dividing line.
  • ⑥指某种界限。生命～、贫困～。A limit or threshold. 生命线 a lifeline; 贫困线 the poverty line.
  • ⑦量词，用于抽象事物，数词限用"一"。一～希望、一～生机。Measure word for abstract things, only ever with 一. 一线希望 a ray of hope.
