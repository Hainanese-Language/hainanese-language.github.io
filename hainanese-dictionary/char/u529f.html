字义解释

gang¹ 同音字 8

gōng

  • ①功劳；贡献；跟"过"相对。立～、～臣、～过分明。Merit; a contribution; the opposite of 过. 立功 to render meritorious service; 功臣 one who has rendered great service; 功过分明 to draw a clear line between merit and fault.
  • ②成效；成就。成～、事半～倍、大～告成。Result; achievement. 成功 success; 事半功倍 half the effort for twice the result; 大功告成 the task is accomplished.
  • ③技术和技术修养。～夫、唱～、基本～。Skill and its cultivation. 功夫 skill acquired by training; 唱功 singing technique; 基本功 basic skills.
  • ④物理学上指力使物体移动所做的量。～率、做～。In physics, work. 功率 power; 做功 to do work.
