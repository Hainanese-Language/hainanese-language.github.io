字义解释

hhue³ 同音字 3

huǒ

  • ①同伴；同伴的关系。～伴、同～、结～、合～。A companion; companionship. 伙伴 a partner; 同伙 an associate; 结伙 to band together; 合伙 to go into partnership.
  • ②量词，用于人群。一～人、三～游客。Measure word for groups of people. 一伙人 a group of people; 三伙游客 three parties of tourists.
  • ③合在一起；共同。～同、～办、～着干。Jointly; together. 伙同 in company with; 伙办 to run jointly; 伙着干 to work together.
  • ④指伙食。～房、开～、起～。Meals provided. 伙房 a kitchen; 开伙 to start providing meals; 起伙 to set up a mess.
