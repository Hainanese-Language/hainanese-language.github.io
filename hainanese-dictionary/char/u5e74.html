字义解释

hhi²

nián

  • ①时间单位，地球绕太阳一周的时间。今～、～度、一～四季。A year, the time the earth takes to circle the sun. 今年 this year; 一年四季 the four seasons of the year.
  • ②每年的。～鉴、～会、～产量。Annual. 年鉴 a yearbook; 年产量 annual output.
  • ③年节。过～、～货、拜～。New Year. 过年 to celebrate the New Year; 拜年 to pay New Year calls.
  • ④岁数。～纪、～龄、～轻。Age. 年纪 age; 年轻 young.
  • ⑤人一生按年龄划分的阶段。童～、壮～、晚～。A stage of life. 童年 childhood; 晚年 old age.
  • ⑥时期。近～、明清～间。A period. 近年 in recent years; 明清年间 during the Ming and Qing.
  • ⑦收成。丰～、～成。A harvest. 丰年 a good year; 年成 the year's crop.
