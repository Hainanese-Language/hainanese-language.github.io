字义解释

song² 同音字 5

cóng

  • ①跟随。～师、～军、随～、～者如云。To follow. 从师 to study under a teacher; 从军 to join the army; 随从 an attendant; 从者如云 followers in throngs.
  • ②依顺；听从。服～、～命、力不～心。To comply; to obey. 服从 to submit to; 从命 to do as told; 力不从心 the spirit is willing but the strength is not.
  • ③参与；做某种事业。～政、～商、～事研究。To take part in; to engage in. 从政 to enter politics; 从商 to go into business; 从事研究 to do research.
  • ④采取某种态度或办法。～严处理、～简、～宽。To act in a certain manner. 从严处理 to deal with severely; 从简 to keep it simple; 从宽 to be lenient.
  • ⑤介词，表示起点或经过的路线。～今天起、～北京来、～门前经过。From; indicating a starting point or route. 从今天起 from today; 从北京来 to come from Beijing; 从门前经过 to pass by the door.
  • ⑥副词，用于否定，向来。～未去过、～不迟到。Ever; used with a negative. 从未去过 never been there; 从不迟到 never late.
  • ⑦堂房的亲属。～兄、～弟。Of the same clan branch. 从兄 an older male cousin; 从弟 a younger male cousin.
