字义解释

gi⁵ 同音字 5

jì

  • ①嫉妒。猜～、～才。To be jealous of. 猜忌 suspicious and envious; 忌才 to resent talent in others.
  • ②怕；顾虑。顾～、肆无～惮。To fear; scruple at. 顾忌 misgivings; 肆无忌惮 utterly unscrupulous.
  • ③禁戒。～口、～烟、禁～。To abstain from. 忌口 to avoid certain foods; 忌烟 to give up smoking; 禁忌 a taboo.
  • ④认为不吉利而避免。～讳、～日。To shun as unlucky. 忌讳 a taboo; 忌日 the anniversary of a death.
