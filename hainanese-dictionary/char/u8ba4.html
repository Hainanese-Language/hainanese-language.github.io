字义解释

yin¹

rèn

  • ①分辨；识别。～字、～路、辨～。To recognise. 认字 to learn to read; 认路 to know the way; 辨认 to identify.
  • ②表示同意；承担。～错、～账、～输。To acknowledge; to own up to. 认错 to admit a fault; 认输 to concede defeat.
  • ③建立关系。～亲、～干妈。To take on as kin. 认亲 to acknowledge a relative; 认干妈 to take an adoptive mother.
