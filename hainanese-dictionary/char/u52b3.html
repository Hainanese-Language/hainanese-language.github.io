字义解释

lao² 同音字 10

láo

  • ①劳动；工作。～力、～作、按～分配、不～而获。Labour; work. 劳力 manpower; 劳作 to toil; 按劳分配 distribution according to work; 不劳而获 to reap without sowing.
  • ②疲累。疲～、～累、积～成疾。Weary. 疲劳 tired; 劳累 worn out; 积劳成疾 to fall ill from overwork.
  • ③烦劳，请人做事的客气话。～驾、～您费心。A polite word used when asking a favour. 劳驾 excuse me; 劳您费心 sorry to trouble you.
  • ④功绩。功～、汗马之～。Meritorious service. 功劳 merit; 汗马之劳 distinguished service in the field.
  • ⑤慰劳。～军、犒～。To reward with comforts. 劳军 to bring comforts to the troops; 犒劳 to reward with food and drink.
