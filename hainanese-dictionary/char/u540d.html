字义解释

mia²

míng

  • ①名字；名称。姓～、地～、～称。A name. 姓名 a full name; 地名 a place name; 名称 a designation.
  • ②叫出；说出。莫～其妙、不可～状。To name; put into words. 莫名其妙 unaccountable; 不可名状 beyond description.
  • ③名义。以个人～义。In the name of. 以个人名义 in a personal capacity.
  • ④出名的；有名声的。～医、～著、～胜。Famous; well known. 名医 a noted doctor; 名著 a famous work; 名胜 a scenic spot.
  • ⑤名声；名誉。出～、～誉、有～无实。Fame; reputation. 出名 to become known; 有名无实 in name only.
  • ⑥量词，用于人。三～学生。Measure word for people. 三名学生 three students.
