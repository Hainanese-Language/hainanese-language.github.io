字义解释

gia²

xíng

  • ①走。步～、～走、日～千里。To walk; go. 步行 to go on foot; 日行千里 to cover a thousand li a day.
  • ②出外时用的。～装、～李。For use when travelling. 行装 luggage; 行李 baggage.
  • ③流通；推行。发～、～销、～之有效。To circulate; put into effect. 发行 to issue; 行之有效 proved effective in practice.
  • ④做；办。～礼、～医、～不通。To do; carry out. 行礼 to salute; 行医 to practise medicine; 行不通 it won't work.
  • ⑤可以。这样也～、～不～？All right; will do. 这样也行 that will do too.
  • ⑥能干。你真～。Capable. 你真行 you're really good.

o² 同音字 2

háng

  • ①行列。排～、单～、字里～间。A row; a line. 排行 order of seniority; 字里行间 between the lines.
  • ②行业。同～、内～、各～各业。A trade; a line of business. 同行 of the same trade; 各行各业 all walks of life.
  • ③某些营业机构。银～、商～、车～。A firm; a business house. 银行 a bank; 商行 a trading firm.
  • ④兄弟姐妹的次序。～二、排～。Order of birth among siblings. 行二 the second child.
  • ⑤量词，用于成行的东西。一～字、几～树。Measure word for things in a row. 一行字 a line of writing; 几行树 several rows of trees.
