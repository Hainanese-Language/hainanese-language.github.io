字义解释

ue¹ 同音字 3

huà

  • ①分开；分出界限。～分、～界、～清界限。To separate; to mark off. 划分 to divide; 划界 to demarcate; 划清界限 to draw a clear line.
  • ②拨给；转拨。～拨、～款、～归地方管理。To allocate; to transfer. 划拨 to appropriate; 划款 to transfer funds; 划归地方管理 to place under local control.
  • ③谋划；打算。计～、筹～、策～。To plan; to scheme. 计划 a plan; 筹划 to map out; 策划 to plot.
hhua² 同音字 8

huá

  • ①用桨拨水使船前进。～船、～桨、～水。To move a boat forward with oars. 划船 to row; 划桨 to paddle; 划水 to paddle in water.
  • ②在物体表面擦过或割开。～火柴、手～破了。To scrape across or cut open a surface. 划火柴 to strike a match; 手划破了 to cut one's hand.
  • ③合算。～得来、～不来。To be worthwhile. 划得来 to be worth it; 划不来 to be not worth it.
ue⁵ 同音字 2

huai

  • 用于"划拉"，指来回拨动、随手写画。～拉两下、在纸上乱～拉。Used in 划拉: to move back and forth; to scribble. 划拉两下 to give it a quick going-over; 在纸上乱划拉 to scribble on paper.
