字义解释

suan² 同音字 9

chuán

  • ①由一方交给另一方；由上代交给下代。～递、～授、～代、言～身教。To pass from one to another, or from one generation to the next. 传递 to hand on; 传授 to impart; 传代 to pass down; 言传身教 to teach by word and example.
  • ②散布；使广泛知道。～播、～说、宣～、～开了。To spread; to make widely known. 传播 to disseminate; 传说 it is said; 宣传 to publicize; 传开了 word got around.
  • ③命人前来。～讯、～唤。To summon. 传讯 to summon for questioning; 传唤 to serve a summons.
  • ④导。～热、～电、～导。To conduct. 传热 to conduct heat; 传电 to conduct electricity; 传导 conduction.
  • ⑤疾病感染。～染、～上感冒。To pass on a disease. 传染 to infect; 传上感冒 to catch a cold from someone.
duan⁵ 同音字 4

zhuàn

  • ①叙述人物生平的文字。～记、自～、小～。A written account of a person's life. 传记 a biography; 自传 an autobiography; 小传 a short biography.
  • ②解释经义的文字。经～、《左～》。A commentary expounding a classic. 经传 the classics and their commentaries, 《左传》 the Zuo Commentary.
