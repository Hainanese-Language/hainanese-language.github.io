字义解释

bin⁴ 同音字 6

biàn

  • ①和原来不同。～化、～样、情况有～。To change; to become different. 变化 change; 情况有变 the situation has altered.
  • ②使改变。～废为宝、～被动为主动。To transform. 变废为宝 to turn waste into wealth.
  • ③有重大影响的突然变化。事～、政～、兵～。A sudden change of consequence. 事变 an incident; 政变 a coup d'état.
  • ④变通。通权达～、随机应～。To adapt. 通权达变 to act as circumstances require; 随机应变 to respond to changing conditions.
  • ⑤变卖。～产。To sell off. 变产 to sell one's property.
