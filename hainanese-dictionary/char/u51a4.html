字义解释

in¹ 同音字 16

yuān

  • ①冤枉；不公平的待遇。～屈、伸～、不白之～。An injustice. 冤屈 a wrong; 伸冤 to right a wrong; 不白之冤 an unredressed injustice.
  • ②仇恨。～仇、～家、～～相报。Enmity. 冤仇 a feud; 冤家 a foe; 冤冤相报 to repay one wrong with another.
  • ③上当；吃亏。花～钱、这趟白跑真～。To be taken in; to lose out. 花冤钱 to waste money; 这趟白跑真冤 that wasted trip was a real swindle.
