字义解释

zio³ 同音字 3

shǎo

  • ①数量小。～数、～量、～见多怪。Few; little. 少数 a minority; 少见多怪 to marvel at what is merely unfamiliar.
  • ②缺；不够。～一个人、～不了。To lack; be short of. 少一个人 one person missing.
  • ③丢失。屋里～了东西。To be missing; go astray. 屋里少了东西 something has gone from the room.
  • ④暂时；稍微。～等、～安毋躁。For a moment; slightly. 少等 wait a little; 少安毋躁 be patient a while.
siao⁶

shào

  • ①年纪轻。～年、～女、男女老～。Young. 少年 a youth; 男女老少 men and women, old and young.
  • ②〈旧〉指有钱人家的儿子。～爷、阔～。〈former〉 The son of a rich family. 少爷 a young master; 阔少 a rich young idler.
