字义解释

gang¹ 同音字 8

gāng

  • ①才；表示行为发生在不久以前。他～走、～来就要回去。Just; indicating something happened a moment ago. 刚走 just left; 刚来就要回去 to want to leave right after arriving.
  • ②恰好；勉强达到。～好、大小～合适、这点钱～够。Exactly; barely. 刚好 just right; 大小刚合适 exactly the right size; 这点钱刚够 this money is just barely enough.
  • ③硬；坚强；跟"柔"相对。～强、～直、～正不阿。Hard; firm; the opposite of 柔. 刚强 staunch; 刚直 upright; 刚正不阿 upright and unyielding.
