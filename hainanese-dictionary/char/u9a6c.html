mǎ

  • ①哺乳动物，颈上有鬃，善跑，可供人骑或拉车。骑～、～车、～蹄。The horse, a maned mammal that runs well and is ridden or used for draught. 骑马 to ride a horse; 马车 a carriage; 马蹄 a hoof.
  • ②大。～蜂、～勺。Large. 马蜂 a hornet; 马勺 a large ladle.
  • ③姓。A surname.
