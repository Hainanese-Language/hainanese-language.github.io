字义解释

hao¹ 同音字 7

tōu

  • ①私下拿别人的东西据为己有。～窃、～东西、小～。To take what belongs to another. 偷窃 to steal; 偷东西 to pilfer; 小偷 a thief.
  • ②瞒着人做。～看、～听、～～摸摸。To do something behind others' backs. 偷看 to peek; 偷听 to eavesdrop; 偷偷摸摸 furtively.
  • ③抽出（时间）。～空、～闲、忙里～闲。To snatch (time). 偷空 to find a moment; 偷闲 to steal a little leisure; 忙里偷闲 to snatch a break from a busy schedule.
  • ④苟且；只顾眼前。～安、～生。To muddle along; to think only of the present. 偷安 to seek ease at the cost of the future; 偷生 to drag out an ignoble existence.
