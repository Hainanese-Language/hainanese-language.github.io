字义解释

hu² 同音字 7

tú

  • ①步行。～步。To go on foot. 徒步 on foot.
  • ②空的；没有凭借的。～手、～然。Bare; without support. 徒手 empty-handed; 徒然 to no purpose.
  • ③白白地。～劳、～费口舌。In vain. 徒劳 wasted effort; 徒费口舌 to waste one's breath.
  • ④只；仅仅。家～四壁、不～无益。Only; merely. 家徒四壁 nothing but four bare walls; 不徒无益 not merely useless.
  • ⑤徒弟；学生。～工、学～、师～。An apprentice; a disciple. 徒工 an apprentice; 学徒 a trainee; 师徒 master and pupil.
  • ⑥同一派别的人（多含贬义）。～众、党～、匪～。A member of a faction — usually disparaging. 党徒 a partisan; 匪徒 a bandit.
  • ⑦徒刑。有期～刑。Imprisonment. 有期徒刑 a fixed term of imprisonment.
