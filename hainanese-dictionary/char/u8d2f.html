字义解释

guan⁴ 同音字 8

guàn

  • ①穿过；连通。～穿、～通、横～。to pass through; run right through.
  • ②连续；一个接一个。～串、鱼～而入。continuous; one after another.
  • ③世代居住的地方。籍～、乡～。the place where a family has lived for generations.
  • ④旧时穿钱的绳子，也指一千个钱。万～家财。a string for copper coins in former times; also a thousand coins.
