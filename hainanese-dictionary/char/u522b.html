bid⁸ 同音字 6

bié

  • ①分离。～离、告～、临～、久～重逢。To part. 别离 to be parted; 告别 to say goodbye; 临别 on parting; 久别重逢 to meet again after a long separation.
  • ②分辨；区分。分～、辨～、鉴～。To tell apart; to distinguish. 分别 to differentiate; 辨别 to discern; 鉴别 to appraise.
  • ③差别；不同。天壤之～、～有风味。A difference. 天壤之别 a world of difference; 别有风味 to have a distinctive flavour.
  • ④另外的。～人、～处、～的办法。Other; another. 别人 other people; 别处 elsewhere; 别的办法 another way.
  • ⑤用别针等把东西卡住或插住。～上校徽、腰里～着一支笔。To fasten or stick something in place with a pin or the like. 别上校徽 to pin on a school badge; 腰里别着一支笔 with a pen stuck in one's belt.
  • ⑥副词，表示禁止或劝阻。～说话、～去了。Don't; used to forbid or dissuade. 别说话 don't talk; 别去了 don't go.
  • ⑦副词，表示揣测。他～是病了吧。Used to express a guess. 他别是病了吧 could he be ill?
bid⁸ 同音字 6

biè

  • 仅用于"别扭"：不顺心；合不来；说话做事不通顺。闹～扭、心里～扭、这句话很～扭。Only in 别扭: awkward; not getting along; not reading smoothly. 闹别扭 to be at odds; 心里别扭 to feel uneasy; 这句话很别扭 this sentence is clumsy.
