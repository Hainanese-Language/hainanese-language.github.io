字义解释

pan³ 同音字 2

fǎn

  • ①翻转；颠倒。～复、易如～掌、～败为胜。To turn over; to reverse. 反复 repeatedly; 反败为胜 to turn defeat into victory.
  • ②方向相背，跟"正"相对。～面、～方向、相～。Opposite in direction — the opposite of 正. 反面 the reverse side; 相反 contrary.
  • ③抵制；反对。～抗、～攻、造～。To resist; to oppose. 反抗 to revolt; 造反 to rebel.
  • ④类推。举一～三。To infer by analogy. 举一反三 to draw inferences from one instance.
  • ⑤回；还。～问、～省、～躬自问。Back; in return. 反问 to ask in return; 反躬自问 to examine oneself.
  • ⑥反而；相反地。～之、画虎不成～类犬。On the contrary. 反之 conversely; 画虎不成反类犬 to draw a tiger and end with a dog.
  • ⑦旧时的注音方法，反切。A former method of indicating pronunciation, 反切.
