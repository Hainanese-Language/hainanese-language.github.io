字义解释

se¹ 同音字 26

sī

  • ①想；考虑。～考、～索、深～熟虑。To think; consider. 思考 to reflect; 思索 to ponder; 深思熟虑 after careful thought.
  • ②想念。～念、相～、～乡。To long for. 思念 to miss; 相思 lovesickness; 思乡 homesick.
  • ③思路；想法。文～、构～、心～。A line of thought. 文思 literary inspiration; 构思 to conceive; 心思 what is on one's mind.
