字义解释

gid⁷ 同音字 25

jié

  • 〔jié〕
  • ①用线、绳等打成的疙瘩。打～、活～、蝴蝶～。A knot. 打结 to tie a knot; 活结 a slipknot; 蝴蝶结 a bow.
  • ②系；扎。～网、张灯～彩。To tie; fasten. 结网 to weave a net; 张灯结彩 hung with lanterns and streamers.
  • ③凝聚；聚合。～冰、～晶、凝～。To congeal; form. 结冰 to freeze over; 结晶 to crystallise; 凝结 to coagulate.
  • ④了结；收束。～束、～账、～案。To conclude; settle. 结束 to end; 结账 to settle a bill; 结案 to close a case.
  • ⑤发生某种关系。～交、～婚、～盟。To enter into a relationship. 结交 to make friends with; 结婚 to marry; 结盟 to form an alliance.
  • 〔jiē〕
  • ⑥长出果实或种子。开花～果、树上～满了桃子。To bear fruit or seed. 开花结果 to blossom and bear fruit; 树上结满了桃子 the tree is laden with peaches.
