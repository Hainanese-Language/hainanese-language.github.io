字义解释

se⁵ 同音字 3

shì

  • ①事情。～物、～件、大～小～。A matter; an affair. 事物 things; 事件 an incident.
  • ②职业；工作。谋～、共～。An occupation; work. 谋事 to look for a job; 共事 to work together.
  • ③事故；变故。出～、～端、平安无～。An accident; a mishap. 出事 to have an accident; 平安无事 safe and sound.
  • ④做；从事。不～生产、大～渲染。To engage in. 不事生产 to do no productive work.
  • ⑤侍奉。～亲。To serve; to wait on. 事亲 to serve one's parents.
