sui² 同音字 7

suí

  • ①跟着。～从、跟～、～行、～声附和。To follow. 随从 an attendant; 跟随 to follow; 随行 to travel with; 随声附和 to echo whatever is said.
  • ②顺从；任凭。～意、～和、～你便。To comply; to let be. 随意 as one pleases; 随和 easy-going; 随你便 suit yourself.
  • ③顺便；就着。～手关门、～口说说。Along with something else. 随手关门 to close the door on the way out; 随口说说 to say something offhand.
  • ④方像。他长得～他父亲。dial. To take after. 他长得随他父亲 he takes after his father.
