字义解释

giao² 同音字 7

qiáo

  • ①在国外居住。～居、～胞、～汇。To live abroad. 侨居 to reside abroad; 侨胞 compatriots overseas; 侨汇 remittances from abroad.
  • ②旅居国外的人。华～、外～、～眷。A person living abroad. 华侨 an overseas Chinese; 外侨 a foreign resident; 侨眷 the family of one living abroad.
