字义解释

li³ 同音字 12

lǐ

  • ①内部，跟"外"相对。～面、～屋、心～。The inside, as opposed to the outside.
  • ②衣服等的内层。被～、～子。The lining of a garment.
  • ③街坊；邻居。邻～、～弄。A neighbourhood; neighbours.
  • ④家乡。故～、乡～。One's native place.
  • ⑤长度单位，一～合五百米。～程、公～。The li, a unit of length equal to 500 metres.
