字义解释

van¹

wàn

  • ①数目，十个一千。～里长城。The number ten thousand. 万里长城 the Great Wall.
  • ②极多。～分、～能、气象～千。Very many. 万分 extremely; 气象万千 a scene of infinite variety.
  • ③极；很。～难、～不得已。Extremely. 万难 extremely difficult; 万不得已 as a last resort.
  • ④姓。A surname.
