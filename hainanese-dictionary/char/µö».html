字义解释

zi¹ 同音字 17

zhī

  • ①撑住；架起。～撑、～架、把帐篷～起来。To prop up; to put up. 支撑 to support; 把帐篷支起来 to pitch a tent.
  • ②受得住。乐不可～、体力不～。To bear; to hold out. 乐不可支 overcome with delight; 体力不支 unable to stand the strain.
  • ③领取或付出。～取、～付、开～。To draw or pay out. 支取 to draw money; 支付 to disburse; 开支 expenditure.
  • ④调派；使离开。～配、把人～走。To send away on an errand. 支配 to allocate; 把人支走 to get someone out of the way.
  • ⑤分出的；附属的。～流、～店、～部。A branch; subsidiary. 支流 a tributary; 支店 a branch shop; 支部 a branch organisation.
  • ⑥量词，用于杆状物、队伍、歌曲等。一～笔、一～队伍、一～歌。Measure word for rod-like things, for bodies of troops, and for songs. 一支笔 a pen; 一支队伍 a contingent.
  • ⑦地支的简称。干～。Short for the Earthly Branches. 干支 the Stems and Branches.
