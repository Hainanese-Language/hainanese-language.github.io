字义解释

sam¹ 同音字 3

cān

  • 〔cān〕
  • ①加入；参加。～军、～战、～政。To join; to take part in. 参军 to join the army; 参战 to enter a war; 参政 to take part in government.
  • ②参考；对照。～看、～照、～阅。To consult; to compare. 参看 to see also; 参照 to refer to; 参阅 to consult.
  • ③谒见；进见。～拜、～见。To pay one's respects to a superior. 参拜 to pay formal homage; 参见 to have an audience with.
  • ④弹劾。～他一本。To impeach. 参他一本 to lodge an impeachment against him.
  • 〔shēn〕
  • ⑤人参、党参等药用植物。～茸、海～。Ginseng and similar medicinal roots. 参茸 ginseng and antler; 海参 a sea cucumber.
sim¹ 同音字 3

cēn

  • 用于"参差"，指长短高低不齐。～差不齐。
