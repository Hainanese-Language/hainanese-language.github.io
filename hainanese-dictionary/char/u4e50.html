字义解释

log⁸ 同音字 10

lè

  • ①快乐；高兴。～趣、欢～、～不可支。Happy; glad. 乐趣 delight; 乐不可支 overcome with joy.
  • ②乐于；喜欢做。～意、～此不疲、助人为～。Glad to; to enjoy doing. 乐意 willing; 助人为乐 to take pleasure in helping others.
  • ③笑。把大家逗～了。To laugh. 把大家逗乐了 he had everyone laughing.
yiag⁸ 同音字 3

yuè

  • ①音乐。～曲、～器、奏～。Music. 乐曲 a musical composition; 乐器 a musical instrument.
  • ②姓。A surname.
