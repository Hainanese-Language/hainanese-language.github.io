字义解释

sid⁷ 同音字 16

qiē

  • 〔qiē〕
  • ①用刀把东西分开。～菜、～开、～片。To cut something apart with a blade. 切菜 to cut vegetables; 切开 to cut open; 切片 to slice.
  • ②几何学上指直线或圆同圆相接于一点。～线、～点。In geometry, to touch a circle at a single point. 切线 a tangent; 切点 the point of tangency.
  • 〔qiè〕
  • ③贴近；符合。～身、～合实际、贴～。Close to; matching. 切身 of direct personal concern; 切合实际 in keeping with the facts; 贴切 apt.
  • ④急迫。急～、迫～、～盼。Urgent. 急切 pressing; 迫切 urgent; 切盼 to look forward eagerly.
  • ⑤深；深切。～记、～齿、关～。Deep; keenly felt. 切记 to bear firmly in mind; 切齿 to gnash one's teeth; 关切 deep concern.
  • ⑥一定；务必。～勿大意、～不可粗心。By all means; be sure to. 切勿大意 on no account be careless; 切不可粗心 you must not be careless.
  • ⑦中医指诊脉。～脉、望闻问～。In Chinese medicine, to take the pulse. 切脉 to feel the pulse; 望闻问切 the four methods of diagnosis.
