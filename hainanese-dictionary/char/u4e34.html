字义解释

lim² 同音字 5

lín

  • ①靠近；对着。～街、～窗、居高～下。To be close to; to face. 临街 facing the street; 居高临下 to command from a height.
  • ②到；来到。光～、～场、双喜～门。To arrive. 光临 to honour with one's presence; 双喜临门 a double blessing.
  • ③将要；快要。～走、～别、～渴掘井。Just before. 临走 on the point of leaving; 临渴掘井 to dig a well only when thirsty.
  • ④照着字画摹仿。～摹、～帖。To copy from a model. 临摹 to copy; 临帖 to practise from a copybook.
