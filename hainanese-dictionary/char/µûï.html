字义解释

dai¹ 同音字 9

zhāi

用于第 ❶ 义

  • ①〈旧〉祭祀前整洁身心，表示虔敬。～戒、～沐。〈former〉 To purify oneself before a sacrifice. 斋戒 to fast and abstain; 斋沐 to bathe and fast.
  • ②信教的人所吃的素食。吃～、～饭。Vegetarian food eaten by the religious. 吃斋 to keep a vegetarian diet; 斋饭 a monastery meal.
  • ③舍饭给僧道。～僧。To give food to monks. 斋僧 to feed the monks.
  • ④书房或某些商店的名称。书～、荣宝～。A study, or the name of certain shops. 书斋 a study; 荣宝斋 the Rongbaozhai studio.
