字义解释

hiu² 同音字 4

qiú

  • ①请求；恳求。～助、～情、～人不如～己。To ask; to beg. 求助 to seek help; 求人不如求己 better to rely on oneself than on others.
  • ②设法得到；追求。～学、～职、力～上进。To seek; to pursue. 求学 to study; 力求上进 to strive to improve.
  • ③需要。供不应～、供～关系。To need; demand. 供不应求 supply falls short of demand.
  • ④数学上指演算出答案。～和、～积。In mathematics, to work out. 求和 to find the sum; 求积 to find the product.
