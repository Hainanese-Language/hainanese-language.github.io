字义解释

peng² 同音字 14

pēng

  • 〔pēng〕
  • ①多用于"～湃"，形容波涛相击，也形容气势雄壮。Mostly used in 澎湃, describing waves dashing together, and also a surging momentum.
  • 〔péng〕
  • ②用于地名。～湖(在台湾)。Used in place names: 澎湖 (the Penghu Islands, in Taiwan).
