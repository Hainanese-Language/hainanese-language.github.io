字义解释

ddan¹ 同音字 5

dān

  • ①一个；独一。～身、～独、～枪匹马、～行本。Single; sole. 单身 unmarried; 单独 alone; 单枪匹马 single-handed; 单行本 a separate edition.
  • ②奇数的；跟"双"相对。～数、～日、～号。Odd-numbered; the opposite of 双. 单数 an odd number; 单日 odd-numbered days; 单号 odd numbers.
  • ③只；仅。～说不做、～凭这一点还不够。Only; merely. 单说不做 to talk without acting; 单凭这一点还不够 this alone is not enough.
  • ④不复杂。～纯、简～、～调。Uncomplicated. 单纯 simple; 简单 plain; 单调 monotonous.
  • ⑤薄弱。～薄、势～力孤。Weak; thin. 单薄 flimsy; 势单力孤 weak and isolated.
  • ⑥只有一层的。～衣、～裤、被～。Of a single layer. 单衣 unlined clothing; 单裤 unlined trousers; 被单 a bed sheet.
  • ⑦记载事项的纸片。名～、账～、清～、菜～。A slip recording items. 名单 a name list; 账单 a bill; 清单 an itemized list; 菜单 a menu.

shàn

  • ②用于地名，如单县（在山东）。Used in place names, such as 单县 Shan County in Shandong.
