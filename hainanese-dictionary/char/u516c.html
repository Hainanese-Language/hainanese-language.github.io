字义解释

gong¹ 同音字 14

gōng

  • ①属于国家或大众的；跟"私"相对。～共、～物、～款、大～无私。Belonging to the state or the public; the opposite of 私. 公共 public; 公物 public property; 公款 public funds; 大公无私 selfless.
  • ②共同的；大家承认的。～认、～约、～海、～理。Shared; commonly accepted. 公认 generally acknowledged; 公约 a joint pledge; 公海 the high seas; 公理 an axiom.
  • ③让大家知道。～布、～开、～告。To make known to all. 公布 to announce; 公开 to make public; 公告 a proclamation.
  • ④公平；不偏私。～正、～道、办事很～。Fair; impartial. 公正 just; 公道 equitable; 办事很公 to deal fairly.
  • ⑤国家或集体的事务。办～、～事、因～出差。State or collective business. 办公 to work in an office; 公事 official business; 因公出差 to travel on official business.
  • ⑥雄性的（禽兽）。～鸡、～牛。Male (of birds and beasts). 公鸡 a rooster; 公牛 a bull.
  • ⑦丈夫的父亲；也用于对年长男子的称呼。～婆、外～、张～。A husband's father; also a form of address for an older man. 公婆 parents-in-law; 外公 maternal grandfather; 张公 Mr Zhang.
