字义解释

gao⁴

dào

  • ①达于某处；抵达。～达、～站、～期、火车～了。To reach a place; to arrive. 到达 to arrive; 到站 to reach a station; 到期 to fall due; 火车到了 the train has arrived.
  • ②往；去。～北京去、～朋友家坐坐。To go to. 到北京去 to go to Beijing; 到朋友家坐坐 to drop in on a friend.
  • ③用在动词后，表示动作有结果。看～、办得～、说～做～。Used after a verb to show the action achieved its result. 看到 to catch sight of; 办得到 can be done; 说到做到 to do as one says.
  • ④周全；细致。想得很～、面面俱～。Thorough; attentive. 想得很到 to have thought it through; 面面俱到 to attend to every aspect.
