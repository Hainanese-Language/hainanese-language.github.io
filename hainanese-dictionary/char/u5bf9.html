字义解释

ddui⁴ 同音字 5

duì

  • ①回答。～答如流、无言以～。To answer. 对答如流 to answer readily; 无言以对 to have no reply.
  • ②朝着；面向。～着镜子、面～面。To face. 对着镜子 in front of a mirror; 面对面 face to face.
  • ③对待；对付。～事不～人、～症下药。To treat; to deal with. 对事不对人 to address the matter, not the person; 对症下药 to suit the remedy to the case.
  • ④相合；正确。～头、说得～、答案不～。To match; correct. 对头 on the right lines; 说得对 rightly said; 答案不对 the answer is wrong.
  • ⑤使两样东西配合或接触。～号、～光、～表。To bring two things together. 对号 to check numbers against each other; 对光 to adjust the focus; 对表 to set watches together.
  • ⑥核对；比较。～账、校～、～笔迹。To check against. 对账 to check accounts; 校对 to proofread; 对笔迹 to compare handwriting.
  • ⑦两个；成双的。～联、～称、～偶。Two; forming a pair. 对联 a couplet; 对称 symmetrical; 对偶 antithesis.
  • ⑧量词，用于成双的人或物。一～夫妻、两～枕头。Measure word for pairs. 一对夫妻 a married couple; 两对枕头 two pairs of pillows.
  • ⑨掺和。～水、～点儿酒。To mix in. 对水 to add water; 对点儿酒 to add a little wine.
  • ⑩介词，向；朝。～我笑了笑、～他说。To; towards. 对我笑了笑 to smile at me; 对他说 to say to him.
