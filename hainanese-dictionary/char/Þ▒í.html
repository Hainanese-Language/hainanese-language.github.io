字义解释

sio⁶ 同音字 2

xiàng

用于第 ❶ 义

  • ①哺乳动物，鼻子长而能伸卷，多有长牙。大～、～牙。The elephant, a mammal with a long flexible trunk and usually long tusks. 大象 an elephant; 象牙 ivory.
  • ②形状；样子。景～、气～、印～、万～更新。A form; an appearance. 景象 a scene; 气象 the atmosphere or weather; 印象 an impression; 万象更新 all things take on a new look.
  • ③仿效；摹拟。～形、～声。To imitate; to model on. 象形 pictographic; 象声 onomatopoeic.
