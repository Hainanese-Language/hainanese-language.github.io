字义解释

dduad⁸

duó

  • ①强取；抢。抢～、掠～、～权。To seize by force. 抢夺 to snatch; 掠夺 to plunder; 夺权 to seize power.
  • ②争取得到。～冠、～标、～丰收。To win; carry off. 夺冠 to take the title; 夺丰收 to bring in a bumper harvest.
  • ③使失去；剥去。剥～、～去他的权利。To strip of; deprive. 剥夺 to deprive.
  • ④冲出；闪耀。～门而出、光彩～目。To burst out; dazzle. 夺门而出 to rush out of the door; 光彩夺目 dazzlingly brilliant.
