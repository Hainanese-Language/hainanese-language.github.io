字义解释

in¹ 同音字 16

yān

  • ①物质燃烧时产生的气状物。冒～、～雾、～囱。Smoke. 冒烟 to give off smoke; 烟雾 haze; 烟囱 a chimney.
  • ②像烟的东西。～霞、云～。Something resembling smoke. 烟霞 mist and rosy clouds; 云烟 clouds and mist.
  • ③烟草制成品。香～、～斗、抽～。Tobacco products. 香烟 a cigarette; 烟斗 a pipe; 抽烟 to smoke.
  • ④鸦片。～土、大～。Opium. 烟土 raw opium; 大烟 opium.
  • ⑤烟气刺激眼睛。～了眼睛。(of smoke) To sting the eyes. 烟了眼睛 the smoke made my eyes smart.
