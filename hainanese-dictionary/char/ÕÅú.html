字义解释

hao³ 同音字 4

kǒu

  • ①人和动物进饮食的器官；嘴。张～、～腔、～渴。The mouth, the organ by which people and animals take food. 张口 to open the mouth; 口渴 thirsty.
  • ②容器通到外面的地方。瓶～、碗～。The opening of a container. 瓶口 the neck of a bottle.
  • ③出入通过的地方。门～、路～、出～、关～。A place of entry or passage. 门口 a doorway; 路口 a crossing; 出口 an exit.
  • ④破裂的地方。伤～、裂～、决～。A tear or breach. 伤口 a wound; 决口 a burst dyke.
  • ⑤刀剑锋利的部分。刀～、～子。The cutting edge of a blade. 刀口 the edge of a knife.
  • ⑥指人口。三～之家、拖家带～。Population; head count. 三口之家 a family of three.
  • ⑦量词，用于人、牲畜或有口的器物。三～人、一～猪、两～锅。Measure word for people, livestock and vessels with an opening. 三口人 three people; 两口锅 two pots.
  • ⑧指说的话。～才、～音、～是心非。What one says; speech. 口才 eloquence; 口是心非 to say one thing and mean another.
