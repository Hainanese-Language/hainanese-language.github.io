字义解释

dai³ 同音字 7

shǐ

  • ①派遣；支使。～唤、指～、～人前往。To send on an errand; to order about. 使唤 to order about; 指使 to instigate; 使人前往 to send someone there.
  • ②用。～用、～劲、～筷子。To use. 使用 to use; 使劲 to exert oneself; 使筷子 to use chopsticks.
  • ③让；致使。～人满意、～问题得到解决。To make; to cause. 使人满意 to give satisfaction; 使问题得到解决 to bring about a solution.
  • ④假如。假～、即～、～有不测。If. 假使 supposing; 即使 even if; 使有不测 should anything go wrong.
  • ⑤奉命办事的人。～者、大～、公～、特～。An envoy sent on a mission. 使者 a messenger; 大使 an ambassador; 公使 a minister; 特使 a special envoy.
