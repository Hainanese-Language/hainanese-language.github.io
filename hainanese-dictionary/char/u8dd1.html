字义解释

pao³

pǎo

  • ①快速前进；奔跑。～步、奔～、～得很快。To run; to move quickly. 跑步 to run; 奔跑 to run or dash; 跑得很快 to run very fast.
  • ②逃走；离开。～掉、逃～、别让他～了。To escape; to get away. 跑掉 to get away; 逃跑 to flee; 别让他跑了 don't let him get away.
  • ③为某事四处奔走。～业务、～买卖、～手续。To go around handling or arranging something. 跑业务 to make business calls; 跑买卖 to conduct business; 跑手续 to handle formalities.
  • ④离开原来的位置、范围或状态。～气、～电、～水、～题。To move or stray from its proper place, range, or state; to leak or escape. 跑气 to leak gas; 跑电 to leak electricity; 跑水 to leak water; 跑题 to go off topic.
