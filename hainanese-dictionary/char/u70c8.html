字义解释

lid⁸ 同音字 6

liè

  • ①猛；强烈。猛～、～火、～酒。Fierce; intense. 猛烈 violent; 烈火 a raging fire; 烈酒 strong liquor.
  • ②刚直；有节操。刚～、贞～、～女。Upright; steadfast. 刚烈 staunch; 贞烈 chaste and unyielding.
  • ③为正义事业献身的人。～士、先～。One who dies for a just cause. 烈士 a martyr; 先烈 the fallen heroes.
