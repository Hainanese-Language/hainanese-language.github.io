字义解释

leng² 同音字 20

líng

  • ①冰。～汛、冰～。Ice. 凌汛 an ice-jam flood; 冰凌 icicles.
  • ②侵犯；欺压。～辱、欺～、盛气～人。To insult; to bully. 凌辱 to humiliate; 盛气凌人 overbearing.
  • ③逼近。～晨。To draw near. 凌晨 in the small hours.
  • ④升高；在空中。～空、～云壮志。To rise high; in the air. 凌空 to soar; 凌云壮志 lofty aspirations.
