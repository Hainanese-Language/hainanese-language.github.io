字义解释

bag⁷ 同音字 8

bǔ

  • ①占卜，古代用火灼龟甲以预测吉凶。～卦、占～。Divination by scorching a tortoise shell. 卜卦 to cast a hexagram; 占卜 to divine.
  • ②预料；推断。预～、生死未～。To foretell. 预卜 to predict; 生死未卜 whether alive or dead is unknown.
  • ③书选择处所。～居。lit. To choose a place to settle. 卜居 to choose a dwelling place.
