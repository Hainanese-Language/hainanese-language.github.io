字义解释

dong⁴ 同音字 2

zòng

  • ①南北方向；跟"横"相对。～贯、～深、～队。Running north and south; the opposite of 横. 纵贯 to run through lengthwise; 纵深 depth; 纵队 a column.
  • ②释放。～虎归山、～火。To let loose. 纵虎归山 to let the tiger back to the mountain; 纵火 to set fire.
  • ③放任；不约束。放～、～容、～情。To indulge; give free rein to. 放纵 unrestrained; 纵容 to connive at; 纵情 to one's heart's content.
  • ④身体猛然向上或向前。～身一跳。To leap. 纵身一跳 to spring forward.
  • ⑤即使。～然、～有千难万险。Even if. 纵然 even though; 纵有千难万险 however many hardships there may be.
