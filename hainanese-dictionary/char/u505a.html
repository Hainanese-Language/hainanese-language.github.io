字义解释

do⁶ 同音字 5

zuò

  • ①制造。～衣服、～家具、～饭。To make. 做衣服 to make clothes; 做家具 to make furniture; 做饭 to cook a meal.
  • ②从事某种工作或活动。～事、～买卖、～作业。To engage in work or an activity. 做事 to work; 做买卖 to do business; 做作业 to do homework.
  • ③举行；举办。～寿、～生日、～礼拜。To hold; to put on. 做寿 to celebrate a birthday; 做生日 to hold a birthday party; 做礼拜 to go to church.
  • ④充当；担任。～官、～东、～榜样。To serve as. 做官 to be an official; 做东 to play host; 做榜样 to set an example.
  • ⑤用作。这块布可以～窗帘。To use as. 这块布可以做窗帘 this cloth will do for curtains.
  • ⑥结成某种关系。～朋友、～亲、～伴。To enter into a relationship. 做朋友 to be friends; 做亲 to become relatives by marriage; 做伴 to keep company.
