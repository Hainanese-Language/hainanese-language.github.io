hhua² 同音字 8

hān

  • ①熟睡时因呼吸受阻而发出的粗重声音。打～、～声如雷。Snoring; the heavy breathing sound made during sleep when airflow is partly obstructed. 打鼾 to snore; 鼾声如雷 snoring as loud as thunder.
  • ②打鼾；睡觉时发出鼾声。昨晚他～了一夜。To snore; to make a snoring sound while asleep. 他鼾了一夜 he snored all night.'
