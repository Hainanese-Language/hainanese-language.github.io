字义解释

ddoi¹ 同音字 2

dī

  • ①从下向上距离小；跟"高"相对。～处、～空、水位～。Low in height; the opposite of 高. 低处 a low place; 低空 low altitude; 水位低 the water level is low.
  • ②在一般标准或平均程度之下。～级、～温、～价、～估。Below the usual standard or average. 低级 elementary; 低温 low temperature; 低价 a low price; 低估 to underrate.
  • ③等级在下的。～年级、职位～。Junior in rank. 低年级 the lower grades; 职位低 low in position.
  • ④向下垂。～头、～眉顺眼。To lower. 低头 to bow one's head; 低眉顺眼 meek and submissive.
