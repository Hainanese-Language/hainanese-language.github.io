字义解释

sang⁴ 同音字 4

chuàng

  • 开始做；第一次做。～立、～办、～作、开～、首～。To start; to do for the first time. 创立 to found; 创办 to set up; 创作 to create a work; 开创 to initiate; 首创 to originate.
suang¹

chuāng

  • ①伤口。～伤、～口、刀～。A wound. 创伤 a wound; 创口 an incision; 刀创 a knife wound.
  • ②使受重大损害。重～敌军。To inflict heavy damage on. 重创敌军 to deal the enemy a heavy blow.
