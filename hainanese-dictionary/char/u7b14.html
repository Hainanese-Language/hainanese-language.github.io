bid⁷ 同音字 4

bǐ

  • ①写字画画的用具。毛～、钢～、～尖。A writing or drawing implement; a pen or brush.
  • ②用笔写。～录、代～、～之于书。To write down.
  • ③写字画画的技巧或风格。～法、～力、伏～。Technique or style in writing and painting.
  • ④量词，用于款项、书画。一～钱、几～账。Measure word for sums of money and for works of calligraphy or painting.
