字义解释

de¹ 同音字 3

shēng

  • ①生育；出生。～孩子、～日、出～。To give birth; to be born. 生孩子 to bear a child; 出生 to be born.
  • ②生长。～根、～芽、土～土长。To grow. 生根 to take root; 土生土长 born and bred locally.
  • ③活着；有生命力。～存、～物、贪～怕死。Alive; living. 生存 to survive; 贪生怕死 to cling to life and fear death.
  • ④生命。丧～、舍～取义。Life. 丧生 to lose one's life; 舍生取义 to die for a just cause.
  • ⑤一辈子。今～、一～一世。A lifetime. 今生 this life; 一生一世 all one's life.
  • ⑥产生；发生。～病、～锈、～事。To produce; to arise. 生病 to fall ill; 生锈 to rust.
  • ⑦使燃料燃烧。～火、～炉子。To light a fire. 生火 to make a fire; 生炉子 to light a stove.
  • ⑧没有煮熟的。～饭、夹～。Uncooked. 生饭 half-cooked rice; 夹生 underdone.
  • ⑨没有加工过的。～铁、～石灰、～丝。Unprocessed; raw. 生铁 pig iron; 生丝 raw silk.
  • ⑩不熟悉；不熟练。～人、～疏、～字。Unfamiliar; unpractised. 生人 a stranger; 生字 a new word.
  • ⑪学习的人。学～、师～、招～。A student. 学生 a pupil; 师生 teachers and students.
  • ⑫戏曲角色行当之一。小～、老～、武～。 ⑬ 后缀，用在某些副词后。好～、怎～是好。A role type in Chinese opera. 小生 a young male role; 武生 a martial role. ⑬ Suffix after certain adverbs. 好生 carefully; 怎生是好 what is to be done?
