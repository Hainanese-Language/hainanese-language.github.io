min¹ 同音字 2

miàn

  • ①头的前部；脸。～孔、～带笑容、当～说清楚。The face. 面孔 a face; 面带笑容 with a smile on one's face.
  • ②物体的表面。地～、水～、桌～。The surface of a thing. 地面 the ground; 水面 the surface of water.
  • ③方位；部位。前～、四～八方、正～。A side; an aspect. 前面 in front; 四面八方 on every side.
  • ④当面。～谈、～议、～交。Face to face. 面谈 to talk in person; 面交 to hand over in person.
  • ⑤几何学上指线移动所成的形迹，有长有宽没有厚。～积、平～。In geometry, a surface — having length and breadth but no thickness. 面积 area; 平面 a plane.
  • ⑥范围。全～、～面俱到、片～。Scope; extent. 全面 comprehensive; 片面 one-sided.
  • ⑦量词，用于扁平的物体或会见的次数。一～旗、见过两～。Measure word for flat objects and for meetings. 一面旗 a flag; 见过两面 to have met twice.

  • ②粉末。药～儿、胡椒～儿。Powder. 药面儿 powdered medicine.
  • ③面条。挂～、下～。Noodles. 挂面 dried noodles; 下面 to cook noodles.
  • ④〈方〉食物纤维少而柔软。这块地瓜很～。〈dial.〉 Soft and floury. 这块地瓜很面 this sweet potato is very floury.
