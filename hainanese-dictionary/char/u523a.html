字义解释

si⁴ 同音字 12

cì

  • ①尖锐像针的东西。鱼～、竹～、～猬、话里带～。Something sharp like a needle. 鱼刺 a fish bone; 竹刺 a bamboo splinter; 刺猬 a hedgehog; 话里带刺 to speak with a barb.
  • ②用尖的东西扎进去。～穿、～伤、～绣。To stab or pierce with something sharp. 刺穿 to pierce through; 刺伤 to stab and wound; 刺绣 to embroider.
  • ③暗杀。～客、行～、遇～。To assassinate. 刺客 an assassin; 行刺 to make an attempt on someone's life; 遇刺 to be assassinated.
  • ④讽刺；指责。讥～、～儿话。To satirize; to censure. 讥刺 to gibe; 刺儿话 cutting remarks.
  • ⑤使感官受到强烈作用。～鼻、～眼、～激。To act strongly on the senses. 刺鼻 pungent; 刺眼 dazzling; 刺激 to stimulate.

cī

  • 拟声，多用于"刺棱""刺溜"。～溜一声滑了下去。Imitating a sound, mostly in 刺棱 and 刺溜. 刺溜一声滑了下去 to slip down with a swish.
