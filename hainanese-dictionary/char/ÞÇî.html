字义解释

lu³ 同音字 7

ér

  • ①连词，表示并列。伟大～光荣。Conjunction marking coordination. 伟大而光荣 great and glorious.
  • ②连词，表示承接。取～代之。Conjunction marking succession. 取而代之 to take its place.
  • ③连词，表示转折。华～不实、少～精。Conjunction marking contrast. 华而不实 showy but without substance.
  • ④连词，连接状语和中心语。侃侃～谈、挺身～出。Conjunction joining an adverbial to its verb. 挺身而出 to step boldly forward.
  • ⑤〈书〉助词，表示从……到……。自上～下、由古～今。〈lit.〉 Particle marking a range from one point to another. 自上而下 from top to bottom; 由古而今 from ancient times to the present.
