字义解释

diao¹ 同音字 19

xiào

  • ①撮起嘴唇发出长而清越的声音。长～、～聚。To whistle with pursed lips. 长啸 to give a long whistle; 啸聚 to gather a band by whistling.
  • ②某些动物拉长声音叫。虎～、猿～。(of certain animals) To howl. 虎啸 a tiger's roar; 猿啸 the cry of gibbons.
  • ③自然界发出的长声。海～、风～。A prolonged natural sound. 海啸 a tsunami; 风啸 the howling of wind.
