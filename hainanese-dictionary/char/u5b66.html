字义解释

o⁵ 同音字 2

xué

  • ①学习。～文化、勤～苦练、活到老～到老。To learn; study. 学文化 to acquire an education; 活到老学到老 one is never too old to learn.
  • ②模仿。～舌、～鸟叫。To imitate. 学舌 to parrot; 学鸟叫 to mimic birdsong.
  • ③学问；知识。博～、才疏～浅、治～。Learning; knowledge. 博学 erudite; 才疏学浅 of modest ability and shallow learning.
  • ④分门别类的知识体系。文～、数～、～科。A branch of knowledge. 文学 literature; 数学 mathematics; 学科 a discipline.
  • ⑤学校。上～、小～、～费。A school. 上学 to attend school; 小学 a primary school; 学费 tuition fees.
