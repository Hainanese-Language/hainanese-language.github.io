字义解释

sa¹ 同音字 16

chà

  • ①由主干分出的。～路、～道、三～路口。Branching off from the main line. 岔路 a fork in the road; 三岔路口 a three-way junction.
  • ②转移话题。打～、把话～开。To turn the conversation aside. 打岔 to interrupt; 把话岔开 to change the subject.
  • ③时间上错开。把两个会～开。To stagger in time. 把两个会岔开 to hold the two meetings at different times.
  • ④差错；意外。出～子。A mishap; something going wrong. 出岔子 to go awry.
