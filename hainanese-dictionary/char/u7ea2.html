字义解释

ang² 同音字 2

hóng

  • ①像血的颜色。～旗、～花、脸～了。Red, the colour of blood. 红旗 a red flag; 脸红了 to blush.
  • ②象征喜庆的红布。挂～、披～。Red cloth as a token of celebration. 挂红 to hang red cloth; 披红 to drape in red.
  • ③顺利；受人重视。走～、～人、开门～。Successful; in favour. 走红 to be in vogue; 红人 a favourite; 开门红 a good start.
  • ④象征革命。～军、～色政权。Symbolising revolution. 红军 the Red Army; 红色政权 red political power.
  • ⑤红利。分～。A dividend. 分红 to share out profits.
