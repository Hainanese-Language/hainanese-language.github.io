字义解释

nab⁸ 同音字 4

nà

  • ①收进来；接受。～入、采～、出～。To take in; accept. 纳入 to bring into; 采纳 to adopt; 出纳 to receive and pay out.
  • ②享受。～凉、～福。To enjoy. 纳凉 to take the cool air; 纳福 to live in comfort.
  • ③交付。～税、缴～。To pay in. 纳税 to pay tax; 缴纳 to hand over.
  • ④缝纫时密密地缝。～鞋底。To stitch closely. 纳鞋底 to stitch a shoe sole.
