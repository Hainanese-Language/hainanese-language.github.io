字义解释

ziang¹ 同音字 12

zhāng

  • ①使合拢的东西分开或张紧。～嘴、～弓、纲举目～。To open or draw taut. 张嘴 to open one's mouth; 张弓 to draw a bow; 纲举目张 once the main rope is lifted the meshes open.
  • ②陈设；铺排。～灯结彩、大～旗鼓。To set out; display. 张灯结彩 to hang lanterns and streamers; 大张旗鼓 with great fanfare.
  • ③看；望。东～西望。To look. 东张西望 to gaze about.
  • ④商店开业。开～。(of a shop) To open for business. 开张 to open up.
  • ⑤量词，用于纸、桌子、嘴、弓等。一～纸、两～床。Measure word for paper, tables, mouths and bows. 一张纸 a sheet of paper; 两张床 two beds.
  • ⑥姓。A surname.
