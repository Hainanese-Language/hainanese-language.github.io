字义解释

se¹ 同音字 26

zhī

  • ①〈书〉助词，相当于"的"。三分～一、光荣～家。〈lit.〉 Particle equivalent to 的. 三分之一 one third; 光荣之家 a family of honour.
  • ②〈书〉代词，代人或事物，只作宾语。取而代～、言～有理。〈lit.〉 Pronoun standing for a person or thing; used only as an object. 取而代之 to take its place; 言之有理 what is said makes sense.
  • ③〈书〉往；到。由京～沪。〈lit.〉 To go to. 由京之沪 to go from Beijing to Shanghai.
