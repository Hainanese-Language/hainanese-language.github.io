字义解释

mao³ 同音字 3

mǎo

  • ①地支的第四位。The fourth of the twelve Earthly Branches.
  • ②卯时，旧式计时法指早晨五点到七点。点～、应～。The double-hour from five to seven in the morning by the old reckoning. 点卯 to call the roll at the start of the day; 应卯 to answer the roll.
  • ③器物接榫的凹进部分。～眼、凿～。The socket of a mortise-and-tenon joint. 卯眼 a mortise; 凿卯 to cut a mortise.
