字义解释

bo³ 同音字 3

bǎo

  • ①保护；使不受损害。～卫、～护、～养、～密。To protect from harm. 保卫 to defend; 保护 to safeguard; 保养 to maintain; 保密 to keep secret.
  • ②保持；使继续存在。～温、～鲜、～存、～质。To keep; to preserve. 保温 to keep warm; 保鲜 to keep fresh; 保存 to preserve; 保质 to guarantee quality.
  • ③担保；负责。～证、～修、～险、～荐。To guarantee; to answer for. 保证 to warrant; 保修 a service guarantee; 保险 insurance; 保荐 to recommend on one's own surety.
  • ④旧时的一种户籍编制。～甲。A household registration unit in former times. 保甲 the baojia system.
