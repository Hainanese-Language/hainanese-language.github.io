字义解释

ddin⁶ 同音字 4

diàn

  • ①一种能量，可产生光、热、动力等。～灯、发～、～能。Electricity. 电灯 an electric light; 发电 to generate power.
  • ②闪电。雷～、风驰～掣。Lightning. 雷电 thunder and lightning; 风驰电掣 as fast as lightning.
  • ③电报；打电报。急～、～告、通～。A telegram; to telegraph. 急电 an urgent wire; 电告 to inform by wire.
  • ④触电。被～了一下。To get an electric shock. 被电了一下 to get a shock.
