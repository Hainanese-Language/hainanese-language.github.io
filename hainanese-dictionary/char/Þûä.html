字义解释

bo⁶ 同音字 3

báo

  • ①扁平物上下两面之间距离小。～纸、～片、～被。Thin; slight in thickness. 薄纸 thin paper; 薄片 a slice.
  • ②味道淡。酒味很～。Weak in flavour. 酒味很薄 the wine is thin.
  • ③感情冷淡；交情浅。待他不～、交情～。Cool in feeling; slight in friendship. 交情薄 on distant terms.
  • ④土地不肥沃。～地、～田。(of soil) Poor; infertile. 薄地 barren land.
bog⁷ 同音字 23

bó

  • ①微少；不厚道。～礼、～利、刻～。Meagre; ungenerous. 薄礼 a small gift; 薄利 a slim margin; 刻薄 harsh.
  • ②轻视。鄙～、厚此～彼。To slight; look down on. 鄙薄 to disdain; 厚此薄彼 to favour one over another.
  • ③迫近。日～西山。To draw near. 日薄西山 the sun sinking in the west.
