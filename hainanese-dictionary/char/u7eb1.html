字义解释

sa¹ 同音字 16

shā

  • ①用棉、麻等纺成的细缕。棉～、～厂、～锭。Yarn spun from cotton, hemp and the like. 棉纱 cotton yarn; 纱厂 a spinning mill; 纱锭 a spindle.
  • ②经纬稀疏的织品。～布、窗～、面～。A loosely woven fabric. 纱布 gauze; 窗纱 window screening; 面纱 a veil.
  • ③像纱布的制品。铁～、塑料～。A mesh product resembling gauze. 铁纱 wire gauze; 塑料纱 plastic mesh.
