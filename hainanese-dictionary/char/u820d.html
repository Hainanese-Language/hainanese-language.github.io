字义解释

de³

shè

  • ①房屋；住处。宿～、校～、寒～、旅～。A house; lodgings. 宿舍 a dormitory; 校舍 school buildings; 寒舍 my humble home; 旅舍 an inn.
  • ②谦称自己家里比自己年幼的亲属。～弟、～侄。A humble term for one's younger relatives. 舍弟 my younger brother; 舍侄 my nephew.
  • ③古代行军三十里为一舍。退避三～。Thirty li of march in ancient times. 退避三舍 to give way to avoid a conflict.
dia³ 同音字 2

shě

  • ①放弃；不要。～弃、～己为人、依依不～。To give up; to let go. 舍弃 to abandon; 舍己为人 to sacrifice oneself for others; 依依不舍 reluctant to part.
  • ②施舍。～粥、～药。To give alms. 舍粥 to hand out gruel; 舍药 to give out free medicine.
