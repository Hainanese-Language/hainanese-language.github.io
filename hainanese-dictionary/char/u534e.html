字义解释

hhua¹ 同音字 8

huá

  • 〔huá〕
  • ①光彩；光辉。光～、～灯。Splendour; brilliance. 光华 radiance; 华灯 ornamental lamps.
  • ②繁盛。繁～、荣～富贵。Flourishing. 繁华 bustling and prosperous; 荣华富贵 wealth and honour.
  • ③事物最好的部分。精～、含英咀～。The finest part of a thing. 精华 the essence; 含英咀华 to savour the best of literature.
  • ④奢侈。奢～、～而不实。Extravagant. 奢华 luxurious; 华而不实 showy but hollow.
  • ⑤指中国。～夏、～侨、中～。China. 华夏 ancient China; 华侨 an overseas Chinese; 中华 China.
  • ⑥〈书〉花白。～发。〈lit.〉 Grey-haired. 华发 greying hair.
  • 〔huà〕
  • ⑦华山，在陕西。Mount Hua, in Shaanxi.
  • ⑧姓。A surname.
  • 〔huā〕
  • ⑨〈古〉同"花"。〈arch.〉 Same as 花.
