字义解释

hham² 同音字 5

xián

  • ①用嘴含着。燕子～泥。To hold in the mouth. 燕子衔泥 swallows carrying mud in their beaks.
  • ②心里怀着。～恨、～冤。To harbour in the heart. 衔恨 to nurse a grievance; 衔冤 to suffer a wrong in silence.
  • ③相连接。～接、首尾相～。To link up. 衔接 to join; 首尾相衔 head joined to tail.
  • ④职务或级别的名号。军～、职～、头～。A rank or title. 军衔 military rank; 头衔 a title.
