duō

  • ①数量大；跟"少"相对。～数、许～、人～。Many; much — the opposite of 少. 多数 the majority; 许多 a great many.
  • ②超出原有或应有的数目。～心、～事、～了两个。More than there was or should be. 多心 oversuspicious; 多了两个 two too many.
  • ③表示相差的程度大。好得～、大～了。Marking a large difference. 好得多 much better; 大多了 far bigger.
  • ④过分的；不必要的。～嘴、～虑。Excessive; uncalled for. 多嘴 to speak out of turn; 多虑 to worry needlessly.
  • ⑤用在问句中，问程度或数量。～大、～远、～长时间。In questions, asking degree or amount. 多大 how big; 多远 how far.
  • ⑥表示程度高。～好啊、～美的景色。Marking a high degree. 多好啊 how good; 多美的景色 what a beautiful view.
