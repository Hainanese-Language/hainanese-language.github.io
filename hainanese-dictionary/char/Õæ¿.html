ziu¹ 同音字 7

zhōu

  • ①圈子；环绕一圈。圆～、～长、～而复始。A circle; a circuit. 圆周 a circumference; 周而复始 to go round and begin again.
  • ②普遍；全面。～身、众所～知、～游列国。All over; general. 周身 all over the body; 众所周知 as everyone knows.
  • ③完备；细密。～到、～密、计划不～。Thorough; careful. 周到 attentive; 计划不周 the plan is not thought through.
  • ④星期。上～、～末、～刊。A week. 上周 last week; 周末 the weekend.
  • ⑤满一年。～年、～岁。A full year. 周年 an anniversary; 周岁 a full year of age.
  • ⑥接济。～济。To help out. 周济 to give relief to.
  • ⑦朝代名；姓。The Zhou dynasty; a surname.
