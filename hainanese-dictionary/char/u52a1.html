字义解释

mu⁵ 同音字 5

wù

  • ①事情。事～、任～、公～、家～。Affairs. 事务 business; 任务 a task; 公务 official duties; 家务 household chores.
  • ②从事；致力。～农、～实、不～正业。To engage in; to devote oneself to. 务农 to farm; 务实 to be practical; 不务正业 to neglect one's proper work.
  • ③副词，必须；一定。～必、～须、除恶～尽。Must; be sure to. 务必 without fail; 务须 must; 除恶务尽 evil must be rooted out entirely.
