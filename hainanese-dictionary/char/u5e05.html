字义解释

suai⁴ 同音字 3

shuài

  • ①军队中最高的指挥官。元～、统～、主～。A commander-in-chief. 元帅 a marshal; 统帅 the supreme commander.
  • ②统率；带领。～师、～众。To command; lead. 帅师 to lead an army; 帅众 to head a body of men.
  • ③方漂亮；英俊。字写得真～。dial. Handsome; smart. 字写得真帅 the writing looks splendid.
