字义解释

ziu⁵

jiù

  • ①凑近；靠近。迁～、～着灯光看书。To move close to. 迁就 to accommodate oneself to; 就着灯光看书 to read by lamplight.
  • ②到；开始从事。～位、～业、～职。To take up. 就位 to take one's place; 就业 to find employment.
  • ③完成；确定。造～、成～、～绪。To accomplish. 造就 to bring up; 成就 an achievement; 就绪 to be in order.
  • ④趁着。～近、～便、～手。Taking the opportunity. 就近 nearby; 就便 while one is at it.
  • ⑤表示在很短时间以内。他～来、这～办。Marks a very short time. 他就来 he will be here in a moment.
  • ⑥表示前后事情紧接着。说完～走、一学～会。Marks one thing following straight on another. 说完就走 to leave as soon as one has spoken.
  • ⑦表示肯定语气。他～是不听、我～不信。Marks emphatic affirmation. 他就是不听 he simply will not listen.
  • ⑧表示假设的让步。你～不说，我也知道。Marks a concessive supposition. 你就不说，我也知道 even if you say nothing, I know.
