字义解释

diang¹ 同音字 9

shuāng

  • ①两个；一对；跟"单"相对。～方、～手、～管齐下。Two; a pair; the opposite of 单. 双方 both sides; 双手 both hands; 双管齐下 to work on two fronts at once.
  • ②量词，用于成对的东西。一～鞋、两～筷子。Measure word for things in pairs. 一双鞋 a pair of shoes; 两双筷子 two pairs of chopsticks.
  • ③偶数的。～数、～号、～日。Even-numbered. 双数 an even number; 双号 even numbers; 双日 even-numbered days.
  • ④加倍的。～份、～料。Double. 双份 a double portion; 双料 double-strength.
