字义解释

ddai⁵ 同音字 3

dài

  • 〔dài〕
  • ①对待；招待。～人接物、优～、款～。To treat; entertain. 待人接物 the way one deals with people; 优待 to give preferential treatment.
  • ②等；等候。～业、等～、～机而动。To wait for. 待业 to await employment; 待机而动 to bide one's time.
  • ③将要；打算。正～出门。To be about to. 正待出门 just about to go out.
  • 〔dāi〕
  • ④停留。～一会儿、在家～着。To stay. 待一会儿 to stay a while; 在家待着 to stop at home.
