字义解释

pid⁷ 同音字 3

pǐ

  • ①量词，用于马、骡等和整卷的绸布。三～马、一～布。Measure word for horses and mules and for bolts of cloth. 三匹马 three horses; 一匹布 a bolt of cloth.
  • ②相当；比得上。～敌、～配。To match; to be equal to. 匹敌 to be a match for; 匹配 to pair.
  • ③单独。～马单枪。Single; alone. 匹马单枪 single-handed.
