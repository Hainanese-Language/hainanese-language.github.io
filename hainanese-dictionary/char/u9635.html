字义解释

zin⁵ 同音字 4

zhèn

用于第 ❶ 义

  • ①军队作战时布置的队伍。～势、～容、严～以待。Troops drawn up for battle. 阵势 a battle array; 阵容 a line-up; 严阵以待 to stand ready in full battle array.
  • ②战场。～地、上～、临～脱逃。The battlefield. 阵地 a position; 上阵 to go into battle; 临阵脱逃 to desert on the eve of battle.
  • ③一段时间。这～子、～发。A spell of time. 这阵子 these days; 阵发 occurring in bouts.
  • ④量词，用于持续一段时间的事物。一～风、一～笑声、下了一～雨。Measure word for things lasting a while. 一阵风 a gust of wind; 一阵笑声 a burst of laughter; 下了一阵雨 there was a shower.
