字义解释

hhan⁴

hàn

  • ①汉族，我国人数最多的民族。～人、～语、～字。The Han, the most numerous of China's peoples. 汉人 a Han Chinese; 汉语 the Chinese language; 汉字 Chinese characters.
  • ②朝代名。～代、～朝。The name of a dynasty. 汉代 the Han period; 汉朝 the Han dynasty.
  • ③男子。老～、好～、彪形大～。A man. 老汉 an old man; 好汉 a brave fellow; 彪形大汉 a burly fellow.
  • ④银河。星～、河～。The Milky Way. 星汉 the starry river; 河汉 the Milky Way.
