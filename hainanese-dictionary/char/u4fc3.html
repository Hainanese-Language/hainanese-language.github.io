字义解释

sog⁷ 同音字 14

cù

  • ①时间短。短～、急～。Short in time. 短促 brief; 急促 hurried.
  • ②催；推动。催～、～进、～成、督～。To urge on. 催促 to press; 促进 to advance; 促成 to bring about; 督促 to supervise and urge.
  • ③靠近。～膝谈心。To draw close. 促膝谈心 to sit close and talk heart to heart.
