字义解释

hhou⁵ 同音字 4

hòu

  • ①等待。等～、～车、～诊、静～佳音。To wait. 等候 to await; 候车 to wait for a bus; 候诊 to wait to see a doctor; 静候佳音 to await good news.
  • ②问好。问～、致～。To send regards. 问候 to send greetings; 致候 to convey regards.
  • ③时节。时～、气～、季～。A season. 时候 the time; 气候 climate; 季候 the seasons.
  • ④情况；状态。火～、症～、征～。A condition; a state. 火候 the right heat or moment; 症候 symptoms; 征候 a sign.
