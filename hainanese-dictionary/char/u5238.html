字义解释

hin⁶ 同音字 2

quàn

  • 〔quàn〕
  • ①作为凭证的票据。入场～、国库～、优待～。A ticket or certificate serving as proof. 入场券 an admission ticket; 国库券 a treasury bond; 优待券 a discount voucher.
  • 〔xuàn〕
  • ②门窗、桥梁上做成弧形的部分。～门、拱～。The arched part of a doorway, window or bridge. 券门 an arched gateway; 拱券 an arch.
