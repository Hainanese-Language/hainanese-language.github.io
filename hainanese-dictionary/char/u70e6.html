字义解释

pan² 同音字 9

fán

  • ①苦闷；急躁。心～、～躁、～闷。Vexed; agitated. 心烦 troubled; 烦躁 fretful; 烦闷 depressed.
  • ②厌烦。腻～、不耐～。To be sick of. 腻烦 fed up; 不耐烦 impatient.
  • ③又多又乱。～琐、～杂、要言不～。Overabundant and confused. 烦琐 tediously detailed; 要言不烦 concise and to the point.
  • ④敬辞，麻烦。～您带个信。Polite word for troubling someone. 烦您带个信 could I trouble you to pass on a message.
