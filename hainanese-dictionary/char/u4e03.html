字义解释

sid⁷ 同音字 16

qī

  • ①数目，六加一所得。～上八下、～嘴八舌。The number seven; six plus one. 七上八下 in a flutter of nerves.
  • ②旧俗人死后每七天祭奠一次，共七次。头～、断～。By former custom, a memorial rite held every seventh day after a death, seven times in all. 头七 the first seventh day.
