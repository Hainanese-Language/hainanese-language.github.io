字义解释

heng² 同音字 15

qīng

  • ①歪；斜。～斜、～听、身子向前～。To lean; to tilt. 倾斜 to slant; 倾听 to listen attentively; 身子向前倾 to lean forward.
  • ②使器物歪斜，让里面的东西出来。～盆大雨、～箱倒箧。To tip a vessel so that its contents come out. 倾盆大雨 a downpour; 倾箱倒箧 to empty out every box and case.
  • ③全部拿出。～诉、～吐、～囊相助、～巢出动。To pour out everything one has. 倾诉 to pour out one's heart; 倾吐 to unburden oneself; 倾囊相助 to give all one has to help; 倾巢出动 to turn out in full force.
  • ④压倒；胜过。～轧、～国～城。To overwhelm; to surpass. 倾轧 to engage in infighting; 倾国倾城 of surpassing beauty.
  • ⑤倒塌。～覆、大厦将～。To collapse. 倾覆 to overturn; 大厦将倾 the great house is about to fall.
