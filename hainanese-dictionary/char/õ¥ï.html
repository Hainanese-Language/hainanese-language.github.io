字义解释

loi¹

lì

  • ①可以作依据的事物；同类中的一个。～子、举～、事～、～题。Something to go by; one instance of a kind. 例子 an example; 举例 to cite an instance; 事例 a case; 例题 a worked example.
  • ②从前有过、后来可以仿效的事情。惯～、援～、破～。A past occurrence that may be followed. 惯例 established practice; 援例 to cite a precedent; 破例 to make an exception.
  • ③规则；按规定进行的。条～、～会、～行公事。A rule; done as a matter of rule. 条例 regulations; 例会 a regular meeting; 例行公事 a routine formality.
  • ④调查或统计中的一个个案。病～、～数。A single case in a survey or count. 病例 a case of illness; 例数 the number of cases.
