字义解释

liao³ 同音字 2

liǎo

  • 〔liǎo〕
  • ①完毕；结束。～结、没完没～、不～了之。To finish; to settle. 了结 to bring to a close; 没完没了 endless.
  • ②明白；懂得。～解、一目～然、～如指掌。To understand; to grasp. 了解 to know; 一目了然 clear at a glance.
  • ③用在动词后，跟"得""不"连用，表示可能。做得～、受不～。After a verb, with 得 or 不, marking possibility. 做得了 able to do it; 受不了 unable to bear it.
  • 〔le〕
  • ④助词，用在动词、形容词后，表示动作或变化已完成。写完～、天黑～。Particle after a verb or adjective, marking a completed action or change. 写完了 have finished writing; 天黑了 it has grown dark.
  • ⑤助词，用在句末，表示出现新情况。下雨～、他不来～。Particle at the end of a sentence, marking a new situation. 下雨了 it has started raining; 他不来了 he is not coming after all.
