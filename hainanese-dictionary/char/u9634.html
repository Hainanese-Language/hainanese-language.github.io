字义解释

im¹ 同音字 4

yīn

  • ①天空有云，不见阳光。～天、～云、天～了。Overcast, with the sun hidden. 阴天 a cloudy day; 阴云 dark clouds; 天阴了 the sky has clouded over.
  • ②山的北面；水的南面，多用于地名。华～、江～。The north side of a hill or the south bank of a river, mostly in place names. 华阴 Huayin; 江阴 Jiangyin.
  • ③不见阳光的地方。树～、背～。A place out of the sun. 树阴 the shade of a tree; 背阴 shaded from the sun.
  • ④凹进的。～文。Sunken; incised. 阴文 characters cut in intaglio.
  • ⑤暗中的；不光明的。～谋、～险、～沟。Covert; underhand. 阴谋 a plot; 阴险 sinister; 阴沟 a covered drain.
  • ⑥带负电的。～极、～离子。Negatively charged. 阴极 a cathode; 阴离子 an anion.
  • ⑦指人死后的世界。～间、～曹地府。The world of the dead. 阴间 the nether world; 阴曹地府 the realm of the dead.
  • ⑧我国古代哲学中跟"阳"相对的一面。～阳、～盛阳衰。In Chinese philosophy, the counterpart of 阳. 阴阳 yin and yang; 阴盛阳衰 yin waxing while yang wanes.
