字义解释

zi⁴ 同音字 25

zhì

  • ①意志；志向。～气、立～、～同道合。Will; aspiration. 志气 ambition; 立志 to set one's mind on; 志同道合 of like mind and purpose.
  • ②记；记载。～喜、永～不忘。To record. 志喜 to mark a happy occasion; 永志不忘 never to be forgotten.
  • ③记载的文字。杂～、县～、墓～。A written record. 杂志 a magazine; 县志 a county gazetteer; 墓志 an epitaph.
  • ④记号。标～。A mark. 标志 a sign.
