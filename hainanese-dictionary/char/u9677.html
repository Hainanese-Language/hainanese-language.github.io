字义解释

hham⁵ 同音字 4

xiàn

  • ①掉进；坠入。～入泥里、～进去。To sink into. 陷入泥里 to sink into the mud; 陷进去 to fall in.
  • ②凹进。两眼深～、地基下～。To become sunken. 两眼深陷 with deep-set eyes; 地基下陷 the foundations have subsided.
  • ③设计害人。～害、诬～。To frame someone. 陷害 to entrap; 诬陷 to frame on a false charge.
  • ④被攻破。失～、～落、城～。To fall to an attacker. 失陷 to fall; 陷落 to be captured; 城陷 the city fell.
  • ⑤缺点。缺～。A defect. 缺陷 a flaw.
