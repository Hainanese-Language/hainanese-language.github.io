字义解释

ge⁶ 同音字 6

gé

  • ①遮断；阻隔。～开、～离、～音、～岸观火。To separate; to keep apart. 隔开 to partition off; 隔离 to isolate; 隔音 soundproof; 隔岸观火 to watch a fire from the other bank.
  • ②相距；间隔。相～、～三天、～壁。To be apart by a given distance or interval. 相隔 to be separated by; 隔三天 every third day; 隔壁 next door.
