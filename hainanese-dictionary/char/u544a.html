字义解释

go⁴ 同音字 3

gào

  • ①说给别人；通知。～诉、报～、转～。To tell; inform. 告诉 to tell; 报告 to report; 转告 to pass word on.
  • ②向司法机关检举、控诉。控～、～状。To bring a charge before the law. 控告 to accuse; 告状 to file a suit.
  • ③请求。～假、～饶。To ask for. 告假 to ask for leave; 告饶 to beg for mercy.
  • ④表明。～辞、自～奋勇。To declare. 告辞 to take one's leave; 自告奋勇 to volunteer.
  • ⑤宣布某种情况的实现。～成、～急、大功～成。To announce that something has come about. 告成 to be completed; 告急 to report an emergency.
