字义解释

lai² 同音字 5

lái

  • ①从别处到说话人所在的地方；到达。～客、～信、快～！To come; to arrive. 来客 a guest arrives; 来信 a letter arrives; 快来! Come quickly!
  • ②（消息、事情等）来到；发生。～电话、～电、麻烦～了。(Of news, calls, matters, etc.) To arrive; to happen. 来电话 to receive a phone call; 来电 an incoming message or call; 麻烦来了 trouble has come.
  • ③代替某些动词，表示做、取、吃等。～一杯、～一盘棋、～一碗面。Used in place of a specific verb: to do, to take, to have, etc. 来一杯 to have a drink; 来一盘棋 to play a game of chess; 来一碗面 to have a bowl of noodles.
  • ④将来的；以后的。～年、～日、～世。Future; coming. 来年 next year; 来日 future days; 来世 a future life.
  • ⑤多用于"从来、向来、以来"等，表示从过去到现在。从～、向～、以～。Used in 从来, 向来, 以来, etc., meaning from the past until now. 从来 always; 向来 all along; 以来 since.
  • ⑥招致；引起。招～、惹～麻烦、引～注意。To incur; to invite; to attract. 招来 to invite or attract; 惹来麻烦 to invite trouble; 引起注意 to attract attention.
  • ⑦用在动词后，表示趋向、结果或可能。拿～、起～、想～、做不～。After a verb, marking direction, result or possibility. 拿来 to bring; 起来 to rise; 想来 to suppose; 做不来 to be unable to do it.
  • ⑧用在数词或量词后，表示约数。十～个人、三十～岁、两米～高。After a numeral or measure word, marking approximation. 十来个人 about ten people; 三十来岁 around thirty years old; 两米来高 about two metres high.
  • ⑨用在"一""二"等后面，列举原因。一～路远，二～天冷。After 一, 二, etc., listing reasons: 一来…, 二来…. 一来路远，二来天冷 first, it is far; second, it is cold.
