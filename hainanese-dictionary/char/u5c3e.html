wěi

  • 〔wěi〕
  • ①尾巴。～端、摇头摆～、虎头蛇～。A tail. 尾端 the tail end; 虎头蛇尾 a fine start and a poor finish.
  • ②末端；最后的部分。～声、结～、排～。The end; the last part. 尾声 a coda; 结尾 the conclusion; 排尾 the rear of a line.
  • ③主要部分以外的部分。～数、扫～。The part left over. 尾数 the odd figures; 扫尾 to wind up.
  • ④量词，用于鱼。一～鱼。Measure word for fish. 一尾鱼 one fish.
  • 〔yǐ〕
  • ⑤用于"马尾儿"等词，指马尾上的毛。Used in words such as 马尾儿, the hair of a horse's tail.
