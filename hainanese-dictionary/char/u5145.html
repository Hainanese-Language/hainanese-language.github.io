字义解释

song¹ 同音字 14

chōng

  • ①满；足。～满、～分、～足、～沛。Full; ample. 充满 to be filled with; 充分 ample; 充足 sufficient; 充沛 abundant.
  • ②装满；填满。～电、～气、～实。To fill up; to charge. 充电 to charge a battery; 充气 to inflate; 充实 to enrich.
  • ③担任；当。～任、～当、～军。To serve as. 充任 to fill a post; 充当 to act as; 充军 to be banished to military service.
  • ④假装。～内行、冒～。To pass oneself off as. 充内行 to pose as an expert; 冒充 to pretend to be.
