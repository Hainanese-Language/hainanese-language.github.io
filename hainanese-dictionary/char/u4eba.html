字义解释

nang² 同音字 2

rén

  • ①能制造和使用工具进行劳动的高等动物。～类、男～、～口。A human being, the higher animal that makes and uses tools. 人类 humankind; 男人 a man; 人口 population.
  • ②每人；一般人。～手一册、～所共知。Each person; people in general. 人手一册 a copy for everyone; 人所共知 as everyone knows.
  • ③指某种人。工～、军～、主～、诗～。A person of a particular kind. 工人 a worker; 军人 a serviceman; 主人 a host; 诗人 a poet.
  • ④别人。待～诚恳、助～为乐。Others. 待人诚恳 to treat others sincerely; 助人为乐 to take pleasure in helping others.
  • ⑤人的品性或名声。为～、～品、丢～。A person's character or good name. 为人 the way one conducts oneself; 人品 character; 丢人 to lose face.
  • ⑥指人的身体状况。这几天～不大舒服。Referring to one's physical condition. 这几天人不大舒服 I've not been feeling well these few days.
