字义解释

ngi⁵ 同音字 3

yì

  • ①公正合宜的道理或行为。正～、道～、见～勇为。What is just and right; righteous conduct. 正义 justice; 道义 morality; 见义勇为 to act boldly for a just cause.
  • ②情谊。情～、忘恩负～。Bonds of affection; loyalty. 情义 friendly feeling; 忘恩负义 ungrateful and disloyal.
  • ③意思；意义。字～、含～、望文生～。Meaning; sense. 字义 the sense of a word; 含义 implication; 望文生义 to take a text at face value.
  • ④因拜认或抚养而结成的亲属关系。～父、～子。Of kinship formed by adoption or sworn bond. 义父 adoptive father; 义子 adopted son.
  • ⑤人工制造的人体部分。～齿、～肢。Artificial, of a body part. 义齿 false teeth; 义肢 an artificial limb.
  • ⑥姓。A surname.
