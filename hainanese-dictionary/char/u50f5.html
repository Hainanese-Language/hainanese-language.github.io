字义解释

giang¹ 同音字 9

jiāng

  • ①僵硬；不能活动。～直、冻～了、～尸。Stiff; unable to move. 僵直 rigid; 冻僵了 numb with cold; 僵尸 a corpse.
  • ②事情难于转圜。～局、～持、闹～了。Of a situation, impossible to ease. 僵局 a deadlock; 僵持 to be at a stalemate; 闹僵了 to have reached breaking point.
