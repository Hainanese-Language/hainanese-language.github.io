字义解释

dog⁸ 同音字 5

sú

  • ①社会上长期形成的风气、习惯。风～、习～、～话、移风易～。Custom; a long-established social practice. 风俗 customs; 习俗 usage; 俗话 a common saying; 移风易俗 to change prevailing habits.
  • ②大众的；通行的。通～、～称、～语。Popular; in common use. 通俗 popular; 俗称 the common name; 俗语 a common expression.
  • ③趣味不高；平庸。庸～、～气、～不可耐。In poor taste; commonplace. 庸俗 vulgar; 俗气 tasteless; 俗不可耐 unbearably vulgar.
  • ④指没有出家的人；跟"僧"相对。～人、还～、僧～。A layman; the opposite of 僧. 俗人 a layman; 还俗 to return to lay life; 僧俗 monks and laymen.
