yi² 同音字 44

rú

  • ①儒家，先秦以孔子为宗师的学派。～学、～术、～家。The Confucian school, which looked to Confucius as its master. 儒学 Confucian learning; 儒术 Confucian doctrine; 儒家 the Confucians.
  • ②旧时指读书人。～生、～医、腐～。In former times, a scholar. 儒生 a Confucian scholar; 儒医 a scholar-physician; 腐儒 a pedant.
