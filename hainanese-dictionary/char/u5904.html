字义解释

dde⁴

chù

  • ①地方。～所、住～、到～。A place. 处所 a location; 住处 a dwelling; 到处 everywhere.
  • ②机关，或机关中的部门。办事～、教务～。An office, or a department within one. 办事处 an agency; 教务处 the academic affairs office.

si³ 同音字 8

chǔ

  • ①居住；存在。穴居野～、～世。To dwell; live. 穴居野处 to live in caves and the wild; 处世 to conduct oneself in society.
  • ②交往；相待。～得来、相～融洽。To get on with. 处得来 to get along; 相处融洽 on good terms.
  • ③办理；决定。～理、～置。To handle; decide. 处理 to deal with; 处置 to dispose of.
  • ④惩罚。～罚、～分、～决。To punish. 处罚 to penalise; 处分 to discipline; 处决 to execute.
