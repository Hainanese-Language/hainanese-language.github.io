字义解释

ba² 同音字 2

bā

  • ①盼望；急切地希望。～望、～不得。To long for. 巴望 to hope earnestly; 巴不得 to be dying for.
  • ②紧贴。爬山虎～在墙上。To cling to. 爬山虎巴在墙上 the ivy clings to the wall.
  • ③粘住；粘结的东西。锅～、～锅。To stick; a crust. 锅巴 crisp rice at the bottom of the pot; 巴锅 to stick to the pan.
  • ④挨着；靠近。前不～村，后不～店。To be close to. 前不巴村，后不巴店 with no village ahead and no inn behind.
  • ⑤指四川东部一带。～蜀。Eastern Sichuan. 巴蜀 the Ba and Shu region.
