字义解释

iu⁴俗读 同音字 2

xì

  • ①条状物横剖面小；跟"粗"相对。～线、～沙、～腰。Thin; slender — the opposite of 粗. 细线 fine thread; 细沙 fine sand; 细腰 a slim waist.
  • ②颗粒小。～盐、～面。Fine-grained. 细盐 fine salt; 细面 fine flour.
  • ③音量小。嗓音～、～声～气。(of sound) Soft; thin. 嗓音细 a thin voice; 细声细气 in a small soft voice.
  • ④精致。～瓷、精～、～工。Finely made. 细瓷 fine porcelain; 精细 exquisite; 细工 fine workmanship.
  • ⑤仔细；周密。～心、～看、～致。Careful; thorough. 细心 attentive; 细看 to look closely; 细致 meticulous.
  • ⑥微小的；不重要的。～节、～枝末节、事无巨～。Minor; trifling. 细节 a detail; 细枝末节 minor matters; 事无巨细 no matter how large or small.
