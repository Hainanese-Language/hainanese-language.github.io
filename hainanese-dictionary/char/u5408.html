字义解释

hhab⁸ 同音字 3

hé

  • ①闭；合拢。～眼、～不拢嘴、悲欢离～。To close; to shut. 合眼 to close one's eyes; 悲欢离合 joys and sorrows, partings and reunions.
  • ②聚集；凑到一起。汇～、集～、～围。To gather; to converge. 汇合 to converge; 集合 to assemble.
  • ③共同；一起。～作、～唱、～伙。Together; jointly. 合作 to cooperate; 合唱 chorus; 合伙 to form a partnership.
  • ④符合；不违背。～适、～理、～法、情投意～。To conform; to suit. 合适 suitable; 合理 reasonable; 合法 legal; 情投意合 to hit it off.
  • ⑤全；整个。～家、～村。Whole; entire. 合家 the whole family; 合村 the whole village.
  • ⑥计算；折合。～计、一美元～多少人民币。To calculate; to convert. 合计 to total up.
  • ⑦应当；应该。～该、～当。Ought to; should. 合该 it is only right.
  • ⑧量词，旧时指交战的次数。大战三十～。Measure word for rounds in combat 〈former〉. 大战三十合 fought thirty rounds.
  • ⑨姓。A surname.

gab⁷ 同音字 5

gě

  • ①量词，市制容量单位，一升的十分之一。A unit of dry measure, one-tenth of a sheng (升).
