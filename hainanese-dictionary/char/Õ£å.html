字义解释

i² 同音字 2

yuán

  • ①从中心到周边各点距离相等的形。～圈、～形、～桌。Round; a circle. 圆圈 a ring; 圆形 circular; 圆桌 a round table.
  • ②完备；周全。～满、～熟、话说得不～。Complete; well rounded. 圆满 satisfactory; 话说得不圆 the account doesn't hold together.
  • ③使周全。～场、～谎。To make good; smooth over. 圆场 to mediate; 圆谎 to patch up a lie.
  • ④货币单位；也指圆形的硬币。银～、铜～。A unit of currency; also a round coin. 银圆 a silver dollar; 铜圆 a copper coin.
