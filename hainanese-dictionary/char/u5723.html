字义解释

deng⁴

shèng

  • ①品德最高尚、智慧最高超的。～人、～贤、神～。Of the highest virtue and wisdom. 圣人 a sage; 圣贤 sages and worthies; 神圣 sacred.
  • ②称在学问或技艺上有极高成就的人。诗～、棋～。One who has reached the summit of a field. 诗圣 the sage of poetry; 棋圣 a master of chess.
  • ③旧时称帝王的。～旨、～上。Belonging to the emperor, in former times. 圣旨 an imperial edict; 圣上 His Majesty.
  • ④宗教徒尊称跟教主有关的事物。～经、～诞、～地。Applied by believers to things connected with the founder of their faith. 圣经 the Bible; 圣诞 the Nativity; 圣地 a holy place.
