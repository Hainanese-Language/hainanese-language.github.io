字义解释

hhue³ 同音字 3

huǒ

  • ①物体燃烧时发出的光和焰。～光、生～、～灾。Fire. 火光 the light of flames; 生火 to light a fire; 火灾 a blaze.
  • ②中医指引起发炎、烦躁等的病因。上～、败～、～气大。In Chinese medicine, internal heat causing inflammation or irritability. 上火 to get inflamed; 火气大 hot-tempered.
  • ③发怒。～冒三丈、发～、～性。To lose one's temper. 火冒三丈 to flare up; 发火 to get angry.
  • ④兴旺；热闹。生意很～、～爆。Thriving; lively. 生意很火 business is booming; 火爆 roaring.
  • ⑤武器弹药。～器、军～、～力。Arms and ammunition. 火器 firearms; 军火 munitions; 火力 firepower.
  • ⑥紧急。～急、～速。Urgent. 火急 pressing; 火速 at top speed.
