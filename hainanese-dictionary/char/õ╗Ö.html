字义解释

din¹ 同音字 15

xiān

  • ①神话中长生不老、有神通的人。～人、神～、～境。An immortal of legend, deathless and possessed of magic powers. 仙人 an immortal; 神仙 a celestial being; 仙境 a fairyland.
  • ②称某方面才能出众的人。诗～、酒～。One outstanding in some field. 诗仙 a poetic genius; 酒仙 a great drinker.
