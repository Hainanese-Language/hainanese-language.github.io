字义解释

giam¹ 同音字 2

jiān

  • ①同时涉及或具有几种事物。～职、～任、～顾、德才～备。To hold or cover several things at once. 兼职 to hold concurrent posts; 兼任 to serve concurrently; 兼顾 to attend to both; 德才兼备 to have both integrity and ability.
  • ②加倍的。～程前进。Double. 兼程前进 to travel at double speed.
