字义解释

hhio¹ 同音字 2

xiāng

  • ①农村；跟"城"相对。～下、城～、～村。The countryside; the opposite of 城. 乡下 in the country; 城乡 town and country; 乡村 a village.
  • ②家乡。故～、～亲、背井离～。One's native place. 故乡 one's home town; 乡亲 a fellow villager; 背井离乡 to leave one's home behind.
  • ③县以下的行政区划单位。～长、～政府。A township, the administrative unit below a county. 乡长 a township head.
