字义解释

hi⁴ 同音字 8

qì

  • ①用具。～皿、～具、武～。An implement; utensil. 器皿 vessels; 器具 tools; 武器 a weapon.
  • ②生物体中有特定功能的部分。～官、脏～。An organ of a living body. 器官 an organ; 脏器 the internal organs.
  • ③度量；才能。～量、大～晚成。Capacity; talent. 器量 breadth of mind; 大器晚成 great talents mature late.
  • ④看重。～重。To think highly of. 器重 to hold in esteem.
