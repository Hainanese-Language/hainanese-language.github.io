字义解释

di¹ 同音字 6

shī

  • ①实行；施展。实～、～工、～加。To carry out; apply. 实施 to implement; 施工 to build; 施加 to bring to bear.
  • ②给予。～礼、～压、～恩。To bestow. 施礼 to salute; 施压 to apply pressure; 施恩 to show favour.
  • ③施舍。～舍、布～。To give in charity. 施舍 to give alms; 布施 to make offerings.
  • ④加上；用上。～肥、～粉。To apply to. 施肥 to fertilise; 施粉 to apply powder.
  • ⑤姓。A surname.
