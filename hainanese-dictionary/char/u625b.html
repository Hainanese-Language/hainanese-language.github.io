hang² 同音字 10

káng

  • ①用肩膀承担。～枪、～行李、～麻袋。To carry on the shoulder. 扛枪 to carry a rifle; 扛麻袋 to carry a sack. cTo bear; to endure. 扛得住 can stand it; 扛不住 cannot stand it.
  • ②承受；忍受。～得住、～不住。
  • ③〈旧〉受雇做工。～活。〈former〉 To work as a hired labourer. 扛活 to do hired labour.

  • ②〈方〉抬东西。<dial.〉To lift or carry something.
