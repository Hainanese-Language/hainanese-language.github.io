字义解释

dan³ 同音字 3

chǎn

  • ①（人或动物）生育。～子、～卵、助～。To give birth. 产子 to bear a child; 产卵 to lay eggs; 助产 midwifery.
  • ②制造；生长出。生～、出～、～量。To produce; to yield. 生产 to produce; 产量 output.
  • ③出产的东西。土～、水～、特～。Produce; products. 土产 local produce; 特产 a speciality.
  • ④财物。财～、房～、家～。Property. 财产 property; 房产 real estate; 家产 family possessions.
