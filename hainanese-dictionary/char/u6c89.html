字义解释

sim² 同音字 8

chén

  • ①沉没；向下落；跟"浮"相对。下～、～船、石～大海。To sink; the opposite of 浮. 下沉 to go down; 沉船 a sunken ship; 石沉大海 to sink like a stone and never be heard of again.
  • ②使降落；放下。～住气、～下脸来。To let sink; to lower. 沉住气 to keep calm; 沉下脸来 to pull a long face.
  • ③分量重。这箱子很～、～甸甸。Heavy. 这箱子很沉 this case is heavy; 沉甸甸 weighing heavily.
  • ④程度深。～醉、～睡、～思。Deep in degree. 沉醉 to be intoxicated; 沉睡 to sleep soundly; 沉思 to be lost in thought.
