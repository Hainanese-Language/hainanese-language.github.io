字义解释

ban⁵ 同音字 2

bàn

  • ①做；处理。～事、～公、～手续、照～。To do; to handle. 办事 to attend to business; 办公 to work in an office; 办手续 to go through the formalities; 照办 to act accordingly.
  • ②创设；经营。～学、创～、～工厂。To found; to run. 办学 to run a school; 创办 to establish; 办工厂 to set up a factory.
  • ③采购；置备。～货、～年货。To buy in; to lay in. 办货 to purchase goods; 办年货 to shop for the New Year.
  • ④惩治。惩～、法～、查～。To punish. 惩办 to penalize; 法办 to deal with according to law; 查办 to investigate and punish.
