ddiab⁸ 同音字 4

dié

  • ①重复；一层加一层。重～、层～、～韵。To repeat; to pile one layer on another. 重叠 overlapping; 层叠 layer upon layer; 叠韵 rhyming syllables.
  • ②把东西折起来。～衣服、～被子、～纸。To fold. 叠衣服 to fold clothes; 叠被子 to fold up bedding; 叠纸 to fold paper.
