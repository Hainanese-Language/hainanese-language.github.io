字义解释

hham² 同音字 5

hán

  • ①东西放在嘴里，不咽下也不吐出。～着糖、～在嘴里。To hold in the mouth without swallowing or spitting out. 含着糖 with a sweet in the mouth.
  • ②里面存在着某种成分。～水量、～有杂质。To contain a certain element. 含水量 water content; 含有杂质 containing impurities.
  • ③带着某种情感或意思，不完全表露。～怒、～笑、～情脉脉。To harbour a feeling without fully showing it. 含怒 with suppressed anger; 含情脉脉 with tender feeling in one's eyes.
