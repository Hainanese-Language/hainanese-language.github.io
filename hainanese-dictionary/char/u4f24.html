字义解释

diang¹ 同音字 9

shāng

  • ①人体或物体受损坏的地方。～口、刀～、创～。The damaged place on a body or object. 伤口 a wound; 刀伤 a knife cut; 创伤 a wound.
  • ②损害。～害、～脑筋、～和气、～筋动骨。To harm. 伤害 to injure; 伤脑筋 to be a headache; 伤和气 to hurt a friendship; 伤筋动骨 to injure sinew and bone.
  • ③因某种外因而致病。～风、～寒、～暑。To fall ill from an outside cause. 伤风 to catch cold; 伤寒 typhoid; 伤暑 heatstroke.
  • ④悲哀。悲～、哀～、～感。Grief. 悲伤 sorrowful; 哀伤 mournful; 伤感 sentimental.
  • ⑤因过度而感到厌烦。吃糖吃～了。To be sick of something through excess. 吃糖吃伤了 to be sick of sweets.
