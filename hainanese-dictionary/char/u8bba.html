字义解释

lun⁵

lùn

  • ①分析说明事理。～述、讨～、评～。To expound; to discuss. 论述 to expound; 讨论 to debate; 评论 to comment on.
  • ②分析说明事理的话或文章。舆～、社～、立～。A discourse; an argument. 舆论 public opinion; 社论 an editorial; 立论 to set out an argument.
  • ③看待；衡量。相提并～、一概而～、～功行赏。To regard; to weigh. 相提并论 to mention in the same breath; 论功行赏 to reward according to merit.
  • ④按照。～件计酬、～斤卖。By; according to. 论件计酬 paid by the piece; 论斤卖 sold by the catty.
  • ⑤用于书名，指《论语》。～语。In the title 论语, the Analects.
