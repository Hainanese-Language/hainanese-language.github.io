字义解释

din⁵ 同音字 4

shàn

  • ①善良；心地好。～心、～意、慈～。Kind; good-hearted. 善心 a kind heart; 慈善 charitable.
  • ②好的行为或事情。行～、～举、隐恶扬～。A good deed. 行善 to do good; 隐恶扬善 to hide faults and publish virtues.
  • ③办好；弄妥。～后、～始～终。To settle properly. 善后 to deal with the aftermath; 善始善终 to see a thing through well.
  • ④擅长。～辩、能歌～舞、多谋～断。To be good at. 善辩 eloquent; 能歌善舞 gifted in song and dance.
  • ⑤容易；常常。～变、～忘。Apt to; often. 善变 changeable; 善忘 forgetful.
