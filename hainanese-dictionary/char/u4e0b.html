字义解释

e⁵

xià

  • ①位置在低处的，跟"上"相对。～面、～游、楼～。Lower in position — the opposite of 上. 下面 below; 下游 lower reaches; 楼下 downstairs.
  • ②等级或品质低的。～等、～策、～品。Lower in grade or quality. 下等 inferior; 下策 a poor plan; 下品 inferior quality.
  • ③次序或时间在后的。～次、～半年、～不为例。Later in order or time. 下次 next time; 下半年 the second half of the year; 下不为例 not to be repeated.
hhia⁵ 同音字 6

xià

  • ①等级或品质低的。～等、～策、～品。Lower in grade or quality. 下等 inferior; 下策 a poor plan; 下品 inferior quality.
  • ②攻克；占领。攻～、连～三城。To capture; to occupy. 攻下 to capture; 连下三城 to take three cities in succession.
  • ③（动物）生产。～蛋、～崽。(Of animals) to give birth; to lay. 下蛋 to lay eggs; 下崽 to give birth.
  • ④用在数量词前，表示少于。不～三百人。Used before a quantity to indicate less than. 不下三百人 no fewer than three hundred people.
lo⁵ 同音字 2

xià

  • ①由高处到低处。～山、～楼、～雨。To go down; to move from a higher place to a lower one. 下山 to descend a hill; 下楼 to go downstairs; 下雨 to rain.
  • ②去；到。～乡、～馆子。To go; to go to. 下乡 to go to the countryside; 下馆子 to eat at a restaurant.
  • ③投入；放进。～种、～面条、～网。To put in; to place into. 下种 to sow; 下面条 to put noodles in to cook; 下网 to cast a net.
  • ④做出（言论、判断等）。～命令、～结论、～定义。To make or issue (a statement, judgment, etc.). 下命令 to give an order; 下结论 to draw a conclusion; 下定义 to define.
  • ⑤卸除；去掉。～货、～枪。To unload; to remove. 下货 to unload goods; 下枪 to take down/remove a gun.
  • ⑥退场；结束。～班、～课、～台。To leave or finish. 下班 to finish work; 下课 for class to end; 下台 to step down.
  • ⑦用在动词后，表示由高到低、有空间或完成。放～、坐得～、写～。Used after a verb to indicate downward movement, available space, or completion. 放下 to put down; 坐得下 to have room to sit; 写下 to write down.
