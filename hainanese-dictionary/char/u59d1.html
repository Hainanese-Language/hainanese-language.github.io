字义解释

gou¹ 同音字 4

gū

  • ①父亲的姐妹。～母、～妈。A father's sister. 姑母 a paternal aunt; 姑妈 auntie.
  • ②丈夫的姐妹。大～子、小～子。A husband's sister. 大姑子 an elder sister-in-law; 小姑子 a younger sister-in-law.
  • ③丈夫的母亲。翁～。A husband's mother. 翁姑 one's husband's parents.
  • ④出家修行的女子。尼～、道～。A nun. 尼姑 a Buddhist nun; 道姑 a Daoist nun.
  • ⑤暂且。～且、～妄言之。For the moment. 姑且 for the time being; 姑妄言之 let me venture a word.
