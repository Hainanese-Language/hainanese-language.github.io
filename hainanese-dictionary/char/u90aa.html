字义解释

dia² 同音字 4

xié

  • ①不正当的；跟"正"相对。～恶、～念、～门歪道。Improper; the opposite of 正. 邪恶 wicked; 邪念 an evil thought; 邪门歪道 crooked ways.
  • ②迷信指鬼神带来的灾祸。中～、驱～。In superstition, harm brought by spirits. 中邪 to be possessed; 驱邪 to drive out evil spirits.
  • ③不正常；奇怪。～乎、一股～劲。Odd; out of the ordinary. 邪乎 uncanny; 一股邪劲 a strange fervour.
  • ④中医指引起疾病的外来因素。风～、寒～。In Chinese medicine, an external factor causing illness. 风邪 pathogenic wind; 寒邪 pathogenic cold.
