字义解释

ghua¹

wài

  • ①外面；跟"内"相对。～边、门～、～表。Outside; the opposite of 内. 外边 outside; 门外 outside the door; 外表 outward appearance.
  • ②不属于自己范围内的。～国、～地、～行。Not within one's own sphere. 外国 a foreign country; 外地 another part of the country; 外行 a layman.
  • ③关系疏远的。见～、内～有别。Distant in relationship. 见外 to treat as an outsider; 内外有别 to distinguish insiders from outsiders.
  • ④指外国。～语、～贸、对～开放。Foreign. 外语 a foreign language; 外贸 foreign trade; 对外开放 opening to the outside world.
  • ⑤母亲、姐妹或女儿方面的亲属。～婆、～甥、～孙。Relatives on the mother's, sister's or daughter's side. 外婆 maternal grandmother; 外甥 a nephew.
  • ⑥另外；以外。此～、～加、～带。Besides; apart from. 此外 in addition; 外加 plus; 外带 as well as.
  • ⑦传统戏曲角色行当，扮演老年男子。A role type in traditional Chinese opera, typically portraying an elderly man.
