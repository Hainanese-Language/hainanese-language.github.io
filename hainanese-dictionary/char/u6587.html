字义解释

vun² 同音字 4

wén

  • ①字；文字。甲骨～、～盲、～字。A character; writing. 甲骨文 oracle bone script; 文盲 illiteracy; 文字 written characters.
  • ②文章；书面语言。作～、散～、～体。A piece of writing. 作文 composition; 散文 prose; 文体 literary form.
  • ③文言。半～半白、～白夹杂。Classical Chinese. 半文半白 half classical and half colloquial.
  • ④指社会科学。～科、～理分科。The humanities. 文科 arts subjects; 文理分科 dividing arts from sciences.
  • ⑤非军事的；跟"武"相对。～职、～官、能～能武。Civil; the opposite of 武. 文职 a civil post; 能文能武 accomplished with pen and sword alike.
  • ⑥柔和；不猛烈。～雅、～火、温～。Gentle; mild. 文雅 refined; 文火 a slow fire; 温文 mild-mannered.
  • ⑦自然界或社会的某些现象。天～、水～、人～。Certain phenomena of nature or society. 天文 astronomy; 水文 hydrology; 人文 the humanities.
  • ⑧掩饰。～过饰非。To gloss over. 文过饰非 to cover up one's faults.
  • ⑨量词，用于旧时的铜钱。一～不值。Measure word for old copper coins. 一文不值 not worth a penny.
  • ⑩姓。A surname.
