字义解释

hi¹ 同音字 9

tiān

  • ①天空。～边、顶～立地。The sky. 天边 the horizon; 顶天立地 of dauntless spirit.
  • ②位置在顶部的；架在空中的。～窗、～桥。Overhead; built up in the air. 天窗 a skylight; 天桥 an overpass.
  • ③一昼夜；白天。今～、三～三夜。A day; daytime. 今天 today; 三天三夜 three days and nights.
  • ④一天里的某段时间。五更～、～不亮。A part of the day. 天不亮 before daybreak.
  • ⑤季节。春～、冷～。A season. 春天 spring; 冷天 cold weather.
  • ⑥天气。阴～、～晴了。The weather. 阴天 an overcast day; 天晴了 it has cleared up.
  • ⑦自然的；生来的。～然、～性、～灾。Natural; inborn. 天然 natural; 天性 one's nature; 天灾 a natural disaster.
  • ⑧迷信指自然界的主宰或神佛所住的地方。～意、～堂。In superstition, the ruler of nature or the abode of gods. 天意 the will of heaven; 天堂 paradise.
