字义解释

se¹ 同音字 26

shī

  • ①传授知识技艺的人。教～、～生、～徒。One who imparts knowledge or a skill. 教师 a teacher; 师徒 master and apprentice.
  • ②掌握专门技术的人。工程～、理发～、厨～。A skilled practitioner. 工程师 an engineer; 厨师 a cook.
  • ③效法；学习。～法、～古。To model oneself on. 师法 to follow the methods of.
  • ④值得效法的榜样。为人～表。An example to be followed. 为人师表 to be a model for others.
  • ⑤军队的编制单位。～长。A division, an army formation. 师长 a division commander.
  • ⑥军队。出～、班～回朝。Troops. 出师 to send out an army; 班师回朝 to return in triumph.
