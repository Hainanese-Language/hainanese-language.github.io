字义解释

ziao³ 同音字 4

jiǎo

  • 讨伐；消灭。～灭、围～、清～。To suppress by force; to wipe out. 剿灭 to exterminate; 围剿 to encircle and annihilate; 清剿 to mop up.
sao¹ 同音字 9

chāo

  • 仅用于"剿说"，指抄袭别人的言论。Only in 剿说: to pass off another's words as one's own.
