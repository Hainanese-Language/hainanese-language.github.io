字义解释

guan¹ 同音字 7

guān

  • ①帽子。衣～、～冕、桂～。A hat. 衣冠 dress and hat; 冠冕 a ceremonial cap; 桂冠 a laurel crown.
  • ②形状像帽子或在顶上的东西。鸡～、树～。Anything hat-shaped or on top. 鸡冠 a cockscomb; 树冠 the crown of a tree.
guan⁶

guàn

  • ①戴帽子。～礼、沐猴而～。To put on a cap. 冠礼 the capping ceremony; 沐猴而冠 a monkey in a hat, an impostor.
  • ②居第一位。～军、名～全球。To come first. 冠军 a champion; 名冠全球 first in the world.
  • ③在前面加上称号。～名、～以恶名。To prefix with a title. 冠名 to name after; 冠以恶名 to fasten an ugly label on.
