字义解释

dou⁴ 同音字 4

zòu

  • ①演奏乐器。～乐、伴～、独～。To play a musical instrument. 奏乐 to strike up music; 独奏 a solo.
  • ②取得；发生。～效、大～奇功。To achieve; to produce. 奏效 to take effect; 大奏奇功 to win a signal success.
  • ③旧时臣子向君主陈述意见或报告事情。～章、启～。Formerly, to memorialise the throne. 奏章 a memorial; 启奏 to report to the emperor.
