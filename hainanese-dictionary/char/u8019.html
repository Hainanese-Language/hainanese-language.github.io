字义解释

be² 同音字 2

bà

  • ①把土块弄碎、平整土地的农具。～子。A harrow; a farm implement used to break up clods and level the soil. 耙子 harrow.
  • ②用耙把土地耙碎、耙平。～地。To harrow; to break up and level the soil with a harrow. 耙地 to harrow the land.
pa² 同音字 5

pá

  • ①聚拢、摊开或翻动谷物、柴草等的农具。～子。A rake; a tool used to gather, spread, or turn grain, straw, and similar materials. 耙子 rake.
  • ②用耙聚拢、摊开或翻动物料。～草、～谷子。To rake; to gather, spread, or turn material with a rake. 耙草 to rake grass; 耙谷子 to rake grain.
