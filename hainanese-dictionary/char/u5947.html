字义解释

hi² 同音字 21

qí

  • ①特殊；罕见。～特、～闻、～花异草。Unusual; rare. 奇特 peculiar; 奇闻 a strange tale; 奇花异草 exotic flowers and plants.
  • ②出人意料的。～袭、～兵、出～制胜。Unexpected. 奇袭 a surprise attack; 出奇制胜 to win by an unexpected move.
  • ③惊异。惊～、不足为～。To marvel at. 惊奇 astonished; 不足为奇 nothing to wonder at.
  • ④表示程度深。～痒、～缺。Extremely. 奇痒 maddeningly itchy; 奇缺 in acute short supply.
gi¹ 同音字 21

jī

  • ①单数的；不成对的。～数、～偶。Odd; unpaired. 奇数 an odd number; 奇偶 odd and even.
  • ②零数。～零。A remainder; odd amount. 奇零 an odd fraction.
