字义解释

hi⁴ 同音字 8

qǐ

  • ①踮起脚跟。～足而待。To stand on tiptoe. 企足而待 to wait on tiptoe.
  • ②盼望；希求。～望、～盼、～求。To hope for; to long for. 企望 to look forward to; 企盼 to await eagerly; 企求 to seek.
  • ③谋划；打算。～图。To scheme; to intend. 企图 to attempt.
  • ④指企业。～管、国～、外～。Short for 企业, an enterprise. 企管 business management; 国企 a state-owned enterprise; 外企 a foreign-invested firm.
