song¹ 同音字 14

chōng

  • ①用开水浇。～茶、～鸡蛋。To pour boiling water on. 冲茶 to make tea; 冲鸡蛋 to beat an egg with hot water.
  • ②水流撞击。～刷、～垮、～毁。To be washed away by a current. 冲刷 to erode; 冲垮 to wash away.
  • ③向前直闯。～锋、～破、横～直撞。To charge forward. 冲锋 to charge; 横冲直撞 to barge about.
  • ④相互抵消。～账、～喜。To offset. 冲账 to balance accounts; 冲喜 to hold a wedding to drive off ill luck.
  • ⑤用药水洗印。～洗、～卷。To develop film. 冲洗 to develop; 冲卷 to process a roll.
song⁴ 同音字 2

chòng

  • ①对着；朝着。～南的窗户、～他点头。Facing; towards. 冲南的窗户 a window facing south.
  • ②凭；根据。～他这股干劲，一定能成功。On the strength of. 冲他这股干劲，一定能成功 with drive like his, he is bound to succeed.
  • ③猛烈；力量大。这药劲儿真～、水流很～。Strong; powerful. 这药劲儿真冲 this medicine is potent; 水流很冲 the current is strong.
