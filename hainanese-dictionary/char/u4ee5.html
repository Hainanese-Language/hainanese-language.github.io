字义解释

yi³ 同音字 10

yǐ

  • ①介词，用；拿。～理服人、～身作则、～卵击石。With; by means of. 以理服人 to convince by reasoning; 以身作则 to set an example oneself; 以卵击石 to throw an egg against a rock.
  • ②介词，因为。不～人废言、～功受赏。Because of. 不以人废言 not to reject a view because of who said it; 以功受赏 to be rewarded for merit.
  • ③介词，表示时间、方位、数量的界限。三日～内、长江～南、五十人～上。Marking a limit of time, place or quantity. 三日以内 within three days; 长江以南 south of the Yangtze; 五十人以上 fifty people or more.
  • ④连词，表示目的或结果。～免误会、～便查考、～致失败。So as to; with the result that. 以免误会 so as to avoid misunderstanding; 以便查考 for ease of reference; 以致失败 resulting in failure.
  • ⑤〈书〉认为。～为、不～为然。〈lit.〉 To consider. 以为 to think; 不以为然 to disapprove.
