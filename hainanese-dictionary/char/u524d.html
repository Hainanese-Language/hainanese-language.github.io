字义解释

dai² 同音字 2

qián

  • ①空间上在正面的；跟"后"相对。～面、～门、～排、往～走。In front; the opposite of 后. 前面 the front; 前门 the front gate; 前排 the front row; 往前走 to walk forward.
  • ②时间上较早的。～天、从～、～所未有、史无～例。Earlier in time. 前天 the day before yesterday; 从前 formerly; 前所未有 unprecedented; 史无前例 without precedent in history.
  • ③次序在先的。～三名、～言。Earlier in order. 前三名 the top three; 前言 a foreword.
  • ④向前行进。勇往直～、畏缩不～。To advance. 勇往直前 to press bravely forward; 畏缩不前 to shrink back.
  • ⑤从前的；已经卸任的。～任、～妻、～校长。Former; no longer holding the post. 前任 a predecessor; 前妻 an ex-wife; 前校长 a former principal.
