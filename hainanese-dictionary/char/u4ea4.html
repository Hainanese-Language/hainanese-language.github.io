字义解释

giao¹ 同音字 9

jiāo

  • ①把东西给有关方面。～货、上～、～作业。To hand over. 交货 to deliver goods; 上交 to turn in.
  • ②到（某一时辰或季节）。～九、～秋。To reach a given hour or season. 交九 the onset of the coldest days; 交秋 the coming of autumn.
  • ③连接；交叉。～界、～点、～错。To join; to cross. 交界 to border on; 交点 a point of intersection.
  • ④结交。～朋友、～往、结～。To make friends with. 交朋友 to make friends; 结交 to associate with.
  • ⑤交情；友谊。深～、绝～、一面之～。Friendship. 深交 close friendship; 一面之交 a passing acquaintance.
  • ⑥性交；交配。～媾、杂～。To mate. 交媾 copulation; 杂交 to crossbreed.
  • ⑦互相。～换、～流、～谈。Mutually. 交换 to exchange; 交流 to interchange.
  • ⑧一齐；同时。风雨～加、饥寒～迫。At the same time. 风雨交加 wind and rain together; 饥寒交迫 beset by hunger and cold.
