字义解释

hhiog⁷ 同音字 3

yù

用于第 ❶ 义

  • ①香气浓厚。馥～、浓～。Richly fragrant. 馥郁 sweet-smelling; 浓郁 heavy with scent.
  • ②茂盛。～～葱葱。Luxuriant. 郁郁葱葱 lush and verdant.
  • ③忧愁烦闷积在心里。忧～、抑～、～闷、～结。Gloom pent up within. 忧郁 melancholy; 抑郁 depressed; 郁闷 low in spirits; 郁结 pent-up feelings.
