字义解释

gi³ 同音字 9

jǔ

  • ①往上托；抬起。～手、～重、高～。To raise; to lift. 举手 to raise one's hand; 举重 weightlifting.
  • ②兴起；发动。～事、～兵、～办。To start; to launch. 举事 to rise in revolt; 举办 to hold an event.
  • ③推选；推荐。推～、选～、～贤。To elect; to recommend. 推举 to nominate; 选举 to elect.
  • ④提出。～例、～证、列～。To put forward. 举例 to give an example; 列举 to enumerate.
  • ⑤行为；动作。～止、～动、壮～。Conduct; a deed. 举止 bearing; 壮举 a heroic act.
  • ⑥全；整个。～国、～世闻名。Whole; entire. 举国 the whole nation; 举世闻名 world-renowned.
