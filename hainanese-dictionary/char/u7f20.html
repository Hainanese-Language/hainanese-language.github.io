字义解释

sin² 同音字 11

chán

  • ①绕。～绕、～线、包～。To wind round. 缠绕 to twine; 缠线 to wind thread; 包缠 to bind up.
  • ②纠缠。～住不放、胡搅蛮～。To pester. 缠住不放 to cling on doggedly; 胡搅蛮缠 to make a nuisance of oneself.
  • ③应付。这人真难～。To deal with. 这人真难缠 that fellow is hard to handle.
