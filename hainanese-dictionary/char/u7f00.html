字义解释

dui⁵ 同音字 4

zhuì

  • ①缝上。～扣子、补～。To sew on. 缀扣子 to sew on a button; 补缀 to mend.
  • ②连接；组合。连～、～合成篇。To link; put together. 连缀 to join up; 缀合成篇 to piece together into a whole.
  • ③装饰。点～、～饰。To adorn. 点缀 to embellish; 缀饰 ornament.
