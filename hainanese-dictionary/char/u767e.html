字义解释

be⁶ 同音字 8

bǎi

  • ①数目，十个十。～分之一。The number one hundred. 百分之一 one per cent.
  • ②表示很多。～货、～科全书、～闻不如一见。Very many; all kinds of. 百货 general merchandise; 百闻不如一见 seeing once is better than hearing a hundred times.
bog⁷ 同音字 23

bó

  • 用于地名"百色"，在广西。Used in the place name 百色 (Bose), in Guangxi.
