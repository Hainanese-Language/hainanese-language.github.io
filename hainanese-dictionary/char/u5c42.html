字义解释

seng² 同音字 14

céng

  • ①重叠；重复。～峦叠嶂、～出不穷。Layered; repeated. 层峦叠嶂 range upon range of hills; 层出不穷 emerging one after another.
  • ②量词，用于重叠或累积的东西。二～楼、一～灰。Measure word for layers and storeys. 二层楼 the first floor; 一层灰 a film of dust.
  • ③事物的阶段或部分。上～、深～、～次。A stage or level of something. 上层 the upper level; 层次 gradation.
