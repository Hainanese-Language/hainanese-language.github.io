字义解释

pu³ 同音字 3

pǔ

  • ①按事物类别或系统编成的表册。年～、家～、食～。A register arranged by category. 年谱 a chronological biography; 家谱 a family tree; 食谱 a cookbook.
  • ②记录音乐符号的本子。乐～、歌～、五线～。A book of musical notation. 乐谱 a score; 五线谱 staff notation.
  • ③编写乐曲。～曲、～写。To set to music. 谱曲 to compose; 谱写 to write music for.
  • ④大致的准则；把握。心里没～、离～。A rough standard; assurance. 心里没谱 to have no idea; 离谱 wide of the mark.
