字义解释

gin⁴ 同音字 9

jiàn

  • ①修筑；建造。～筑、～设、修～、～房子。To build. 建筑 to construct; 建设 to develop; 修建 to erect; 建房子 to build a house.
  • ②成立；创设。～立、～国、组～、～军。To found; to set up. 建立 to establish; 建国 to found a state; 组建 to form; 建军 to build an army.
  • ③提出主张。～议、～言。To put forward a proposal. 建议 to suggest; 建言 to offer counsel.
