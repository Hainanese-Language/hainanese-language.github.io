字义解释

hong³ 同音字 2

tǒng

  • ①事物间连续的关系。系～、传～、血～。A continuous relation between things. 系统 a system; 传统 tradition; 血统 lineage.
  • ②总括；总起来。～计、～称、～筹。To sum up; take as a whole. 统计 statistics; 统称 a collective name; 统筹 overall planning.
  • ③全部；都。～共、～统。Wholly; all. 统共 altogether; 统统 the lot.
  • ④管辖；率领。～治、～率、～领。To govern; command. 统治 to rule; 统率 to command; 统领 to lead.
