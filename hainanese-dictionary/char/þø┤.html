字义解释

ddid⁸

zhí

  • ①不弯曲，跟"曲"相对。～线、笔～、～立。Straight — the opposite of 曲. 直线 a straight line; 笔直 dead straight; 直立 upright.
  • ②使直。～起腰来。To straighten. 直起腰来 to straighten one's back.
  • ③公正的；正义的。正～、理～气壮、是非曲～。Upright; just. 正直 honest; 理直气壮 to speak with the confidence of being right; 是非曲直 rights and wrongs.
  • ④爽快；坦率。～爽、～言、心～口快。Frank; forthright. 直爽 straightforward; 心直口快 outspoken.
  • ⑤一直；径直。～达、～通、～奔目的地。Straight; directly. 直达 through service; 直奔目的地 to head straight for one's destination.
  • ⑥一个劲儿地。～笑、疼得～冒汗。Continuously. 直笑 to laugh and laugh; 疼得直冒汗 sweating with pain.
  • ⑦汉字的笔画，即"竖"。The vertical stroke of a Chinese character.
