字义解释

se³ 同音字 19

cí

  • ①语言中能独立运用的最小单位。名～、～汇、～典。A word, the smallest unit of language that can stand alone. 名词 a noun; 词汇 vocabulary; 词典 a dictionary.
  • ②语句；话语。歌～、台～、义正～严。Words; speech. 歌词 lyrics; 台词 an actor's lines; 义正词严 speaking with justice and force.
  • ③一种韵文形式，兴起于唐，盛行于宋。宋～、～牌、填～。Ci, a verse form arising in the Tang and flourishing in the Song. 宋词 Song lyrics; 词牌 a tune pattern; 填词 to write to a tune pattern.
