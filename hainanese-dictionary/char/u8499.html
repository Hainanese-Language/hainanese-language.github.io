字义解释

mong² 同音字 5

méng

  • 〔méng〕
  • ①遮盖。～住眼睛、～上一块布。To cover over. 蒙住眼睛 to blindfold.
  • ②受到；承受。承～关照、～难。To receive; be granted. 承蒙关照 to be favoured with your care; 蒙难 to meet with disaster.
  • ③愚昧；没有知识。启～、～昧。Ignorant; uneducated. 启蒙 to enlighten; 蒙昧 benighted.
  • ④形容雨点细小。～～细雨。Describing fine drizzle. 蒙蒙细雨 a misty rain.
  • 〔mēng〕
  • ⑤欺骗。～骗、别～人。To deceive. 蒙骗 to cheat.
  • ⑥胡乱猜测。～对了。To make a wild guess. 蒙对了 guessed it right.
  • ⑦昏迷；神志不清。头发～、～头转向。Dizzy; dazed. 蒙头转向 confused and disoriented.
  • 〔měng〕
  • ⑧用于"蒙古"，我国少数民族名。Used in 蒙古 (Mongolia), the name of an ethnic group of China.
