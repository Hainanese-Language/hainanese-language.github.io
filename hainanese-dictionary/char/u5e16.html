字义解释

hiab⁷ 同音字 5

tiè

  • 〔tiè〕
  • ①供临摹用的字画范本。字～、碑～、临～。A model of calligraphy or painting for copying. 字帖 a copybook; 碑帖 a rubbing used as a model; 临帖 to copy from a model.
  • 〔tiě〕
  • ②邀请或通知用的纸片。请～、喜～。A card of invitation or notice. 请帖 an invitation card; 喜帖 a wedding invitation.
  • ③量词，用于配好的中药。一～药。Measure word for doses of Chinese medicine. 一帖药 one dose.
  • 〔tiē〕
  • ④妥当；顺从。妥～、服～。Settled; submissive. 妥帖 appropriate; 服帖 docile.
