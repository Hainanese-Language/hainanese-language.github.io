字义解释

pun¹ 同音字 6

bēn

  • 〔bēn〕
  • ①快跑。～跑、狂～、～驰。To run fast. 奔跑 to run; 狂奔 to dash; 奔驰 to speed along.
  • ②急着去做。～忙、～走、东～西跑。To hurry about on business. 奔忙 to bustle about; 东奔西跑 to run here and there.
  • ③逃走。～逃、出～。To flee. 奔逃 to run away; 出奔 to flee the country.
  • 〔bèn〕
  • ④直向目的地去。直～机场、投～。To head straight for. 直奔机场 to go straight to the airport; 投奔 to go and seek shelter with.
  • ⑤年纪接近某个数。他～六十了。To be getting on for (an age). 他奔六十了 he is pushing sixty.
