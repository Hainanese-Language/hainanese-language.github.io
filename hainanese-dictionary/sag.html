〔què〕

〔qiǎo〕
② 义同①，用于口语。家～儿、～盲眼。Same in meaning as sense ①, used in speech. 家雀儿 house sparrow; 雀盲眼 night blindness.
〔qiāo〕
③ 用于"雀子"，即雀斑。Used in 雀子, freckles.
