字义解释
① 不正；偏斜，跟"正"相对。～斜、～戴帽子、上梁不正下梁～。Askew; slanting — the opposite of 正. 歪斜 crooked; 上梁不正下梁歪 if the upper beam is crooked the lower will be too.
② 不正当的；不正派的。～门邪道、～风。Improper; crooked. 歪门邪道 crooked ways; 歪风 an unhealthy trend.
③ 〈方〉侧身躺着休息。～一会儿。〈dial.〉 To lie down on one's side for a rest. 歪一会儿 to lie down for a while.
