字义解释
① 物体之间的相互作用。～学、重～、引～、磁～。The interaction between bodies. 力学 mechanics; 重力 gravity; 引力 attraction; 磁力 magnetic force.
② 力量；能力。人～、财～、体～、能～。Strength; capacity. 人力 manpower; 财力 financial resources; 体力 physical strength; 能力 ability.
③ 尽力；努力。～求、～争、～戒、～挽狂澜。To do one's utmost. 力求 to strive for; 力争 to contend for; 力戒 to guard strictly against; 力挽狂澜 to turn the tide by main force.
④ 效力；作用。感染～、说服～、药～。Effect; potency. 感染力 the power to move; 说服力 persuasiveness; 药力 the strength of a medicine.
