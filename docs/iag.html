海南话iag7音同「医若」
普通话yuē / yāo
字义解释
〔yuē〕
① 提出或商定共同遵守的事。～定、～会、预～。To arrange something to be jointly observed. 约定 to agree on; 约会 an appointment; 预约 to book in advance.
② 共同订立的条文。条～、公～、和～。An agreed set of terms. 条约 a treaty; 公约 a convention; 和约 a peace treaty.
③ 邀请。～请、特～、～他来。To invite. 约请 to ask; 特约 specially engaged; 约他来 to ask him over.
④ 限制；管束。～束、制～。To restrain. 约束 to bind; 制约 to condition.
⑤ 简明；简要。简～、由博返～。Concise. 简约 succinct; 由博返约 to move from breadth back to essentials.
⑥ 大概。～计、大～、～有百人。About; roughly. 约计 an estimate; 大约 approximately; 约有百人 about a hundred people.
⑦ 数学上指减少分数的分子和分母。～分。In mathematics, to reduce a fraction. 约分 to cancel down.
〔yāo〕
⑧ 用秤称。～二斤肉。To weigh on a scale. 约二斤肉 to weigh out two jin of meat.
