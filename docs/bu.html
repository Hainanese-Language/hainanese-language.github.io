普通话bù
字义解释
① 表示否定。～去、～好、～多～少。Marks negation. 不去 not to go; 不多不少 neither more nor less.
② 用在句末表示疑问。你去～？At the end of a sentence, marks a question. 你去不？ are you going?
③ 用在动词、形容词中间，表示不可能。拿～动、来～及。Between a verb and its complement, marks impossibility. 拿不动 unable to lift it; 来不及 too late.
