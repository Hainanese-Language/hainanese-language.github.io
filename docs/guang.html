海南话guang1音同「哥汪」

字义解释
① 使人能看见东西的一种能量。日～、～线、阳～。Light, the energy that makes things visible. 日光 sunlight; 光线 a ray.
② 明亮。～明、～亮。Bright. 光明 bright; 光亮 shining.
③ 荣耀；使荣耀。～荣、为国争～、～宗耀祖。Glory; to bring glory to. 光荣 honour; 为国争光 to win honour for one's country.
④ 平滑；没有毛发。～滑、～溜、头剃～了。Smooth; bare. 光滑 slippery; 头剃光了 the head is shaved bare.
⑤ 一点不剩。吃～、卖～、钱花～了。Used up; with nothing left. 吃光 to eat up; 卖光 to sell out.
⑥ 只；单。～说不做、～剩他一个。Only; merely. 光说不做 all talk and no action.
⑦ 敬辞，称对方的到来。～临、～顾。Polite word for another's visit. 光临 to honour with your presence; 光顾 to patronise.
⑧ 景物。风～、春～。Scenery. 风光 a view; 春光 spring scenery.
