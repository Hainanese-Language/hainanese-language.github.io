普通话dà / dài
字义解释
〔dà〕
① 在体积、面积、数量、力量等方面超过一般或超过所比的对象。～山、～雨、力气～。Big; great — in size, area, number or strength. 大山 a great mountain; 力气大 strong.
② 排行第一。老～、～哥、～姐。Eldest in rank of birth. 老大 the eldest; 大哥 eldest brother.
③ 敬辞，称跟对方有关的事物。～作、尊姓～名。Polite prefix for what belongs to the person addressed. 大作 your writing; 尊姓大名 your honoured name.
④ 表示程度深。～笑、～吃一惊、～有好处。To a high degree. 大笑 to roar with laughter; 大吃一惊 to be greatly startled.
⑤ 用在时间词前，表示更远。～前天、～后年。Before a time word, indicating one further off. 大前天 three days ago; 大后年 three years hence.
⑥ 用在时令、节日前，表示强调。～冷天、～年初一。Before a season or festival, for emphasis. 大冷天 in the bitter cold; 大年初一 New Year's Day itself.
⑦ 〈方〉指父亲或伯父。〈dial.〉 Father or father's elder brother.
〔dài〕
⑧ 用于"大夫""大王"等词。Used in words such as 大夫 (doctor) and 大王 (king).
