② 有才干的。～人、～手、～干。Capable. 能人 an able person; 能手 an expert hand; 能干 competent.
③ 能够；可以。～写会算、他～来。Can; to be able to. 能写会算 able to write and reckon; 他能来 he is able to come.
④ 物理学上指能量。热～、电～、太阳～。In physics, energy. 热能 heat energy; 电能 electrical energy; 太阳能 solar energy.
