字义解释
〔zháo〕
① 接触；挨上。上不～天、脚不～地。To touch; to come into contact with. 上不着天、脚不着地 touching neither sky nor ground.
② 受到；感受。～凉、～急、～火。To be affected by; to experience. 着凉 to catch a chill; 着急 to become anxious; 着火 to catch fire.
③ 燃烧；点燃。火～了、灯～了。To burn; to be lit. 火着了 the fire has caught; 灯着了 the light is on.
④ 用在动词后，表示达到目的或产生结果。找～了、猜～了、买～了。Used after a verb to indicate that an aim has been achieved or a result obtained. 找着了 found it; 猜着了 guessed correctly; 买着了 managed to buy it.
⑤ 入睡。睡～了、躺下就～了。To fall asleep. 睡着了 fell asleep; 躺下就着了 fell asleep as soon as one lay down.
〔zhāo〕
⑥ 下棋时下一子或走一步。～数、高～。A move in a board game; to make a move. 着数 moves or tactics; 高着 a clever move.
⑦ 计策；办法。这一～真绝、没～了。A tactic; a means or method. 这一着真绝 that was a brilliant move; 没着了 there is nothing more one can do.
⑧ 放；加进去。～点盐。To put in; to add. 着点盐 add some salt.
⑨ <方> 表示同意。～，就这么办。<dialect>Used to express agreement. 着，就这么办 all right, let's do it that way.
〔zhe〕
⑩ 助词，表示动作正在进行或伴随另一动作。走～、说～话。A particle indicating an action in progress or accompanying another action. 走着 walking; 说着话 talking while doing something.
⑪ 助词，表示状态持续。墙上挂～画、门开～。A particle indicating a continuing state. 墙上挂着画 a picture is hanging on the wall; 门开着 the door is open.
⑫ 助词，用在某些动词后，表示命令、嘱咐或提醒。你听～、拿～、慢～点。A particle used after certain verbs to express a command, instruction, or reminder. 你听着 listen; 拿着 hold this; 慢着点 take it slowly.
